# WWF April-June 2001 Research Report

**Period Covered:** April 1, 2001 - June 30, 2001

## Executive Summary

The April-June 2001 period marked the beginning of the end of the Attitude Era and the start of the Invasion storyline, featuring four major PPV events — WrestleMania X-Seven (April 1, Houston), Backlash (April 29, Chicago), Judgment Day (May 20, Sacramento), and King of the Ring (June 24, East Rutherford) — and marked by Steve Austin winning the WWF Championship from The Rock at WrestleMania X-Seven, the formation of the Two-Man Power Trip (Austin & Triple H), the Brothers of Destruction (Undertaker & Kane) winning the WWF Tag Team Championship, Edge winning the 2001 King of the Ring tournament, and the beginning of the WCW/ECW Invasion storyline. Key developments included: Steve Austin defeating The Rock in a No Disqualification match at WrestleMania X-Seven to win the WWF Championship; the Two-Man Power Trip (Austin & Triple H) dominating the WWF; the Brothers of Destruction defeating the Two-Man Power Trip to win the WWF Tag Team Championship at Backlash; Kane defeating Triple H in a Chain match to win the Intercontinental Championship at Judgment Day; Edge defeating Kurt Angle to win the 2001 King of the Ring tournament at King of the Ring; and the beginning of the WCW/ECW Invasion storyline after King of the Ring. WrestleMania X-Seven drew 67,925 attendance at the Reliant Astrodome in Houston. Backlash drew 15,592 attendance at the Allstate Arena in Chicago with $831,510 gate and $143,908 merchandise sales. Judgment Day drew 13,623 attendance at the ARCO Arena in Sacramento with $674,855 gate. King of the Ring drew 17,777 attendance at the Continental Airlines Arena in East Rutherford with $1,132,395 gate and 0.84 buyrate.

**Location:** Reliant Astrodome, Houston, Texas
**Attendance:** 67,925

## Conclusion

The April-June 2001 period marked the beginning of the end of the Attitude Era and the start of the Invasion storyline, featuring four major PPV events and major title changes and storylines. Steve Austin defeated The Rock in a No Disqualification match at WrestleMania X-Seven to win the WWF Championship and turned heel by aligning with Vince McMahon. The Two-Man Power Trip (Austin & Triple H) dominated the WWF, winning the WWF Tag Team Championship at Backlash. The Brothers of Destruction (Undertaker & Kane) defeated the Two-Man Power Trip to win the WWF Tag Team Championship at Backlash. Kane defeated Triple H in a Chain match to win the Intercontinental Championship at Judgment Day. Edge defeated Kurt Angle to win the 2001 King of the Ring tournament at King of the Ring. The WCW/ECW Invasion storyline began after King of the Ring. WrestleMania X-Seven drew 67,925 attendance at the Reliant Astrodome in Houston. Backlash drew 15,592 attendance at the Allstate Arena in Chicago with $831,510 gate and $143,908 merchandise sales. Judgment Day drew 13,623 attendance at the ARCO Arena in Sacramento with $674,855 gate. King of the Ring drew 17,777 attendance at the Continental Airlines Arena in East Rutherford with $1,132,395 gate and 0.84 buyrate.
