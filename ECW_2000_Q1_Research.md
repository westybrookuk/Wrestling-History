### ECW World Television Championship — Rob Van Dam → Vacated → Super Crazy

**Title Vacated: Rob Van Dam (Injured)**

- **Date:** March 4, 2000 (officially stripped)
- **Reason:** Rob Van Dam fractured his left ankle on January 29, 2000 during a successful title defense against Rhino in Orlando, FL. The Network (Cyrus) forced Van Dam to relinquish the title on March 4, 2000 at an ECW on TNN taping.
- **Reign duration:** April 4, 1998 - March 4, 2000 (approximately 700 days / 23 months)
- **Why it mattered:** Rob Van Dam's 700-day reign was one of the longest in ECW Television Championship history. The injury was caused by Rhino and forced ECW to hold a tournament for the vacant title.

## 2. Major TV, PPV, Supercard, and Arena Events

### ECW Guilty as Charged 2000 — January 9, 2000

- **Date:** January 9, 2000
- **Venue:** Boutwell Memorial Auditorium
- **City:** Birmingham, Alabama
- **Attendance:** 4,700
- **Buy rate:** 75,000
- **Network:** PPV
- **Main matches:**
  - C.W. Anderson (w/ Lou E. Dangerously & Billy Wiles) defeated Mikey Whipwreck (3:42)
  - Danny Doring, Roadkill & Simon Diamond (w/ Elektra & Mitch) defeated Nova, Kid Kash & Jazz (9:58)
  - Yoshihiro Tajiri & Super Crazy (w/ Steve Corino) defeated Little Guido (w/ Sal E. Graziano) & Jerry Lynn (12:48)
  - Angel (w/ DeVito & Vic Grimes) defeated New Jack (8:48)
  - ECW World Television Championship: Rob Van Dam (c) defeated Sabu to retain the title (14:37)
  - ECW World Tag Team Championship: The Impact Players (Justin Credible & Lance Storm) (w/ Dawn Marie & Jason) defeated Tommy Dreamer & Raven (c) (w/ Francine) to win the titles (9:23)
  - ECW World Heavyweight Championship: Mike Awesome (c) (w/ Judge Jeff Jones) defeated Spike Dudley (14:10) — Awesome Bomb from top rope through a table
- **Major finishes:**
  - The Impact Players won the tag team titles when Credible hit a "That's Incredible" on Raven after Raven got in the way of a kendo stick swing aimed at Francine. Dawn Marie and Francine brawled, and Credible aimed the kendo stick at Francine but Raven intervened.

- **Important debuts/returns/exits:**
  - **Spike Dudley's knee injury** — Dudley injured his knee during the main event against Awesome and had to take time off
- **Why it mattered:** Guilty as Charged 2000 was ECW's first PPV of the new millennium. The Impact Players won the tag team titles, Mike Awesome dominated Spike Dudley, and RVD retained the TV title against Sabu. The show drew 75,000 PPV buys and 4,700 fans. Critics noted the main event probably shouldn't have headlined the show, but the last three matches were strong examples of ECW's brand of wrestling.

## 5. Injuries, Deaths, Legal Issues, Financial Problems, and Backstage Controversies

### New Jack & Vic Grimes Scaffold Incident — March 12, 2000

- **People involved:** New Jack, Vic Grimes
- **Type:** Legitimate serious injury
- **What happened:** During a Danbury Street Fight at Living Dangerously 2000, both men climbed a 13-foot scaffold and fell to the concrete floor. New Jack suffered brain damage, a skull fracture, blindness in his right eye, and a broken leg. Grimes broke his fall on the top rope and dislocated his ankle. New Jack was transported to a local hospital by ambulance.
- **What was confirmed:** New Jack suffered brain damage, skull fracture, blindness in right eye, broken leg. Grimes dislocated ankle and multiple injuries.
- **What is disputed:** Whether the fall was botched or intentional. By New Jack's admission, he was trying to kill Grimes.
- **Immediate creative/business impact:** Both men were seriously injured; New Jack was out of action for several months; Grimes departed ECW
- **Long-term impact:** The injuries affected New Jack for the rest of his life. The incident became one of the most notorious moments in ECW history.
