## 1. Title Changes

### ECW World Heavyweight Championship — Mike Awesome → Masato Tanaka → Mike Awesome

**Title Change #1: Mike Awesome → Masato Tanaka**

- **Date:** December 17, 1999
- **Taping date:** December 17, 1999
- **Air date:** Aired December 24, 1999 on ECW on TNN
- **Championship:** ECW World Heavyweight Championship
- **Winner:** Masato Tanaka
- **Loser:** Mike Awesome (c) (w/ Judge Jeff Jones)
- **Show:** ECW on TNN (TV taping)
- **Venue:** Municipal Auditorium
- **City:** Nashville, Tennessee
- **Attendance:** 2,500
- **Finish:** Masato Tanaka pinned Mike Awesome (c) to win the ECW World Heavyweight Championship (10:25). After the match, Awesome showed respect — shaking Tanaka's hand and fastening the belt around his waist — before attacking him with a clothesline and Awesome Bombing him through a table on the outside.
- **Event type:** TV taping (ECW on TNN)
- **Why it mattered:** Masato Tanaka became the first non-American wrestler and the first Japanese wrestler to hold the ECW World Heavyweight Championship. This was a historic moment for ECW, symbolizing the cross-cultural impact of the promotion. Tanaka's reign lasted only 6 days.

**Title Change #2: Masato Tanaka → Mike Awesome**

- **Date:** December 23, 1999
- **Taping date:** December 23, 1999
- **Air date:** Aired December 30/31, 1999 on ECW on TNN
- **Championship:** ECW World Heavyweight Championship
- **Winner:** Mike Awesome (w/ Judge Jeff Jones)
- **Loser:** Masato Tanaka (c)
- **Show:** ECW on TNN (TV taping)
- **Venue:** Westchester County Center
- **City:** White Plains, New York
- **Attendance:** 2,800
- **Finish:** Mike Awesome pinned Masato Tanaka (c) to regain the ECW World Heavyweight Championship (11:47) with a sit-down powerbomb. After the match, Spike Dudley's music hit and he marched to the ring, attempting an Acid Drop on Awesome, but Judge Jeff Jones intercepted and was clotheslined by Awesome. This led to a confrontation that set up their match at Guilty as Charged 2000.
- **Event type:** TV taping (ECW on TNN)
- **Why it mattered:** Mike Awesome regained the ECW World Heavyweight Championship just 6 days after losing it. This was a brutal 11:47 rematch featuring tables, chairs, and high-impact spots including a DDT off the top rope through a table by Tanaka. Awesome became a two-time ECW World Champion. The post-match angle with Spike Dudley set up their feud for Guilty as Charged 2000.

## 2. Major TV, PPV, Supercard, and Arena Events

### Re-enter the Sandman — October 23, 1999

- **Date:** October 23, 1999 (aired October 29, November 5, and November 19, 1999 on ECW on TNN)
- **Venue:** ECW Arena (Viking Hall)
- **City:** Philadelphia, Pennsylvania
- **Attendance:** ~1,500 (sell out)
- **Main matches:**
  - ECW World Heavyweight Championship: Mike Awesome (c) (w/ Judge Jeff Jones) defeated Mikey Whipwreck (w/ Lou E. Dangerously) by pinfall (5:55)
  - Nova defeated Chris Candido (w/ Tammy Lynn Sytch) by pinfall — During the match, Chris Chetti kissed Tammy Lynn Sytch and carried her backstage
  - Three-Way Dance: Super Crazy defeated Little Guido (w/ Sal E. Graziano) and Spike Dudley by pinfall (10:57)
  - Yoshihiro Tajiri (w/ Jack Victory & Steve Corino) defeated Jerry Lynn by pinfall
  - David Cash vs. Tom Marquez ended in a no contest
  - ECW World Television Championship: Rob Van Dam (c) (w/ Bill Alfonso) vs. Sabu (w/ Bill Alfonso) ended in a no contest (30:00 time limit draw)
  - ECW World Tag Team Championship: Raven & Tommy Dreamer (c) (w/ Francine) vs. The Impact Players (Justin Credible & Lance Storm) (w/ Dawn Marie & Jason) ended in a no contest (4:45)
- **Major finishes:**
  - During the tag team main event, Dreamer and Raven repeatedly failed to cooperate, eventually brawling with each other. Rhino interfered, attacking Dreamer. The three men attacked Dreamer and Raven until **The Sandman made his surprise return to ECW**, driving the Impact Players and Rhino away before celebrating with Dreamer and Francine.
- **Important debuts/returns/exits:**
  - **The Sandman's return to ECW** — The Sandman made a surprise return, cleaning house after the main event tag team match
- **Why it mattered:** Re-enter the Sandman was a significant event as it marked The Sandman's return to ECW after his absence. The event also featured a 30-minute time limit draw between RVD and Sabu for the TV title, and the dysfunctional Dreamer/Raven partnership was on full display. The Sandman's return set up the six-man tag team match at November to Remember.

### ECW November to Remember 1999 — November 7, 1999

- **Date:** November 7, 1999
- **Venue:** Burt Flickinger Center
- **City:** Buffalo, New York
- **Attendance:** 3,000
- **Buy rate:** 80,000
- **Network:** PPV
- **Main matches:**
  - Dark match: Danny Doring & Roadkill defeated CW Anderson & Bill Whiles (4:34)
  - Spike Dudley defeated Simon Diamond (w/ Dick Hertz) (2:59)
  - Little Guido (w/ Sal E. Graziano) defeated Nova (4:20)
  - Three-Way Dance: Jerry Lynn defeated Yoshihiro Tajiri (w/ Steve Corino & Jack Victory) and Super Crazy (10:59) — Tajiri pinned Crazy (6:44); Lynn pinned Tajiri (10:59)
  - Handicap Match: Da Baldies (Spanish Angel, Tony DeVito, Vito LoGrasso & PN News) defeated New Jack & The Hardcore Chair Swingin' Freaks (Axl Rotten & Balls Mahoney) (8:21)
  - Sabu (w/ Bill Alfonso) defeated Chris Candido (w/ Tammy Lynn Sytch) via submission (Arabian Facebuster + Camel Clutch) (17:42)
  - ECW World Heavyweight Championship: Mike Awesome (c) (w/ Judge Jeff Jones) defeated Masato Tanaka (12:26) — Awesome Bomb from top rope
  - ECW World Television Championship: Rob Van Dam (c) (w/ Bill Alfonso) defeated Taz (14:34) — Five-Star Frog Splash; Taz's farewell appearance
  - Six-Man Tag Team Match: Rhino & The Impact Players (Justin Credible & Lance Storm) (w/ Jason Knight & Dawn Marie) defeated Raven, Tommy Dreamer & The Sandman (w/ Francine) (9:19) — Credible pinned Sandman
- **Major finishes:**
  - Mike Awesome retained the ECW World title against Masato Tanaka with a top-rope Awesome Bomb after a brutal match
  - Rob Van Dam defeated Taz in what was Taz's farewell appearance before departing for WWF. RVD hit a leaping Van Daminator, split-legged moonsault (making Taz bleed from the mouth), and Five-Star Frog Splash.
  - The Sandman was revealed as Dreamer and Raven's mystery partner. The Impact Players & Rhino won the main event when Francine and Dawn Marie got into a fight, and Credible pinned Sandman.
- **Important debuts/returns/exits:**
  - **The Sandman's return** as mystery partner for Dreamer & Raven
  - **Taz's final ECW PPV appearance** — Lost to RVD for the TV title; departed for WWF
- **Why it mattered:** November to Remember 1999 was ECW's final PPV of the year (and of the 1990s). It featured Taz's farewell appearance, The Sandman's return, and Mike Awesome's continued dominance as World Champion. The buy rate of 80,000 was solid for ECW. The event set up multiple storylines heading into 2000: the Impact Players' title shot against Dreamer & Raven, Awesome vs. Spike Dudley, and the ongoing Awesome/Tanaka rivalry.
