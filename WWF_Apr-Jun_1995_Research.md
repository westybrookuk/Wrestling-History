## 1. Title Changes

### 1A. WWF Intercontinental Championship — Jeff Jarrett → Razor Ramon → Jeff Jarrett → Razor Ramon → Jeff Jarrett (House Show Chaos)

- **Date:** April 26, 1995 (house show); May 19, 1995 (house show); May 22, 1995 (house show)
- **Championship:** WWF Intercontinental Championship
- **Changes:**
  - **April 26:** Jeff Jarrett was stripped of the title. Razor Ramon won the vacant title.
  - **May 19:** Jeff Jarrett defeated Razor Ramon in a ladder match to regain the title at a house show in Toronto, Ontario, Canada.
  - **May 22:** Razor Ramon won the title back from Jarrett at a house show (location uncertain — possibly Rockford, IL).
  - **Note:** Jarrett regained the title again shortly after (exact date uncertain, possibly May 22 same night or May 23). WWE's official title history lists Jarrett as holding from May 22 to July 23, but the May 19 and May 22 changes are documented in house show results and WWF Magazine/Action Zone.
- **Confidence:** The rapid title changes are confirmed by multiple house show databases, WWF Magazine coverage, and Action Zone TV tapings. The exact sequence and locations of some changes are uncertain.
- **Source:** WWE.com title history; thehistoryofwwe.com 1995 results; profightdb.com house show results; wweppvresultz.weebly.com; reddit r/SquaredCircle discussions; officialfan.proboards.com.

### 1B. WWF Tag Team Championship — Smoking Gunns → Owen Hart & Yokozuna

- **Date:** April 2, 1995
- **Championship:** WWF World Tag Team Championship
- **Event:** WrestleMania XI (Hartford Civic Center, Hartford, CT)
- **Winners:** Owen Hart & Yokozuna (w/ Mr. Fuji & Jim Cornette)
- **Losers:** The Smoking Gunns (Billy Gunn & Bart Gunn) (c)
- **Details:** Owen Hart's "mystery partner" was revealed as Yokozuna, returning after months of absence. Yokozuna hit the Bonzai Drop on Billy Gunn, then Owen pulled Billy into the middle of the ring for the pin at 9:42. This was Yokozuna's surprise return following Survivor Series 1994.
- **Note:** This was the beginning of Owen Hart & Yokozuna's tag team reign, managed by Jim Cornette as "Camp Cornette." They would hold the titles until September 24, 1995 (In Your House 3).
- **Source:** WrestleMania XI results (multiple sources); Wikipedia (WrestleMania XI); thehistoryofwwe.com.

### 1C. WWF Women's Championship — Bull Nakano → Alundra Blayze

- **Date:** Approximately April-May 1995 (exact date uncertain)
- **Championship:** WWF Women's Championship
- **Change:** Alundra Blayze (formerly Madusa) won the title from Bull Nakano.
- **Confidence:** [FLAG] The exact date of this title change is uncertain. Blayze/Alundra Blayze was champion by mid-1995, but the specific event and date need verification. Some sources indicate April 1995; others suggest a different timeframe.
- **Note:** Alundra Blayze had previously held the title in 1993-1994 before losing it to Nakano in November 1994. She regained it during this period.
- **Source:** [FLAG — needs verification] WWE.com title history; various databases.

### 1D. WWF World Heavyweight Championship — Diesel (held throughout)

- **Date:** Held throughout April-June 1995
- **Champion:** Diesel (Kevin Nash)
- **Won:** November 26, 1994 (Survivor Series)
- **Lost:** November 19, 1995 (Survivor Series — to Bret Hart)
- **Notes:** Diesel successfully defended at WrestleMania XI (vs. Shawn Michaels, April 2), In Your House 1 (vs. Sid, May 14), and King of the Ring (tag team main event, June 25). He also faced King Mabel at SummerSlam (August 27).

  6. **WWF Championship:** Diesel (c) (w/ Pamela Anderson) defeated Shawn Michaels (w/ Sid Justice & Jenny McCarthy) by pinfall (20:35) — Jackknife Powerbomb
  7. **Main Event:** Lawrence Taylor (w/ NFL All-Pro Team: Ken Norton Jr., Carl Banks, Rickey Jackson, Steve McMichael, Chris Spielman, Reggie White) defeated Bam Bam Bigelow (w/ Million Dollar Corporation) by pinfall (11:42) — Special Guest Referee: Pat Patterson

  - Lawrence Taylor main evented — brought massive mainstream attention but was controversial (non-wrestler main eventing WrestleMania instead of title match)
  - Diesel retained but the match was overshadowed by LT/Bigelow
  - Owen Hart & Yokozuna won tag titles — Yokozuna's surprise return
  - Bret Hart's "I Quit" match was solid but mid-card booking
  - Sid's interference cost Michaels the title — led to Sid turning on Michaels the next night on Raw

  - Shawn Michaels later said he had Diesel pinned but referee was knocked down; Sid got referee back in but Michaels couldn't capitalize

## 3. Roster Moves / Debuts / Exits

### 3A. Sid Justice — Turned on Shawn Michaels (April 3, 1995)

- **Date:** April 3, 1995 (Raw)
- **Person:** Sycho Sid
- **Change:** After WrestleMania XI, where Sid's interference cost Shawn Michaels the WWF Title, Michaels told Sid he was "given the night off" for the next title match. Sid snapped, attacked Michaels with three powerbombs. Diesel came out to save Michaels, reuniting the babyface alliance.
- **Impact:** Sid became a top heel, feuding with Diesel. This led to their In Your House 1 match. Sid was managed by Ted DiBiase and aligned with the Million Dollar Corporation.
- **Confidence:** Confirmed fact.
- **Source:** Wikipedia (WrestleMania XI aftermath); thehistoryofwwe.com; wrestlingrecaps.com; prowestling.fandom.com.

### 3B. Bam Bam Bigelow — Face Turn (Post-WrestleMania)

- **Date:** Late April/early May 1995
- **Person:** Bam Bam Bigelow
- **Change:** After losing to Lawrence Taylor at WrestleMania XI, Bigelow was attacked by Ted DiBiase and the Million Dollar Corporation. Diesel saved Bigelow, leading to a face turn. Bigelow and Diesel formed an alliance.
- **Impact:** Bigelow became a babyface, teaming with Diesel at King of the Ring. This was a significant character change for Bigelow, who had been a heel for most of his WWF run.
- **Note:** Bigelow was reportedly promised a huge face run if he went through with losing to Lawrence Taylor. The push started but was short-lived.
- **Confidence:** Confirmed fact.
- **Source:** Wikipedia (WrestleMania XI); wrestlingrecaps.com; thehistoryofwwe.com.

### 3C. Yokozuna — Surprise Return (WrestleMania XI, April 2, 1995)

- **Date:** April 2, 1995
- **Person:** Yokozuna
- **Change:** Returned after months of absence as Owen Hart's "mystery partner" for the tag team title match. Teamed with Owen Hart, managed by Jim Cornette as "Camp Cornette."
- **Impact:** Won the WWF Tag Team Championship at WrestleMania XI. Held titles until September 1995. Yokozuna was still heavily overweight and not in good physical condition, but was pushed as a dominant force.
- **Confidence:** Confirmed fact.
- **Source:** WrestleMania XI results; Wikipedia; thehistoryofwwe.com.

### 3D. Hunter Hearst Helmsley — PPV Debut (In Your House 1, May 14, 1995)

- **Date:** May 14, 1995
- **Person:** Hunter Hearst Helmsley (later Triple H)
- **Change:** Made his PPV debut in a dark match at In Your House 1, defeating Phil Apollo (6:38).
- **Impact:** This was the first PPV appearance of the character who would become Triple H, one of WWF's biggest stars. At this point, he was a wealthy Connecticut blueblood heel gimmick.
- **Confidence:** Confirmed fact.
- **Source:** Wikipedia (In Your House 1); thehistoryofwwe.com.

### 3E. Savio Vega — Rising Babyface (April-June 1995)

- **Date:** April-June 1995
- **Person:** Savio Vega (formerly Kwang)

- **Confidence:** Confirmed fact.

### 3F. Lawrence Taylor — One-Time Appearance (WrestleMania XI)

- **Date:** April 2, 1995
- **Person:** Lawrence Taylor (NFL legend)
- **Change:** Main evented WrestleMania XI against Bam Bam Bigelow. Trained with Diesel to prepare. Won the match.
- **Impact:** Brought massive mainstream attention to WWF. LT's involvement was controversial — non-wrestler main eventing WrestleMania. His only PPV match (1-0 record). Led to Bigelow's face turn.

## 4. Injuries / Backstage Incidents / Controversies

### 4A. Shawn Michaels — Attacked by Sid (April 3, 1995)

- **Date:** April 3, 1995 (Raw)
- **People involved:** Shawn Michaels, Sid Justice, Diesel
- **What happened:** After WrestleMania XI, where Sid's interference cost Michaels the WWF Title, Michaels said he'd give Sid "the night off" for the next title match. Sid became enraged, attacked Michaels with three powerbombs. Sid was supposed to be Michaels' bodyguard but turned on him. Diesel came out to save Michaels.
- **Why it mattered:** This was a worked shoot angle — Sid turning on Michaels. It set up Sid as a top heel and led to his feud with Diesel. Michaels was written off TV for several weeks (kayfabe injury).
- **Confidence:** Confirmed fact.
- **Source:** Wikipedia (WrestleMania XI aftermath); thehistoryofwwe.com; wrestlingrecaps.com.

### 4B. Intercontinental Title House Show Chaos (April-May 1995)

- **Date:** April 26, May 19, May 22, 1995
- **People involved:** Jeff Jarrett, Razor Ramon, WWF management
- **What happened:** The IC Title changed hands multiple times on house shows in a span of less than a month. Jarrett was stripped on April 26, Razor won the vacant title. Jarrett won it back from Razor in a ladder match on May 19. Razor won it back on May 22. Jarrett regained it again shortly after (possibly same night). These changes were acknowledged on WWF Magazine and Action Zone TV tapings but were not prominently featured on Raw.
- **Why it mattered:** This was part of a pattern of WWF not protecting the IC Title. The rapid house show title changes confused fans and devalued the championship. It demonstrated WWF's focus on the World Title and house show revenue over TV title prestige.
- **Confidence:** Confirmed fact (house show results, WWF Magazine, Action Zone).
- **Source:** WWE.com title history; thehistoryofwwe.com; house show databases; officialfan.proboards.com; reddit discussions.

### 4C. Mabel's King of the Ring Victory — Fan Backlash

- **Date:** June 25, 1995 (King of the Ring)
- **People involved:** Mabel, Savio Vega, Philadelphia crowd, WWF creative
- **What happened:** Mabel (Nelson Frazier) won the King of the Ring tournament, defeating Savio Vega in the finals. The Philadelphia crowd (including many ECW fans) hated the outcome, throwing garbage during Mabel's coronation. Chanted "ECW" in protest. Mabel attacked Razor Ramon and 1-2-3 Kid during the coronation.
- **Why it mattered:** Mabel's win was heavily criticized. He was a 468-pound mid-carder who wasn't over with fans. The "King Mabel" gimmick was cartoonish and didn't work. Fans felt Savio Vega (who wrestled 4 matches that night) deserved to win. The backlash demonstrated WWF's disconnect with their audience. Mabel's push led to a WWF Title shot at SummerSlam, which he lost to Diesel.
- **Confidence:** Confirmed fact.
- **Source:** King of the Ring 1995 results; wrestlingrecaps.com; grokipedia.com; multiple reviews.

### 4D. Diesel's Arm Injury (Post-In Your House 1)

- **Date:** Mid-May 1995
- **People involved:** Diesel, Sid
- **What happened:** At In Your House 1, Sid hit Diesel with a powerbomb. Diesel landed on his elbow, suffering a legitimate arm injury. This injury was worked into storylines and limited Diesel's wrestling for several weeks.
- **Why it mattered:** The injury prolonged the Diesel-Sid feud and limited Diesel's ability to wrestle at full capacity. It was used to explain why Diesel couldn't defend the title in certain matches.
- **Confidence:** Confirmed fact.
- **Source:** thehistoryofwwe.com; wrestlingrecaps.com; wrestling20yrs.com.

### 4E. Bret Hart's "Fake Injury" Angle (In Your House 1)

- **Date:** May 14, 1995 (In Your House 1)
- **People involved:** Bret Hart, Jerry Lawler, Hakushi, Shinja
- **What happened:** Bret Hart had been out with a "knee injury" (kayfabe). At In Your House 1, Hart defeated Hakushi, then revealed in a backstage interview that his injury was fake. Later that night, Hart lost to Jerry Lawler when Hakushi and Shinja interfered. Lawler had introduced "his mother" (actually a 20-something model) before the match, wishing her a happy Mother's Day.
- **Why it mattered:** This was part of the Hart-Lawler feud. The "fake injury" angle was used to explain Hart's absence and build sympathy. Lawler's "mother" angle was a comedic heel tactic.
- **Confidence:** Confirmed fact.
- **Source:** Wikipedia (In Your House 1); thehistoryofwwe.com; grokipedia.com.

### 4F. Yokozuna's Physical Condition

- **Date:** Ongoing through 1995
- **People involved:** Yokozuna
- **What happened:** Yokozuna returned at WrestleMania XI heavily overweight and not in good physical condition. Despite this, he was pushed as a dominant tag team champion with Owen Hart. His mobility was limited, and matches were kept short.
- **Why it mattered:** Yokozuna's physical condition was a legitimate concern. He had won the WWF Title at WrestleMania IX (1993) but lost it quickly due to his inability to maintain the physique and workrate required. By 1995, he was used primarily in tag team competition and short matches.
- **Confidence:** Confirmed fact.
- **Source:** Multiple event reviews; wrestlingrecaps.com; Wikipedia.

## 5. Game Effect Suggestions

### Momentum
- **Diesel:** Stagnant. Retained the WWF Title at WrestleMania XI and In Your House 1 but never had clean, dominant victories. The DQ finish at In Your House 1 (Tatanka interference) was criticized. His feud with Sid was lackluster.
- **Shawn Michaels:** Solid but inconsistent. Lost the WWF Title match at WrestleMania XI (with Sid interference). The angle with Sid turning on him was good, but Michaels was written off TV for weeks.
- **Bret Hart:** Strong. Won the "I Quit" match at WrestleMania XI, worked double duty at In Your House 1 (beat Hakushi, lost to Lawler). Won the "Kiss My Foot" match at King of the Ring. Consistent performer.
- **Sid:** Rising heel. Turned on Michaels, became a top threat. Feuded with Diesel but never won the title.
- **Owen Hart & Yokozuna:** Solid tag team. Won titles at WrestleMania XI, held them through this period. Jim Cornette's management was effective.
- **Jeff Jarrett:** Mid-card with IC Title. The house show title changes were confusing and devalued the championship.
- **Razor Ramon:** Solid babyface. Won IC Title on house shows, lost it back. Consistent performer but not pushed to top.
- **Mabel:** Pushed heavily but not over with fans. Won King of the Ring to massive backlash. The "King Mabel" gimmick was a failure.
- **Bam Bam Bigelow:** Face turn was good but the push was short-lived. Teamed with Diesel but didn't reach top status.
- **Savio Vega:** Rising babyface. Wrestled 4 matches at KOTR but lost in finals. Moderate push.
- **Lawrence Taylor:** One-time celebrity appearance. Brought mainstream attention but was controversial.

### Money
- **PPV buyrates:** WrestleMania XI did 1.3 buyrate (340,000 buys) — solid but not exceptional. In Your House 1 did 332,000 buys (0.83) — good for a new concept. King of the Ring did only 150,000 buys (0.65) — poor, reflecting fan dissatisfaction.
- **House shows:** Still strong revenue source. IC Title changes on house shows drew business.
- **Live events:** WrestleMania XI drew 16,305 (Hartford). King of the Ring drew 16,590 (Philadelphia). In Your House 1 drew 7,000 (Syracuse).
- **In Your House concept:** Lower price point ($14.95 vs. $29.95) was designed to increase PPV buys. The first event did well (332,000 buys).
- **Merchandise:** Diesel was the top merchandise draw. Shawn Michaels, Bret Hart, and Razor Ramon were also strong.

### Morale
- **Mixed.** The Sid/Michaels angle was well-received backstage. Bret Hart was a consistent performer and locker room leader. Mabel's push was unpopular with fans and some wrestlers. Yokozuna's physical condition was a concern. The IC Title house show chaos was seen as unprofessional by some.
- **Diesel (Kevin Nash)** was being pushed as the top babyface but the push was not working with fans. Nash was reportedly frustrated with the booking.
- **Shawn Michaels** was a top star but the Sid angle and injury write-off were frustrating.
- **Bret Hart** was the "company man" — consistent, reliable, but not always pushed to the top.

### Network Health
- **USA Network:** WWF's primary TV partner. Raw was the flagship show. Superstars and Wrestling Challenge were secondary programs.
- **PPV distribution:** Standard cable/satellite. In Your House was a new concept to increase PPV frequency.
- **Mainstream attention:** Lawrence Taylor's involvement brought significant mainstream media coverage to WWF.

### Fan Trust
- **Eroding.** Mabel's King of the Ring victory was a major trust issue — fans hated the outcome. The IC Title house show chaos confused fans. Diesel's push as "the next Hulk Hogan" was not working — fans didn't buy it.
- **Positive:** Bret Hart was consistently over. Shawn Michaels was popular despite not winning the WWF Title. The Sid/Michaels angle was well-received.
- **ECW presence:** Philadelphia crowd at King of the Ring chanted "ECW" — showing fan dissatisfaction with WWF and interest in alternative product.

### Title Prestige
- **WWF Title (Diesel):** Declining. Diesel retained but never had clean, dominant victories. DQ finishes and interference made the belt feel less important.
- **IC Title (Jarrett/Razor):** Near-zero prestige. The house show title changes were a disaster for credibility. The title was treated as a prop.
- **Tag Titles (Owen Hart & Yokozuna):** Stable. Owen & Yokozuna were credible champions. Jim Cornette's management added heat.
- **Women's Title:** [FLAG — needs verification] Bull Nakano/Alundra Blayze. The women's division was minimal and not prominently featured.

## Summary: Key Narrative Threads During This Period

1. **Diesel vs. Sid:** The dominant angle of April-June 1995. Sid turned on Shawn Michaels at WrestleMania, leading to a feud with Diesel. Diesel retained the WWF Title at In Your House 1 by DQ. The feud was lackluster and didn't produce clean finishes.

2. **WrestleMania XI Aftermath:** The Lawrence Taylor main event overshadowed the WWF Title match. Sid's turn on Michaels set up the next major angle. Owen Hart & Yokozuna won the tag titles.

3. **King of the Ring Disaster:** Mabel's victory was heavily criticized. Savio Vega wrestled 4 matches but lost in the finals. The Philadelphia crowd hated the outcome. Mabel's push led to a WWF Title shot at SummerSlam.

4. **IC Title House Show Chaos:** The Intercontinental Championship changed hands multiple times on house shows in April-May, confusing fans and devaluing the title.

5. **In Your House Launch:** The new monthly PPV series debuted with solid buys (332,000). Lower price point was designed to increase accessibility.

6. **Diesel's Failed Push:** Diesel was being pushed as "the next Hulk Hogan" but fans weren't buying it. His matches lacked clean finishes and dominant victories. The push was not working.

7. **Bret Hart's Consistency:** Bret Hart was the reliable performer, working double duty at In Your House 1 and winning the "Kiss My Foot" match at King of the Ring. He was the "company man" but not always pushed to the top.

8. **ECW Presence:** The Philadelphia crowd at King of the Ring chanted "ECW" — showing fan dissatisfaction with WWF and interest in the alternative product. This would lead to ECW invasion angles in 1996-1997.

## Simulator-Use Notes

### Title Lineage Implications
- **IC Title house show changes:** The April-May 1995 IC Title changes on house shows should be modeled as low-prestige title changes that don't significantly impact storylines. The title was treated as a prop during this period.
- **Tag Title reign:** Owen Hart & Yokozuna's reign (April 2 - September 24, 1995) was stable and credible. Jim Cornette's management should be factored in.
- **WWF Title:** Diesel's reign was marked by DQ finishes and interference. Clean finishes were rare. The title's prestige was declining.

### Roster Availability Changes
- **Shawn Michaels:** Written off TV for several weeks after April 3 attack by Sid. Returned for King of the Ring (June 25).
- **Sid:** Became a top heel after April 3 turn. Feuded with Diesel through June.
- **Yokozuna:** Returned at WrestleMania XI (April 2). Physical condition was a concern but he was available for tag team competition.
- **Hunter Hearst Helmsley:** PPV debut at In Your House 1 (dark match). Available but not prominently featured yet.

### Injuries or Absences
- **Diesel's arm injury:** Legitimate injury from Sid's powerbomb at In Your House 1 (May 14). Worked into storylines, limited wrestling for several weeks.
- **Shawn Michaels:** Written off TV after April 3 attack (kayfabe injury). Returned for King of the Ring.

### Morale/Backstage Politics Effects
- **Mabel's push:** Heavily criticized by fans and some wrestlers. The "King Mabel" gimmick was unpopular.
- **Diesel's push:** Being pushed as "the next Hulk Hogan" but not working with fans. Nash reportedly frustrated with booking.
- **IC Title chaos:** House show title changes seen as unprofessional by some. Devalued the championship.
- **Bret Hart:** The "company man" — consistent, reliable, but not always pushed to the top.

### Contract Jumps or Departures
- No major departures during this period. Lawrence Taylor was a one-time celebrity appearance.

### Faction/Team Formations or Breakups
- **Owen Hart & Yokozuna:** Formed at WrestleMania XI (April 2). Managed by Jim Cornette as "Camp Cornette."
- **Sid turns on Shawn Michaels:** April 3, 1995. Led to Sid's heel turn and feud with Diesel.
- **Diesel & Bam Bam Bigelow alliance:** Post-WrestleMania. Bigelow turned face, teamed with Diesel at King of the Ring.
- **Two Dudes with Attitudes reunion:** Diesel saved Michaels from Sid on April 3, reuniting the babyface alliance.

### Network/Censorship/Sponsor Pressure
- **USA Network:** WWF's primary TV partner. No major censorship issues during this period.
- **Lawrence Taylor involvement:** Brought mainstream attention but was controversial. No network pressure issues.

### Business Impact (Ratings, Attendance, PPV Buyrate)
- **WrestleMania XI:** 16,305 attendance (Hartford), 1.3 buyrate (340,000 buys)
- **In Your House 1:** 7,000 attendance (Syracuse), 0.83 buyrate (332,000 buys)
- **King of the Ring 1995:** 16,590 attendance (Philadelphia), 0.65 buyrate (150,000 buys) — poor, reflecting fan dissatisfaction
- **House shows:** Strong revenue source. IC Title changes drew business.

### Uncertain Facts That Should Not Be Hard-Coded Without Verification
- **IC Title changes:** The exact sequence and dates of the April-May 1995 house show changes are uncertain. Multiple sources give slightly different information.
- **Women's Title change:** The exact date of Alundra Blayze winning the title from Bull Nakano is uncertain.
- **Diesel's arm injury:** The exact extent and duration of the injury are uncertain.
