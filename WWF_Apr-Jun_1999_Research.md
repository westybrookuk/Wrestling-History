# WWF April-June 1999 Research Report

**Period Covered:** April 1, 1999 - June 30, 1999

## Executive Summary

The April-June 1999 period was one of the most tragic and eventful in WWF history, featuring three major PPV events — Backlash (April 25, Providence), Over the Edge (May 23, Kansas City), and King of the Ring (June 27, Greensboro) — and marked by one of the darkest moments in professional wrestling history: the death of Owen Hart at Over the Edge. Key developments included: The Undertaker winning the WWF Championship from Steve Austin at Over the Edge, the formation of The Corporate Ministry (April 29, SmackDown), Vince McMahon revealed as the "Higher Power" behind the Ministry (June 7, Raw), Billy Gunn winning the King of the Ring tournament, the McMahons winning control of the WWF from Austin in a ladder match at King of the Ring, Steve Austin winning the WWF Championship back from Undertaker on Raw (June 28), and the tragic death of Owen Hart during a stunt at Over the Edge (May 23). The period also saw The Acolytes win their first WWF Tag Team Championship, Jeff Jarrett winning the Intercontinental Championship, and Al Snow winning the Hardcore Championship. Backlash drew approximately 12,000 attendance in Providence. Over the Edge was surrounded by controversy due to Owen Hart's death. King of the Ring drew 19,761 (announced as 20,108) in Greensboro.

6. **The Rock defeated Triple H (with Chyna)** by disqualification — Singles Match (11:41)
7. **The Undertaker (with Paul Bearer) defeated "Stone Cold" Steve Austin (c)** — WWF Championship — Special Guest Referees: Shane McMahon & Vince McMahon (22:58) **Undertaker WON WWF Championship** [Shane gave fast-count; Undertaker won third WWF Championship; Had Austin been disqualified, he would have lost championship]

**Owen Hart Tragedy:**
- Owen Hart was scheduled to face The Godfather for WWF Intercontinental Championship
- Wrestling under Blue Blazer gimmick, Hart was to descend from arena rafters via safety harness
- Equipment gave way prematurely; Hart fell 78 feet (24m) into ring, landing chest-first on top rope
- Emergency medical technicians rushed him to Truman Medical Center
- Hart was pronounced dead on arrival at 8:12 PM CST
- Cause of death: Internal bleeding from severed aorta due to blunt force trauma
- Incident occurred during pre-recorded promo video; television viewers did not see accident
- Jim Ross repeatedly told viewers this was "not a wrestling angle" and was "a real situation"
- Event halted for 15 minutes before continuing
- Hour after restart, Ross informed viewers Hart had died at age 34
- Fans in attendance were not told until after event
- Event never released on home media; Over the Edge PPV slot replaced by Judgment Day in 2000
- Hart family sued WWF; settled out of court for $18 million
- Owen Hart's funeral held May 31, 1999 in Calgary, Alberta; attended by 300+ wrestlers
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Over_the_Edge_(1999)

- **Hardcore Title:** The Jan-Mar 1999 report showed Hardcore Holly winning at WrestleMania XV (Mar 28). This report shows Al Snow winning at Backlash (Apr 25).

## Conclusion

The April-June 1999 period was one of the most tragic and eventful in WWF history, marked by the death of Owen Hart at Over the Edge, the formation of The Corporate Ministry, Vince McMahon's reveal as the "Higher Power," The Undertaker winning the WWF Championship from Steve Austin, Steve Austin winning the WWF Championship back from Undertaker, Billy Gunn winning the King of the Ring tournament, the McMahons winning control of the WWF from Austin, and The Acolytes winning their first WWF Tag Team Championship. The period was dominated by the Corporate Ministry storyline and the tragic loss of Owen Hart, whose death at Over the Edge remains one of the darkest moments in professional wrestling history. Backlash drew approximately 12,000 attendance in Providence. Over the Edge was surrounded by controversy due to Hart's death and was never released on home media. King of the Ring drew 19,761 (announced as 20,108) in Greensboro and featured Billy Gunn's tournament victory and the McMahons' ladder match victory over Austin for WWF control.
