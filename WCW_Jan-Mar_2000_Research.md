# WCW January-March 2000 Research Report

**Period Covered:** January 1, 2000 - March 31, 2000

## Executive Summary

The January-March 2000 period was marked by unprecedented chaos in WCW, with Bret Hart being forced to vacate the World Title due to concussion injuries from the Goldberg kick at Starrcade 1999. Chris Benoit won the vacant title at Souled Out 2000, only to leave WCW for WWF days later along with Eddie Guerrero, Dean Malenko, and Perry Saturn. The period saw multiple title changes, Vince Russo's hotshot booking, and the continued decline of WCW. SuperBrawl 2000 featured Sid Vicious retaining the World Title in a 3-way match, and Uncensored 2000 continued the chaotic booking. The nWo 2000 faction continued but was losing relevance. This period marked one of the lowest points in WCW's history as the company struggled to compete with WWF.

## Title Changes

### WCW World Heavyweight Championship

**January 16, 2000 - Bret Hart to Vacated**
- **Event:** WCW Souled Out 2000 (Firstar Center, Cincinnati, OH)
- **Match Type:** Singles match (Bret Hart (c) vs. Sid Vicious)
- **Result:** Bret Hart was forced to vacate the title due to concussion injuries
- **Notes:** Hart suffered severe concussion from Goldberg's mule kick at Starrcade 1999. He was forced to withdraw from the main event at Souled Out due to lingering concussion symptoms.
- **Attendance:** 14,132
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Souled_Out_(2000)

**January 16, 2000 - Vacated to Chris Benoit**
- **Event:** WCW Souled Out 2000 (Firstar Center, Cincinnati, OH)
- **Match Type:** Singles match (Chris Benoit vs. Sid Vicious)
- **Result:** Chris Benoit defeated Sid Vicious by submission (14:53)
- **Notes:** Arn Anderson was the special guest referee. Sid submitted to the Crippler Crossface, but his foot was under the rope. Benoit won the vacant WCW World Heavyweight Championship.
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Souled_Out_(2000)

**January 17, 2000 - Chris Benoit to Vacated**
- **Event:** WCW Monday Nitro (Columbus, OH)
- **Result:** Title vacated (storyline)
- **Notes:** Arn Anderson stated he was forced to reverse the decision because Sid's leg was under the rope when he submitted to the crossface. In reality, Benoit left WCW with Eddie Guerrero, Dean Malenko, and Perry Saturn.
- **Source:** https://www.thewrestlinginsomniac.com/2017/05/the-year-wcw-championship-changed-hands.html

**January 24, 2000 - Vacated to Sid Vicious**
- **Event:** WCW Monday Nitro (Los Angeles, CA)
- **Match Type:** Singles match (Sid Vicious vs. Kevin Nash)
- **Result:** Sid Vicious defeated Kevin Nash for the vacant championship
- **Source:** https://www.thewrestlinginsomniac.com/2017/05/the-year-wcw-championship-changed-hands.html

**January 25, 2000 - Sid Vicious retained**
- **Event:** WCW Thunder (Las Vegas, NV) - aired 1/26/2000
- **Match Type:** Triple threat steel cage match (Sid Vicious vs. Kevin Nash & Ron Harris)
- **Result:** Sid Vicious defeated Kevin Nash & Ron Harris by submission
- **Source:** https://www.thewrestlinginsomniac.com/2017/05/the-year-wcw-championship-changed-hands.html

**April 10, 2000 - Sid Vicious to Vacated**
- **Event:** WCW Monday Nitro (Denver, CO)
- **Result:** Vince Russo & Eric Bischoff stripped all champions of their titles, rebooting WCW
- **Source:** https://www.thewrestlinginsomniac.com/2017/05/the-year-wcw-championship-changed-hands.html
