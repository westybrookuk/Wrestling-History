# WWF January-March 1996 Research Report

**Period Covered:** January 1, 1996 - March 31, 1996

## Executive Summary

The January-March 1996 period was a pivotal transitional phase in WWF, marking the end of the "New Generation" era and the beginning of the "Attitude Era." The period featured three major PPV events: Royal Rumble (January 21, Fresno), In Your House 6: Rage in the Cage (February 18, Louisville), and WrestleMania XII (March 31, Anaheim). The most significant development was Shawn Michaels winning his second consecutive Royal Rumble and then defeating Bret Hart in the first-ever Iron Man match at WrestleMania XII to win his first WWF Championship. The period also saw the debut of "The Ringmaster" Steve Austin with Ted DiBiase (who would later become "Stone Cold" Steve Austin), Goldust winning the Intercontinental Championship from Razor Ramon at Royal Rumble, and the return of Ultimate Warrior and Roddy Piper. This period marked the beginning of the end for several long-term WWF employees, as Razor Ramon (Scott Hall) would leave for WCW in May, and Diesel (Kevin Nash) would follow in May as well, setting the stage for the formation of the nWo in WCW.

## Title Changes

### WWF World Heavyweight Championship

**March 31, 1996 - Bret Hart to Shawn Michaels**
- **Event:** WWF WrestleMania XII (Arrowhead Pond, Anaheim, CA)
- **Match Type:** 60-Minute Iron Man Match
- **Result:** Shawn Michaels defeated Bret Hart (c) 1-0 in sudden death overtime (1:01:56)
- **Notes:** The match ended 0-0 after 60 minutes. WWF President Gorilla Monsoon ordered sudden death overtime. Michaels pinned Hart after hitting Sweet Chin Music twice. This was Michaels' first WWF Championship and the first Iron Man match in WWF PPV history. Hart took a six-month hiatus after losing the title.
- **Attendance:** 18,853
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WrestleMania_XII

### WWF Intercontinental Championship

**January 21, 1996 - Razor Ramon to Goldust**
- **Event:** WWF Royal Rumble 1996 (Selland Arena, Fresno, CA)
- **Match Type:** Singles Match
- **Result:** Goldust (w/ Marlena) defeated Razor Ramon (c) (14:17)
- **Notes:** 1-2-3 Kid interfered, costing Razor the title. This was Goldust's first Intercontinental Championship. Marlena (portrayed by Dustin Runnels' real-life wife Terri) made her debut as Goldust's valet. This marked the end of Razor Ramon's fourth and final IC title reign.
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Rumble_(1996)

## Injuries / Backstage Incidents / Controversies

### Shawn Michaels' Syracuse Incident Aftermath

**Incident:** Continued aftermath of October 1995 Syracuse bar fight

**Details:**
- Michaels continued to deal with the aftermath of being legitimately attacked by Marines in Syracuse on October 13, 1995
- The concussion angle was used on November 20, 1995 Raw to write Michaels off TV for knee surgery
- Michaels returned and won the Royal Rumble on January 21, 1996
- He then went on to defeat Bret Hart at WrestleMania XII

**Incident:** The Kliq (Shawn Michaels, Kevin Nash/Diesel, Scott Hall/Razor Ramon, Hunter Hearst Helmsley/Triple H, Sean Waltman/1-2-3 Kid) continued to have significant influence

**Details:**
- The Kliq dominated Royal Rumble 1996, with Michaels winning, Triple H being Iron Man, and Diesel lasting to the final two

### Bret Hart's Frustration

**Incident:** Bret Hart grew frustrated with his position in WWF

**Details:**
- Hart won the WWF Championship at Survivor Series 1995
- He was essentially holding the title for Shawn Michaels' WrestleMania win
- Hart was caught in the middle of Undertaker/Diesel storyline
- He was forced to lose to Michaels at WrestleMania XII in the Iron Man match
- Hart took a six-month hiatus after losing the title
- During that time off, his contract expired and he almost jumped ship to WCW
- **Source:** https://wrestlingrecaps.com/2010/08/24/wwf-wrestlemania-xii-3311996/

### Razor Ramon's Departure Setup

**Incident:** Razor Ramon's departure was set up during this period

**Details:**
- Razor lost the IC title to Goldust at Royal Rumble (January 21)
- He was originally scheduled to face Goldust in a rematch at WrestleMania XII in a Miami Street Fight
- However, Hall was suspended for six weeks due to drug use
- He had already given his notice via telegram and was looking to leave for WCW
- He would leave in May 1996 and join Kevin Nash (Diesel) and Hulk Hogan in forming the nWo
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scott_Hall

### Iron Man Match Controversy

**Incident:** The finish of the Iron Man match at WrestleMania XII was controversial

**Details:**
- The match ended 0-0 after 60 minutes
- WWF President Gorilla Monsoon ordered sudden death overtime
- Michaels pinned Hart after Sweet Chin Music
- Hart had agreed to lose cleanly but later expressed frustration about Michaels' refusal to return the favor
- Michaels' post-match celebration, where he told Hart to "get the hell out of my ring," wasn't part of the agreed-upon finish
- This marked the beginning of their real-life animosity, which would culminate in the Montreal Screwjob at Survivor Series 1997
- **Source:** https://prowrestlingstories.com/pro-wrestling-stories/bret-hart-shawn-michaels-rivalry-history-explosive-backstage-stories/

## Conclusion

The January-March 1996 period was a pivotal transitional phase in WWF, marking the end of the "New Generation" era and the beginning of the "Attitude Era." The period featured three major PPV events, with the most significant being WrestleMania XII, where Shawn Michaels defeated Bret Hart in the first-ever Iron Man match to win his first WWF Championship. The period also saw the debut of "The Ringmaster" Steve Austin, who would later become "Stone Cold" Steve Austin and the face of the Attitude Era. Goldust won the Intercontinental Championship from Razor Ramon at Royal Rumble, and both Razor Ramon (Scott Hall) and Diesel (Kevin Nash) would leave for WCW in May, setting the stage for the formation of the nWo. The period was marked by The Kliq's continued influence, Bret Hart's growing frustration, and the beginning of the real-life animosity between Hart and Michaels that would culminate in the Montreal Screwjob at Survivor Series 1997.
