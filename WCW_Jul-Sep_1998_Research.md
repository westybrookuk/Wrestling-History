# WCW Research Report: July 1998 – September 1998

> **Note re: continuity with previous reports:** At the end of June 1998, the titleholders were:
> - WCW World Heavyweight: **Sting & Kevin Nash** (tag team champions; Sting chose Nash as partner at Great American Bash, 6/14/1998)
> - WCW US Heavyweight: **Goldberg** (won from Raven on Nitro, ~late April 1998)
> - WCW World Tag Team: **Sting & Kevin Nash** (nWo Wolfpac) (Sting chose Nash at Great American Bash, 6/14/1998)
> - WCW World Television: **Booker T** (won from Finlay at Great American Bash, 6/14/1998)
> - WCW Cruiserweight: **Chris Jericho** (won from Malenko at Great American Bash, 6/14/1998)
>
> **Goldberg** was the US Champion with his undefeated streak building toward a World Title shot.
> **The nWo** was split into Hollywood (Hogan, Hall, Giant, Bret Hart, Hennig, Rude, Scott Steiner) and Wolfpac (Nash, Savage, Sting, Lex Luger, Konnan).
> **The Ultimate Warrior** was about to make his surprise return.

## 1. Title Changes

### 1A. WCW World Heavyweight Championship — Hollywood Hogan → Goldberg

- **Date:** July 6, 1998
- **Championship:** WCW World Heavyweight Championship
- **Winner:** Goldberg
- **Loser:** "Hollywood" Hogan (c) (nWo Hollywood)
- **Show:** WCW Monday Nitro
- **Air date:** July 6, 1998 (LIVE)
- **Venue:** Georgia Dome, Atlanta, Georgia
- **Attendance:** 41,412 (largest Nitro attendance ever; some sources say 40,000+)
- **Rating:** 4.8 (Nitro beat Raw's 4.0; main event drew 6.91, nearly 10 million viewers)
- **Details:**
  - Goldberg pinned Hogan at approximately 7:55–8:11 with the spear and Jackhammer.
  - This was Goldberg's **108th consecutive win** (some sources say 112-0 after this match).
  - Goldberg had TWO matches on this night:
    1. Defeated Scott Hall (c) to retain US Title (5:55)
    2. Defeated Hollywood Hogan (c) to WIN World Title (7:55–8:11)
  - After winning the World Title, Goldberg **vacated the US Title** (he couldn't hold both).
  - This was the **biggest moment in Nitro history** — a record crowd, a clean finish, and the passing of the torch from Hogan to Goldberg.
  - Hogan's 4th reign as WCW World Champion ended at 77 days (or 406 days total across all reigns, depending on how you count).
- **Why it mattered:**
  - **GOLDBERGMANIA** reached its peak. The undefeated streak was a phenomenon.
  - This was the **passing of the torch** from the nWo era to the Goldberg era.
  - The biggest Nitro attendance ever (41,412).
  - The biggest Nitro rating ever (4.8 overall, 6.91 for the main event).
  - WCW won the ratings war decisively that night.
  - This was WCW's biggest homegrown star defeating their biggest heel.

## 4. Injuries / Backstage Incidents / Controversies

### 4A. Goldberg's World Title Win — Clean Finish

- **Date:** July 6, 1998 (Nitro, Georgia Dome)
- **What happened:** Goldberg defeated Hogan cleanly with the spear and Jackhammer. No nWo interference. No controversial finish.
- **Why it mattered:** This was a **clean finish** — rare for WCW at the time. Hogan lost cleanly to Goldberg. This was the passing of the torch.
- **Confidence:** High.
- **Source:**
  - https://www.thesportster.com/wcw-goldberg-first-world-championship-atlanta/
  - https://www.ewrestlingnews.com/features/wrestling-history-the-day-goldberg-defeated-hollywood-hulk-hogan

### 4B. Mysterio's Cruiserweight Title Win — Vacated Due to Interference

- **Date:** July 12–13, 1998
- **What happened:** Mysterio won the Cruiserweight Title from Jericho at Bash at the Beach (July 12), but the title was vacated the next night on Nitro due to Dean Malenko's interference.
- **Why it mattered:** The title change was nullified. This was controversial and set up a Mysterio vs. Malenko feud.
- **Confidence:** High.
- **Source:**
  - https://grokipedia.com/page/Bash_at_the_Beach_(1998)
  - https://prowrestling.fandom.com/wiki/Bash_at_the_Beach_1998

### 4C. Scott Hall Wrestles "Drunk" at Fall Brawl

- **Date:** September 13, 1998 (Fall Brawl)
- **What happened:** Scott Hall wrestled Konnan at Fall Brawl while appearing to be "drunk" — he repeatedly went to Vincent for drinks from a cup he brought to the ring. Konnan won by submission.
- **Why it mattered:** This was worked into the angle — Hall's "drunk" behavior was part of his character. He was losing favor in the nWo.
- **Confidence:** High.
- **Source:**
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fall_Brawl_(1998)
  - https://thehistoryofwwe.com/wcw-fall-brawl-results/

### 4D. Scott Steiner vs. Rick Steiner — Brothers Fight

- **Date:** September 13, 1998 (Fall Brawl)
- **What happened:** Rick Steiner and Scott Steiner fought to a No Contest at Fall Brawl. The match ended when referee Mark Curtis stopped the match after noticing Buff Bagwell laying motionless on the apron after Rick grabbed him by the throat and rammed him into the steel ringpost.
- **Why it mattered:** The Steiner Brothers were effectively dissolved. Rick and Scott were on opposite sides.
- **Confidence:** High.
