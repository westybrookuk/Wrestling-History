# WWF January-March 2001 Research Report

**Period Covered:** January 1, 2001 - March 31, 2001

## Executive Summary

The January-March 2001 period was one of the most significant in WWF history, featuring two major PPV events — Royal Rumble (January 21, New Orleans) and No Way Out (February 25, Las Vegas) — and marked by Steve Austin winning his third Royal Rumble (first person to do so), The Rock winning the WWF Championship from Kurt Angle at No Way Out, and the continued dominance of the Attitude Era. Key developments included: Steve Austin winning the Royal Rumble by last eliminating Kane (Austin became first person to win three Royal Rumbles); Kane setting record for most eliminations in a single Royal Rumble (11 eliminations); Drew Carey's celebrity appearance in the Royal Rumble; Chris Jericho winning the Intercontinental Championship from Chris Benoit in a ladder match at Royal Rumble; Kurt Angle retaining the WWF Championship from Triple H at Royal Rumble; The Rock defeating Kurt Angle to win the WWF Championship at No Way Out; and the continued feud between Triple H and Steve Austin. Royal Rumble drew approximately 17,000 attendance at New Orleans Arena. No Way Out drew 15,223 attendance at Thomas & Mack Center in Las Vegas with 1.31 buyrate.

## Major PPV Events

### Royal Rumble 2001 (January 21, 2001)

**Location:** New Orleans Arena, New Orleans, Louisiana
**Attendance:** Approximately 17,000
**PPV Buyrate:** Not specified in sources

**PPV Results:**
1. **Chris Jericho defeated Chris Benoit (c)** — WWF Intercontinental Championship — Ladder Match (18:44) **Chris Jericho WON IC title**
2. **Ivory (c) defeated Chyna** — WWF Women's Championship (3:32) **Ivory retained**
3. **Kurt Angle (c) defeated Triple H** — WWF Championship (24:15) **Kurt Angle retained** [Stephanie McMahon-Helmsley and Trish Stratus fought halfway through match; Steve Austin interfered after ref-bump, knocked out Triple H with belt-shot and Stunner]
4. **"Stone Cold" Steve Austin won the 30-Man Royal Rumble Match** (1:01:55) **Steve Austin WON Royal Rumble** [Austin last eliminated Kane; Austin entered at #27 but was attacked by Triple H before entering ring; Austin became first person to win three Royal Rumbles; Kane set record with 11 eliminations]

**Royal Rumble Match Key Details:**
- 30 entrants; Steve Austin entered at #27
- Austin was attacked by Triple H before entering ring, left in bloody heap
- Austin eventually made it to ring, eliminated Haku
- Final four: Austin, The Rock, Kane, Billy Gunn
- Austin eliminated Gunn; Austin and Kane combined to eliminate The Rock
- Austin eliminated Kane to win (after multiple chair shots to head)
- Drew Carey (celebrity comedian) entered at #5, eliminated himself
- Kane set record with 11 eliminations
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Rumble_2001

### No Way Out 2001 (February 25, 2001)

**Location:** Thomas & Mack Center, Las Vegas, Nevada
**Attendance:** 15,223
**PPV Buyrate:** 1.31 (approximately 500,000 buys)

**PPV Results:**
1. **Rikishi defeated Matt Hardy** by DQ (3:50)
2. **The Big Show defeated Raven** — WWF Hardcore Championship [Billy Gunn ran in and pinned Raven to win Hardcore title via 24/7 rule; Big Show then pinned Raven/Billy Gunn to claim Hardcore title]
3. **Chris Jericho (c) defeated Eddie Guerrero, Chris Benoit, and X-Pac** — WWF Intercontinental Championship — Fatal 4-Way Match **Chris Jericho retained**
4. **Stephanie McMahon-Helmsley defeated Trish Stratus** — Women's Grudge Match
5. **Triple H defeated "Stone Cold" Steve Austin** — Three Stages of Hell Match — 2-1 [Triple H won 2-1]
6. **Steven Richards (with Ivory) defeated Jerry Lawler (with The Kat)** (5:31) [Due to pre-match stipulations, The Kat was forced to join Right to Censor]
7. **The Dudley Boyz (Bubba Ray & D-Von) defeated Edge & Christian and The Undertaker & Kane** — WWF Tag Team Championship — Three-Way Tables Match (12:06) **Dudley Boyz retained**
8. **The Rock defeated Kurt Angle (c)** — WWF Championship (16:53/16:54) **The Rock WON WWF Championship** [Rock hit two consecutive Rock Bottoms after Angle hit unprotected turnbuckle; Rock became six-time WWF Champion]

**Key Notes:**
- The Rock won WWF Championship from Kurt Angle
- Rock became six-time WWF Champion (tying Hulk Hogan's record)
- Triple H defeated Steve Austin in Three Stages of Hell match
- Dudley Boyz retained Tag Team titles in Three-Way Tables match
- Chris Jericho retained IC title in Fatal 4-Way match
- The Big Show won Hardcore Championship in chaotic 24/7 match
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_Way_Out_(2001)

## Conflicts with Previous Reports

### WWF October-December 2000 Report Context
- **World Title:** The Oct-Dec 2000 report showed Kurt Angle retaining at No Mercy, Survivor Series, and Armageddon. This report shows Angle retaining at Royal Rumble (Jan 21), then losing to The Rock at No Way Out (Feb 25).
- **IC Title:** The Oct-Dec 2000 report showed Chris Benoit winning from Billy Gunn at Armageddon (Dec 10). This report shows Benoit losing to Chris Jericho at Royal Rumble (Jan 21).

## Conclusion

The January-March 2001 period was one of the most significant in WWF history, featuring two major PPV events and historic moments. Steve Austin won his third Royal Rumble by last eliminating Kane, becoming the first person to win three Royal Rumbles. Kane set a record with 11 eliminations in the Royal Rumble. Drew Carey made a celebrity appearance in the Royal Rumble. Chris Jericho won the Intercontinental Championship from Chris Benoit in a ladder match at Royal Rumble. Kurt Angle retained the WWF Championship from Triple H at Royal Rumble. The Rock defeated Kurt Angle to win the WWF Championship at No Way Out, becoming a six-time WWF Champion and tying Hulk Hogan's record. Triple H defeated Steve Austin in a Three Stages of Hell match at No Way Out. The Dudley Boyz retained the WWF Tag Team Championship in a Three-Way Tables match at No Way Out. Royal Rumble drew approximately 17,000 attendance at New Orleans Arena. No Way Out drew 15,223 attendance at Thomas & Mack Center in Las Vegas with 1.31 buyrate.
