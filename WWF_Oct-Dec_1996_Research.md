# WWF October-December 1996 Research Report

**Period Covered:** October 1, 1996 - December 31, 1996

## Executive Summary

The October-December 1996 period marked a pivotal transition in WWF, featuring the debut of The Rock (as Rocky Maivia), Sycho Sid's first WWF Championship reign, and the continuation of the Undertaker-Mankind feud with the first-ever Buried Alive match. The period featured three major PPV events: In Your House 11: Buried Alive (October 20, Indianapolis), Survivor Series 1996 (November 17, New York), and In Your House 12: It's Time (December 15, West Palm Beach). The most significant developments were Sycho Sid winning the WWF Championship from Shawn Michaels at Survivor Series, Rocky Maivia's debut at Survivor Series (who would later become The Rock), and the first Buried Alive match between Undertaker and Mankind. Hunter Hearst Helmsley won the Intercontinental Championship from Marc Mero in late October. This period also saw the continued rise of "Stone Cold" Steve Austin and the beginning of the Bret Hart-Shawn Michaels rivalry that would culminate in the Montreal Screwjob at Survivor Series 1997. The fake Diesel and Razor Ramon angle continued but was universally panned.

## Major PPV Events

### WWF In Your House 11: Buried Alive (October 20, 1996)

**Location:** Market Square Arena, Indianapolis, Indiana
**Attendance:** 9,649
**PPV Buyrate:** 110,000

**Results:**
1. [Dark] The Stalker defeated Justin Bradshaw (w/ Uncle Zebekiah) (20:00)
2. "Stone Cold" Steve Austin defeated Hunter Hearst Helmsley (15:30)
3. Owen Hart & British Bulldog (c) (w/ Clarence Mason) defeated The Smoking Gunns (Bart & Billy Gunn) - WWF Tag Team Championship (9:17)
4. Marc Mero (c) (w/ Sable) defeated Goldust (w/ Marlena) - WWF Intercontinental Championship (11:38)
5. Sycho Sid defeated Vader (w/ Jim Cornette) - #1 Contender Match for WWF Championship (8:00)
6. **The Undertaker defeated Mankind (w/ Paul Bearer) - Buried Alive Match (18:25)** [First-ever Buried Alive match; Undertaker chokeslammed Mankind into grave, but Executioner (Terry Gordy) and others buried Undertaker after the match]
7. [Dark] The Godwinns defeated The New Rockers
8. [Dark] Shawn Michaels (c) defeated Goldust - WWF Championship

**Key Notes:**
- This was the first WWF PPV where the reigning world champion (Shawn Michaels) did not compete on-air
- First-ever Buried Alive match between Undertaker and Mankind
- Undertaker won the match but was buried alive after the match by Mankind's allies
- This was the final time Undertaker wore his traditional purple gloves
- Sycho Sid won #1 Contender spot by defeating Vader
- Steve Austin continued his rise with a win over Triple H
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_Your_House_11:_Buried_Alive

### WWF Survivor Series 1996 (November 17, 1996)

**Location:** Madison Square Garden, New York, New York
**Attendance:** 18,647
**PPV Buyrate:** 160,000

**Results:**
1. [Free For All] Aldo Montoya, Bart Gunn, Bob Holly, & Jesse James defeated Billy Gunn, Justin Bradshaw, Salvatore Sincere, & The Sultan (w/ Iron Sheik & Uncle Zebekiah) - 4-on-4 Survivor Series Elimination Match (10:46)
2. Doug Furnas, Henry Godwinn, Phil Lafon, & Phineas Godwinn (w/ Hillbilly Jim) defeated British Bulldog, Leif Cassidy, Marty Jannetty, & Owen Hart (w/ Clarence Mason) - 4-on-4 Survivor Series Elimination Match (20:41)
3. The Undertaker defeated Mankind by pinfall (14:54) [Paul Bearer was suspended above the ring in a shark cage]
4. Jake Roberts, Marc Mero, Rocky Maivia, & The Stalker (w/ Sable) defeated Crush, Goldust (w/ Marlena), Jerry Lawler, & Hunter Hearst Helmsley - 4-on-4 Survivor Series Elimination Match (23:44)
5. **Bret Hart defeated "Stone Cold" Steve Austin - #1 Contender Match for WWF Championship (28:36)**
6. Faarooq, Vader, "Razor Ramon" (Fake), & "Diesel" (Fake) (w/ Clarence Mason, PG-13, & Jim Cornette) vs. Flash Funk, Jimmy Snuka, Savio Vega, & Yokozuna - No Contest (9:48)
7. **Sycho Sid defeated Shawn Michaels (c) (w/ Jose Lothario) - WWF Championship (20:02)** [Sid hit Jose Lothario with TV camera, then hit Michaels with powerbomb]

**Key Notes:**
- **Rocky Maivia (The Rock) made his WWF debut** at Survivor Series, receiving mixed reactions (fans chanted "Die Rocky Die")
- Sycho Sid won his first WWF Championship, ending Shawn Michaels' 8-month reign
- Bret Hart defeated Steve Austin in #1 Contender match, beginning their legendary rivalry
- The Undertaker defeated Mankind with Paul Bearer suspended in shark cage
- First PPV appearance of fake Diesel and Razor Ramon
- Shawn Michaels was noticeably frustrated after losing the title
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Survivor_Series_(1996)

## Roster Movements / Debuts / Exits

### Key Debuts

**Rocky Maivia (The Rock) - November 17, 1996**
- **Event:** WWF Survivor Series 1996
- **Character:** Rocky Maivia, third-generation wrestler (son of Rocky Johnson, grandson of High Chief Peter Maivia)
- **Impact:** Rocky Maivia made his WWF debut at Survivor Series as part of Marc Mero's team. He received mixed reactions, with fans chanting "Die Rocky Die" due to his overly positive babyface character. He would later become "The Rock" and become one of the biggest stars in wrestling history.
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Survivor_Series_(1996)

### Key Character Developments

**The Executioner (Terry Gordy)**
- **Date:** October 20, 1996 (In Your House 11)
- **Character:** The Executioner, masked enforcer for Paul Bearer and Mankind
- **Impact:** The Executioner helped bury Undertaker alive after the Buried Alive match. He would continue his feud with Undertaker, losing to him at In Your House 12 in an Armageddon Rules match.
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_Your_House_11:_Buried_Alive

**Fake Diesel and Razor Ramon**
- **Date:** Continued throughout October-December 1996
- **Characters:** Glen Jacobs (future Kane) as "Fake Diesel" and Rick Bognar as "Fake Razor Ramon"
- **Impact:** The fake Diesel/Razor angle continued but was universally panned. They appeared at Survivor Series and In Your House 12, losing to Owen Hart & British Bulldog. The angle was a desperate attempt to counter WCW's nWo storyline.
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_Your_House_12:_It%27s_Time

### Key Storylines

**Bret Hart-Shawn Michaels Rivalry**
- **Date:** Began at Survivor Series 1996
- **Details:** After Sid won the WWF Championship from Michaels at Survivor Series, Michaels was visibly frustrated. The rivalry between Hart and Michaels began to intensify, with both men blaming each other for the title loss. This rivalry would culminate in the Montreal Screwjob at Survivor Series 1997.
- **Source:** https://pdrwrestling.net/2009/12/01/iyh-xii-it%E2%80%99s-time-12-96/

**Incident:** Rocky Maivia received overwhelmingly negative reactions from fans

**Details:**
- Date: November 17, 1996 (Survivor Series)
- Rocky Maivia made his WWF debut but received negative reactions
- Fans chanted "Die Rocky Die" due to his overly positive babyface character
- This was despite his impressive pedigree (third-generation wrestler)

### Undertaker Buried Alive

**Incident:** Undertaker was buried alive after winning Buried Alive match

**Details:**
- Date: October 20, 1996 (In Your House 11)
- Undertaker defeated Mankind in first-ever Buried Alive match
- After the match, The Executioner (Terry Gordy) and Mankind's allies buried Undertaker alive
- Undertaker's hand emerged from the grave as the show went dark
- This was the final time Undertaker wore his traditional purple gloves
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_Your_House_11:_Buried_Alive

The period featured three major PPV events, with the most significant being Survivor Series 1996, where Sycho Sid won the WWF Championship from Shawn Michaels and Rocky Maivia made his debut. This period also saw the continued rise of "Stone Cold" Steve Austin and the beginning of the Bret Hart-Shawn Michaels rivalry that would culminate in the Montreal Screwjob at Survivor Series 1997.
