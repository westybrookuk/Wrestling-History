# WCW Research Report: January 1997 – March 1997

> **Note re: continuity with previous reports:** At the end of December 1996, the titleholders were:
> - WCW World Heavyweight: **"Hollywood" Hogan** (nWo) (held since Hog Wild, 8/10/1996)
> - WCW US Heavyweight: **Eddie Guerrero** (won at Starrcade, 12/29/1996)
> - WCW World Tag Team: **The Outsiders** (Hall & Nash) (nWo) (won at Halloween Havoc, 10/27/1996)
> - WCW World Television: **Prince Iaukea** (exact date uncertain, held through this period)
> - WCW Cruiserweight: **Último Dragón** (unified with J-Crown at Starrcade, 12/29/1996)
> - WCW Women's: **Akira Hokuto** (inaugural, 12/29/1996)
>
> The Giant had been kicked out of the nWo (January 4, Saturday Night — after hesitating to chokeslam Piper at Starrcade). Sting was still in the rafters as Crow. nWo dominated everything.

## 2. Major TV/PPV Events

### 2A. Sting Comes Down from the Rafters — January 20, 1997

- **Air date:** January 20, 1997 (Nitro #71, LIVE from Chicago)
- **Show:** WCW Monday Nitro
- **Venue:** United Center, Chicago, Illinois
- **What happened:**
  - Randy Savage (on crutches, in black) had taken over Nitro, refusing to leave the ring.
  - **Sting descended from the rafters for the FIRST TIME** — rappelling down on wires, baseball bat in hand.
  - Sting confronted Savage. The crowd went INSANE.
  - Sting circled Savage with the bat, poked him in the chest, threatened him.
  - **Then Sting did the unexpected: he handed Savage the bat and turned his back.**
  - Savage refused to strike. Sting then LEFT WITH SAVAGE through the crowd.
  - The two left the arena together.
- **Why it mattered:** This was listed as one of the 20 greatest Nitro moments ever. Sting had been silent and in the rafters for 3 months. His first physical appearance — coming down on wires — was electric. The moment with Savage (handing him the bat, leaving together) was ambiguous and electrifying.

### 2B. Souled Out 1997 — January 25, 1997

- **PPV date:** January 25, 1997
- **Show:** WCW: nWo Souled Out (the first nWo-branded PPV)
- **Venue:** Five Seasons Center, Cedar Rapids, Iowa
- **Buyrate:** 170,000
- **What happened:**
  - **Format:** WCW vs. nWo — every match was one side vs. the other.
  - Masahiro Chono (nWo) def. Chris Jericho (11:08)
  - Big Bubba Rogers (nWo) def. Hugh Morrus (Mexican Death, 9:03)
  - Jeff Jarrett (WCW) def. Mr. Wallstreet (nWo) (9:22) — Steve McMichael interference
  - Buff Bagwell (nWo) def. Scotty Riggs (13:51)
  - Scott Norton (nWo) def. Diamond Dallas Page (countout, 9:39)
  - **Steiner Brothers (WCW) def. Outsiders (c) to WIN Tag Titles** (14:43)
  - **Eddie Guerrero (c) (WCW) def. Syxx (nWo) in Ladder Match to RETAIN US Title** (13:50)
  - **Hollywood Hogan (nWo) (c) vs. The Giant (WCW) — NO CONTEST** (10:52) — Giant chokeslammed Hogan, Nick Patrick refused to count, nWo attacked Giant, Hogan hit Giant with guitar. Giant's WarGames title shot was wasted.
- **Why it mattered:**
  - Steiner Brothers won the tag titles (returned next night — see above).
  - Eddie retained US Title in a classic ladder match.
  - Giant's title shot was squandered by nWo interference — the entire event showed how rigged WCW had become.
  - The nWo-branded PPV concept was unprecedented.
  - Buyrate of 170,000 was strong.

## 4. Injuries / Backstage Incidents / Controversies

### 4A. Steiner Brothers Title Win Reversed (January 27, 1997)

- **Date:** January 27, 1997 (Nitro)
- **What happened:** Bischoff fired referee Randy Anderson and stripped the Steiner Brothers of the tag titles they had legitimately won at Souled Out, returning them to the Outsiders.
- **Why it mattered:** This was a watershed moment for WCW booking. The audience saw that legitimate title wins could be erased by nWo management. Fan trust eroded further.
- **Confidence:** High.
- **Source:**
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Souled_Out_(1997)

### 4B. Luger/Giant Title Win Reversed (February 24, 1997)

- **Date:** February 24, 1997 (Nitro)
- **What happened:** Bischoff stripped Luger & Giant of the tag titles (won at SuperBrawl VII) citing Luger's lack of medical clearance. Titles returned to Outsiders.
- **Why it mattered:** Same pattern as January — legitimate babyface title wins erased by nWo-favoring authority. Fans were frustrated.
- **Confidence:** High.
- **Source:**
  - https://grokipedia.com/page/SuperBrawl_VII
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1997_in_professional_wrestling

### 4C. SuperBrawl VII Main Event — Worst Finish Ever

- **Date:** February 23, 1997
- **What happened:** Piper clearly won via sleeper (Hogan's hand down three times). Match was restarted for no explained reason. Savage (supposedly injured) handed Hogan knucks. Hogan won. Savage turned heel.
- **Why it mattered:** Voted **Worst Match of the Year** by Wrestling Observer. The booking was so bad it became infamous.
- **Confidence:** High.
- **Source:**
  - http://thewrestlingsection.blogspot.com/2016/04/worst-in-world-hollywood-hogan-vs-roddy.html
  - https://www.cagematch.net/?id=1&nr=1628

### 4D. nWo Dominance Creating Viewer Fatigue

- **Date:** January–March 1997 (ongoing)
- **What happened:** The nWo won almost everything. Babyfaces couldn't hold titles. Title wins were reversed. The Souled Out PPV was entirely nWo vs. WCW. The booking was completely one-sided.
- **Why it mattered:** While nWo heat was still strong, the one-sided booking was beginning to create viewer fatigue. Critics noted this would become a problem by mid-1997.
- **Confidence:** High.
- **Source:**
  - https://grokipedia.com/page/SuperBrawl_VII ("the event's reliance on predictable nWo victories... drew criticism for reinforcing one-sided booking that began fostering viewer fatigue by mid-1997")

## 7. Potential Implementation Corrections

### Event Timing Corrections
- **Souled Out (Jan 25):** nWo-branded PPV. Steiner Brothers win tag titles legitimately — then stripped next night.
- **SuperBrawl VII (Feb 23):** Savage turns heel. Luger/Giant win tag titles — stripped next night.
- **Uncensored (Mar 16):** Malenko wins US Title. nWo wins Triangle Elimination.
- **Title reversals:** Implementation must handle the unique situation of titles being won and returned within 24 hours (Steiner Brothers, Luger/Giant).

### Missing Roster Additions
- **nWo recruits (Jan 1997):** Buff Bagwell, Mr. Wallstreet, Masahiro Chono, Big Bubba, Scott Norton all join nWo in this period.
- **Randy Savage** — must be moved from babyface to nWo heel faction at SuperBrawl VII (Feb 23).
- **The Giant** — must be moved from nWo to babyface faction (kicked out Jan 4).

### Title Lineage Adjustments
- **Tag Titles:** The Jan 25 and Feb 23 babyface wins should be tracked but marked as "reversed by management" — NOT as legitimate reigns for game purposes. The Outsiders should be shown as continuous champions from the babyface perspective.
- **Cruiserweight Title:** Syxx wins at SuperBrawl (Feb 23). Implementation must track Malenko → Syxx.
- **US Title:** Eddie → Malenko at Uncensored (Mar 16).
- **World Title:** Hogan continuous. No change.

### Faction/Team Addition
- **nWo expanded:** Now includes Buff Bagwell, Wallstreet, Big Bubba, Scott Norton, Chono, Savage (from Feb 23).
- **nWo Japan** established under Chono.
- **Giant removed from nWo** — becomes babyface.

### Injury/Morale/Business Effect
- **Title reversals:** MASSIVE NEGATIVE for fan trust and tag title prestige. This must be implemented as a major trust penalty.
- **Savage's heel turn:** MASSIVE for nWo morale/momentum. Negative for babyface morale.
- **SuperBrawl main event:** NEGATIVE for PPV quality/morale. Worst Match of the Year.
- **Sting's rafter descent:** MASSIVE POSITIVE for fan morale/trust. Sting is the only hope.
- **nWo membership drive:** Positive for nWo dominance. Negative for WCW overall diversity.
