# WWF October-December 1999 Research Report

**Period Covered:** October 1, 1999 - December 31, 1999

## Executive Summary

The October-December 1999 period was one of the most chaotic and significant in WWF history, featuring four major PPV events — Rebellion (October 2, Birmingham, UK-only), No Mercy (October 17, Cleveland), Survivor Series (November 14, Detroit), and Armageddon (December 12, Sunrise) — and marked by massive title changes including The Big Show winning his first WWF Championship, Chris Jericho winning the Intercontinental Championship, and the beginning of the McMahon-Helmsley Era. Key developments included: Triple H retaining the WWF Championship from Steve Austin at No Mercy in an Anything Goes match with Rock interference; The Big Show winning the WWF Championship from Triple H in a Triple Threat match also involving The Rock at Survivor Series (Big Show replaced Austin who was "hit by a car" - actually Rikishi under Triple H's orders); Triple H defeating Vince McMahon in a No Holds Barred match at Armageddon with Stephanie McMahon's help, beginning the McMahon-Helmsley Era; Chyna becoming the first female Intercontinental Champion by defeating Jeff Jarrett at No Mercy; Chris Jericho winning the Intercontinental Championship from Chyna at Armageddon; and various other title changes. The period also saw Steve Austin written off television for neck surgery (on-screen: hit by car), the rise of Chris Jericho, the McMahon-Helmsley takeover of WWF, and the Rock 'n' Sock Connection tag team. Rebellion drew approximately 13,500 attendance in Birmingham. No Mercy drew 16,493 attendance in Cleveland. Survivor Series drew 21,179 attendance in Detroit. Armageddon drew 15,749 attendance in Sunrise.

## Conclusion

The October-December 1999 period was one of the most chaotic and significant in WWF history, featuring four major PPV events and massive title changes. Triple H retained the WWF Championship from Steve Austin at No Mercy in an Anything Goes match with Rock interference. The Big Show won his first WWF Championship from Triple H in a Triple Threat match also involving The Rock at Survivor Series (Big Show replaced Austin who was "hit by a car" - actually Rikishi under Triple H's orders). Triple H defeated Vince McMahon in a No Holds Barred match at Armageddon with Stephanie McMahon's help, beginning the McMahon-Helmsley Era. Chyna became the first female Intercontinental Champion by defeating Jeff Jarrett at No Mercy. Chris Jericho won the Intercontinental Championship from Chyna at Armageddon. The period also saw Steve Austin written off television for neck surgery, the rise of Chris Jericho, Kurt Angle's debut, and various other title changes. Rebellion drew approximately 13,500 attendance in Birmingham. No Mercy drew 16,493 attendance in Cleveland. Survivor Series drew 21,179 attendance in Detroit. Armageddon drew 15,749 attendance in Sunrise.
