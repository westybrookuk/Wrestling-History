## Executive Summary

The January-March 2004 period marked one of the most emotionally significant quarters in WWE history, featuring three major PPV events: **Royal Rumble** (January 25, Philadelphia, PA), **No Way Out** (February 15, Daly City, CA), and **WrestleMania XX** (March 14, New York City, NY). This period is forever remembered for the historic achievements of Chris Benoit and Eddie Guerrero, who both won world championships at WrestleMania XX in one of the most emotional moments in wrestling history.

**Key developments included:**
- **Chris Benoit** made history by winning the Royal Rumble from the #1 position, becoming only the second person ever to do so (after Shawn Michaels in 1995)
- **Eddie Guerrero** won his first and only WWE Championship by defeating Brock Lesnar at No Way Out
- **Chris Benoit** won the World Heavyweight Championship at WrestleMania XX in a Triple Threat match against Triple H and Shawn Michaels
- **Evolution** kicked out Randy Orton after he won the Intercontinental Championship
- **Goldberg vs. Brock Lesnar** had their final match at WrestleMania XX with Steve Austin as special guest referee
- **The Undertaker** defeated Kane at WrestleMania XX, extending his WrestleMania streak to 12-0

**PPV Statistics:**
- **Royal Rumble:** 16,000+ attendance (Philadelphia), estimated 400,000+ buys
- **No Way Out:** 11,000 attendance, 350,000 buys, $450,000 gate
- **WrestleMania XX:** 20,000+ attendance (Madison Square Garden), estimated 500,000+ buys

## Injuries / Backstage Incidents / Controversies

### Evolution Kicks Out Randy Orton
After Randy Orton won the Intercontinental Championship at Royal Rumble, Evolution (Triple H, Ric Flair, Batista) kicked him out of the stable. Orton was attacked by his former stablemates, beginning a feud that would continue for months. This was a significant moment as it ended Orton's association with Evolution and established him as a singles competitor.

### Chris Benoit's Royal Rumble Victory
Chris Benoit made history by winning the Royal Rumble from the #1 position, becoming only the second person to do so. Benoit lasted over 61 minutes, breaking Bob Backlund's longevity record from 1993. This victory was a major moment in Benoit's career and set up his World Heavyweight Championship win at WrestleMania XX.

### Eddie Guerrero's WWE Championship Win
At No Way Out, Eddie Guerrero defeated Brock Lesnar to win his first and only WWE Championship. This was Guerrero's crowning achievement in WWE and one of the most emotional moments in wrestling history. Guerrero had been told for years he could never be a top champion, and this victory proved his doubters wrong.

### The Historic WrestleMania XX Moment
At WrestleMania XX, both Chris Benoit and Eddie Guerrero achieved their dreams of becoming world champions. After Benoit won the World Heavyweight Championship, Guerrero came to the ring and both men embraced with tears in their eyes. This moment is considered one of the most emotional in wrestling history - two longtime friends who had been told they could never be champions, now standing together as champions at WrestleMania.

### Goldberg vs. Brock Lesnar Final Match
The Goldberg vs. Brock Lesnar match at WrestleMania XX was their final match (at the time) with Steve Austin as special guest referee. After the match, Austin gave both men Stone Cold Stunners. This was the final match in WWE for both men (temporarily), as they both left the company shortly after.

### Molly Holly's Head Shaved
At WrestleMania XX, Victoria defeated Molly Holly in a Hair vs. Title match. As per the stipulation, Molly Holly had her head shaved bald. This was a significant moment for Holly's character and one of the most talked-about moments of WrestleMania XX.

### The Undertaker's WrestleMania Streak
The Undertaker defeated Kane at WrestleMania XX, extending his WrestleMania undefeated streak to 12-0. Paul Bearer returned as Undertaker's manager for this match.

## Conclusion

The January-March 2004 period was defined by the historic achievements of Chris Benoit and Eddie Guerrero, who both achieved their dreams of becoming world champions at WrestleMania XX. This period is forever remembered for one of the most emotional moments in wrestling history, when both men stood together at Madison Square Garden as champions after being told for years they could never reach the top.

Royal Rumble featured Chris Benoit making history by winning from the #1 position, setting up his World Heavyweight Championship victory at WrestleMania XX. No Way Out was highlighted by Eddie Guerrero's emotional WWE Championship win over Brock Lesnar, the crowning achievement of his career before his tragic death in November 2005.

WrestleMania XX at Madison Square Garden was a landmark event, featuring both Benoit and Guerrero winning their respective championships, Goldberg and Brock Lesnar's final match with Steve Austin as special guest referee, and The Undertaker extending his WrestleMania streak to 12-0. The event also saw Evolution kick out Randy Orton, John Cena win the United States Championship, and Victoria win the Women's Championship (with Molly Holly having her head shaved).

By the end of March 2004, the championship landscape was: Eddie Guerrero (WWE Champion, SmackDown!), Chris Benoit (World Heavyweight Champion, Raw), Randy Orton (Intercontinental Champion, Raw, but kicked out of Evolution), Evolution/Flair & Batista (World Tag Team Champions, Raw), Too Cool (WWE Tag Team Champions, SmackDown!), Victoria (Women's Champion, Raw), Chavo Guerrero (Cruiserweight Champion, SmackDown!), and John Cena (United States Champion, SmackDown!).

The period also marked the temporary departure of Goldberg and Brock Lesnar from WWE after their WrestleMania XX match, and the beginning of Randy Orton's feud with Evolution after being kicked out of the stable.
