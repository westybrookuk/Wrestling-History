## 1. Title Changes

### ECW World Heavyweight Championship — Justin Credible → Jerry Lynn → Steve Corino

**Title Change #1: Justin Credible → Jerry Lynn**

- **Date:** October 1, 2000
- **Championship:** ECW World Heavyweight Championship
- **Winner:** Jerry Lynn
- **Loser:** Justin Credible (c) (w/ Francine)
- **Show:** ECW Anarchy Rulz 2000
- **Venue:** Roy Wilkins Auditorium
- **City:** Saint Paul, Minnesota
- **Attendance:** 4,600
- **Buy rate:** 55,000
- **Finish:** Jerry Lynn pinned Justin Credible (c) to win the ECW World Heavyweight Championship (19:36). Credible superkicked the referee to knock him out. Danny Daniels replaced him and began officiating in favor of Credible. Daniels nailed Lynn with a kendo stick, allowing Credible to deliver "That's Incredible." New Jack broke the pin and attacked Daniels. Lynn then reversed Credible's "That's Incredible" into a cradle piledriver to win the title.
- **Event type:** Pay-per-view
- **Why it mattered:** Jerry Lynn won the ECW World Heavyweight Championship in his hometown (Saint Paul, Minnesota), ending Justin Credible's dominant reign that began at CyberSlam 2000 (April 22). This was Lynn's first ECW World Heavyweight Championship reign. The finish was heavily criticized as overbooked, with Danny Daniels' interference and New Jack's involvement creating controversy.

**Title Change #2: Jerry Lynn → Steve Corino**

- **Date:** November 5, 2000
- **Championship:** ECW World Heavyweight Championship
- **Winner:** Steve Corino (w/ Jack Victory & Dawn Marie)
- **Loser:** Jerry Lynn (c), Justin Credible (w/ Francine), and The Sandman
- **Show:** ECW November to Remember 2000
- **Venue:** Odeum Expo Center
- **City:** Villa Park, Illinois
- **Attendance:** 4,600
- **Finish:** Double Jeopardy Match. The match featured two simultaneous singles contests: Jerry Lynn vs. Justin Credible and The Sandman vs. Steve Corino. Justin Credible eliminated Jerry Lynn (16:22) and Steve Corino eliminated The Sandman (16:22) simultaneously. In the final, Steve Corino defeated Justin Credible with a superkick (Old School Expulsion) to win the title (24:21).
- **Event type:** Pay-per-view
- **Why it mattered:** Steve Corino won the ECW World Heavyweight Championship in a controversial Double Jeopardy match. The elimination of Jerry Lynn and The Sandman simultaneously drew loud "bullshit" chants from fans. The match was widely criticized as a poor booking decision, with many feeling Corino should have defeated Lynn directly rather than going through the confusing Double Jeopardy stipulation. This was Corino's first ECW World Heavyweight Championship reign.

### ECW on TNN Cancellation — October 6, 2000

- **People involved:** Paul Heyman, ECW management, TNN/CBS, WWF
- **Type:** Television deal cancellation
- **What happened:** ECW on TNN was cancelled after 59 episodes (19 in 1999, 40 in 2000) in favor of WWF Raw is War moving to TNN. The final episode aired on October 6, 2000 to a 0.6 rating. ECW's average rating was 0.94, well below TNN's minimum threshold.
- **What was confirmed:** ECW lost its only national television deal; WWF Raw replaced ECW on TNN
- **What is disputed:** Whether ECW could have done more to improve ratings
- **Immediate creative/business impact:** ECW lost all national television exposure; struggled to find new deal
- **Long-term impact:** Paul Heyman stated the inability to land another national television deal was the main cause of ECW's demise; ECW closed in April 2001

### ECW Financial Collapse — Ongoing (Q4 2000)

- **People involved:** Paul Heyman, ECW management
- **Type:** Financial collapse
- **What happened:** ECW's financial situation reached a critical point in Q4 2000. The promotion owed $710,295 to 34 performers and staff alone. PPV distributors would not pay money owed to ECW due to Heyman still not being able to sign with a TV network. ECW was unable to pay wrestlers for a while. Heyman had not told his wrestlers that ECW was on its last legs.
- **What was confirmed:** ECW owed $710,295 to performers and staff; unable to pay wrestlers; PPV distributors withheld payment
