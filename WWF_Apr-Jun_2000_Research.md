# WWF April-June 2000 Research Report

**Period Covered:** April 1, 2000 - June 30, 2000

## Executive Summary

The April-June 2000 period was one of the most significant in WWF history, featuring three major PPV events — Backlash (April 30, Washington D.C.), Judgment Day (May 21, Louisville), and King of the Ring (June 25, Boston) — and marked by the continuation of the McMahon-Helmsley Era, The Rock's WWF Championship victories, Triple H's Iron Man match victory, Kurt Angle's King of the Ring victory, and The Undertaker's "American Badass" gimmick change. Key developments included: The Rock defeating Triple H for the WWF Championship at Backlash with help from Stone Cold Steve Austin and Linda McMahon; Triple H defeating The Rock 6-5 in a 60-minute Iron Man match at Judgment Day with Shawn Michaels as special guest referee (Undertaker's interference caused Rock's disqualification); Kurt Angle winning the 2000 King of the Ring tournament by defeating Rikishi in the finals; The Rock winning the WWF Championship from the McMahon-Helmsley Faction in a six-man tag team match at King of the Ring by pinning Vince McMahon; and The Undertaker debuting his "American Badass" gimmick at Judgment Day. Backlash drew 17,867 attendance at MCI Center in Washington D.C. with 1.62 buyrate. Judgment Day drew 16,827 attendance at Freedom Hall in Louisville with 420,000 buys. King of the Ring drew approximately 17,000 attendance at Fleet Center in Boston.

## Major PPV Events

### Backlash 2000 (April 30, 2000)

**Location:** MCI Center / Verizon Center, Washington, D.C.
**Attendance:** 17,867
**PPV Buyrate:** 1.62 (approximately 540,000 buys)

**PPV Results:**
1. **Edge & Christian (c) defeated D-Generation X (Road Dogg & X-Pac) (with Tori)** — WWF Tag Team Championship (9:23) **Edge & Christian retained**
2. **Dean Malenko (c) defeated Scotty 2 Hotty** — WWF Light Heavyweight Championship (12:59) **Malenko retained**
3. **The Big Boss Man & Bull Buchanan defeated The Acolytes (Faarooq & Bradshaw)** (7:41)
4. **Crash Holly defeated Matt Hardy, Jeff Hardy, Hardcore Holly, Perry Saturn, and Tazz** — WWF Hardcore Championship — Six-Man Hardcore Match (12:20) **Crash Holly retained**
5. **The Big Show defeated Kurt Angle** (2:57)
6. **T & A (Test & Albert) (with Trish Stratus) defeated The Dudley Boyz (Bubba Ray & D-Von Dudley)** (11:04)
7. **Eddie Guerrero (c) (with Chyna) defeated Essa Rios (with Lita)** — WWF European Championship (8:43) **Guerrero retained**
8. **Chris Benoit (c) defeated Chris Jericho** by disqualification — WWF Intercontinental Championship (15:08) **Benoit retained**
9. **The Rock (with "Stone Cold" Steve Austin and Linda McMahon) defeated Triple H (c) (with Vince McMahon and Stephanie McMahon)** — WWF Championship — Special Guest Referee: Shane McMahon (19:24) **The Rock WON WWF Championship** [Vince and Stephanie at ringside; Shane as referee; Austin returned to help Rock; Linda McMahon made Earl Hebner count the pin; Rock hit spinebuster and People's Elbow; Austin and Rock celebrated with beers]

**Key Notes:**
- The Rock won WWF Championship from Triple H with help from Austin and Linda McMahon
- Stone Cold Steve Austin returned to help The Rock
- Linda McMahon made Earl Hebner count the pin
- Austin and Rock celebrated together with beers
- Shane McMahon was special guest referee (giving Triple H advantage)
- Vince and Stephanie McMahon at ringside (giving Triple H advantage)
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backlash_(2000)

### Judgment Day 2000 (May 21, 2000)

**Location:** Freedom Hall, Louisville, Kentucky
**Attendance:** 16,827
**PPV Buyrate:** 420,000

**PPV Results:**
1. **Too Cool (Grand Master Sexay, Rikishi & Scotty 2 Hotty) defeated Team ECK (Edge, Christian & Kurt Angle)** — Six-Man Tag Team Match (9:47)
2. **Eddie Guerrero (c) (with Chyna) defeated Dean Malenko and Perry Saturn** — WWF European Championship — Triple Threat Match (7:56) **Guerrero retained**
3. **Shane McMahon defeated The Big Show** — Falls Count Anywhere Match (7:12)
4. **Chris Benoit (c) defeated Chris Jericho** — WWF Intercontinental Championship — Submission Match (13:27) **Benoit retained**
5. **D-Generation X (Road Dogg & X-Pac) (with Tori) defeated The Dudley Boyz (Bubba Ray & D-Von Dudley)** — Tag Team Tables Match (10:55)
6. **Triple H (with Shane McMahon, Stephanie McMahon-Helmsley & Mr. McMahon) defeated The Rock (c) 6-5** — WWF Championship — 60-Minute Iron Man Match — Special Guest Referee: Shawn Michaels (60:08) **Triple H WON WWF Championship** [Undertaker's interference caused Rock's disqualification in final seconds; Undertaker debuted "American Badass" gimmick]

**Iron Man Match Fall-by-Fall:**
1. Rock pinned Triple H (Rock Bottom) - 10:42 - Rock 1-0
2. Triple H pinned Rock (Pedigree) - 25:26 - 1-1
3. Triple H pinned Rock (inside cradle) - 26:26 - Triple H 2-1
4. Triple H pinned Rock (Piledriver) - 32:22 - Triple H 3-1
5. Rock pinned Triple H (DDT) - 40:33 - Triple H 3-2
6. Rock won by disqualification (Triple H used chair) - 43:30 - 3-3
7. Triple H pinned Rock (using ropes for leverage) - 44:06 - Triple H 4-3
8. Triple H won by technical submission (sleeper hold) - 47:25 - Triple H 5-3
9. Rock won by countout (Triple H counted out after Pedigree on table) - 56:05 - Triple H 5-4
10. Rock pinned Triple H (People's Elbow) - 57:59 - 5-5
11. Rock disqualified (Undertaker interference) - 60:08 - Triple H 6-5 (WINNER)

**Key Notes:**
- Triple H won WWF Championship from The Rock in 60-minute Iron Man match
- Shawn Michaels was special guest referee
- Undertaker debuted "American Badass" gimmick (longer hair, bandanna, leather trench coat, Harley Davidson)
- Undertaker's interference caused Rock's disqualification in final seconds
- Triple H won 6 falls to 5
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judgment_Day_(2000)

### King of the Ring 2000 (June 25, 2000)

**Location:** Fleet Center, Boston, Massachusetts
**Attendance:** Approximately 17,000
**PPV Buyrate:** Not specified in sources

**King of the Ring Tournament:**

**Quarterfinals:**
1. **Rikishi defeated Chris Benoit** by disqualification (3:25) [Benoit disqualified for repeatedly attacking Rikishi with steel chair]
2. **Val Venis (with Trish Stratus) defeated Eddie Guerrero (with Chyna)** (8:04)
3. **Crash Holly defeated Bull Buchanan** (4:07)
4. **Kurt Angle defeated Chris Jericho** (9:50) [Stephanie McMahon distracted referee, allowing Angle to hit Olympic Slam]

**Semifinals:**
5. **Rikishi defeated Val Venis** (3:15)
6. **Kurt Angle defeated Crash Holly** (3:58)

**Final:**
7. **Kurt Angle defeated Rikishi** — King of the Ring Final (5:56) **Kurt Angle WON King of the Ring 2000** [Angle hit Olympic Slam off top rope]

**Other PPV Matches:**
8. **Edge & Christian defeated Too Cool (Grand Master Sexay & Scotty 2 Hotty) (c), The Hardy Boyz (Matt & Jeff Hardy) (with Lita), and T & A (Albert & Test) (with Trish Stratus)** — WWF Tag Team Championship — Fatal 4-Way Elimination Match (14:11) **Edge & Christian WON Tag Team titles** [Edge pinned Sexay after Christian hit Sexay with tag title belt]
9. **Crash Holly pinned Pat Patterson (c) and Gerald Brisco** — WWF Hardcore Championship — Hardcore Evening Gown Match (3:07) **Crash Holly WON Hardcore title** [24/7 Rule]
10. **D-Generation X (Tori, Road Dogg & X-Pac) defeated The Dudley Boyz (Bubba Ray & D-Von)** — Handicap Tables Dumpster Match (9:45)
11. **The Rock, Kane & The Undertaker defeated Triple H (c), Vince McMahon & Shane McMahon (with Stephanie McMahon-Helmsley)** — WWF Championship — Six-Man Tag Team Match (17:54) **The Rock WON WWF Championship** [Stipulation: Whoever scored the pinfall would win the WWF Championship; Rock pinned Vince McMahon with Rock Bottom]

**Key Notes:**
- Kurt Angle won King of the Ring tournament by defeating Rikishi in finals
- The Rock won WWF Championship from McMahon-Helmsley Faction in six-man tag
- Rock pinned Vince McMahon (not Triple H) to win title
- Edge & Christian won Tag Team titles from Too Cool
- Crash Holly won Hardcore title in Evening Gown match
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_of_the_Ring_(2000)

- **World Title:** The Jan-Mar 2000 report showed Triple H winning from Big Show on Raw (Jan 3), retaining at WrestleMania 2000 (Apr 2). This report shows Triple H losing to Rock at Backlash (Apr 30), winning back at Judgment Day (May 21), losing to Rock at King of the Ring (Jun 25).

## Conclusion

The April-June 2000 period was one of the most significant in WWF history, featuring three major PPV events and the continuation of the McMahon-Helmsley Era. The Rock defeated Triple H for the WWF Championship at Backlash with help from Stone Cold Steve Austin and Linda McMahon. Triple H defeated The Rock 6-5 in a 60-minute Iron Man match at Judgment Day with Shawn Michaels as special guest referee (Undertaker's interference caused Rock's disqualification). Kurt Angle won the 2000 King of the Ring tournament by defeating Rikishi in the finals. The Rock won the WWF Championship from the McMahon-Helmsley Faction in a six-man tag team match at King of the Ring by pinning Vince McMahon. The Undertaker debuted his "American Badass" gimmick at Judgment Day. Backlash drew 17,867 attendance at MCI Center in Washington D.C. with 1.62 buyrate. Judgment Day drew 16,827 attendance at Freedom Hall in Louisville with 420,000 buys. King of the Ring drew approximately 17,000 attendance at Fleet Center in Boston.
