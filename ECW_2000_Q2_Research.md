**Title Change #1: Mike Awesome → Taz**

- **Date:** April 13, 2000
- **Taping date:** April 13, 2000 (aired April 14, 2000 on ECW on TNN)
- **Championship:** ECW World Heavyweight Championship
- **Winner:** Taz (WWF-contracted wrestler)
- **Loser:** Mike Awesome (c) (WCW-contracted wrestler)
- **Show:** ECW House Show / ECW on TNN taping
- **Venue:** Market Square Arena (event in Indianapolis, IN area)
- **City:** Indianapolis, Indiana
- **Attendance:** [UNCONFIRMED — FLAG]
- **Finish:** Taz defeated Mike Awesome (c) to win the ECW World Heavyweight Championship. Tommy Dreamer gave Awesome a DDT, and Taz locked in the Tazmission for the win.
- **Event type:** House show / TV taping
- **Why it mattered:** This was one of the most unique moments in professional wrestling history. A WWF-contracted wrestler (Taz) defeated a WCW-contracted wrestler (Mike Awesome) for a third promotion's (ECW) world championship. The match was arranged after Awesome abandoned the ECW title by jumping to WCW on April 10, 2000. Paul Heyman filed a legal injunction, and WWF's Vince McMahon agreed to loan Taz for a one-night appearance to reclaim the title. WCW's Eric Bischoff ultimately agreed to the arrangement. This was the only way to resolve the three-way contractual dispute over the ECW World Heavyweight Championship.

## 2. Major TV, PPV, Supercard, and Arena Events

### Mike Awesome's WCW Departure & Taz Title Win — April 10-14, 2000

- **Date:** April 10, 2000 (WCW debut); April 13, 2000 (title loss to Taz)
- **What happened:**
  - April 6-7, 2000: Mike Awesome no-showed ECW house shows in Cleveland and Warren, OH
  - April 10, 2000: Mike Awesome appeared on WCW Monday Nitro, attacking Kevin Nash, while still reigning as ECW World Heavyweight Champion
  - Paul Heyman filed a legal injunction to prevent Awesome from appearing on WCW programming with the ECW title
  - There were rumors Eric Bischoff wanted Awesome to throw the ECW belt in a trash can on WCW TV (like Madusa did with the WWF Women's title)
  - WCW refrained from showing the belt but acknowledged Awesome as champion
  - A compromise was reached: WWF's Vince McMahon loaned Taz for a one-night appearance
  - April 13, 2000: Taz (WWF employee) defeated Mike Awesome (WCW employee) at an ECW event in Indianapolis to reclaim the title
  - WCW ultimately paid ECW $500,000 in settlements stemming from Awesome's contract dispute
- **Why it mattered:** This was one of the most controversial departures in wrestling history. A reigning champion jumping to a rival promotion was unprecedented. The three-way legal battle between ECW, WCW, and WWF resulted in a unique interpromotional title change. Lance Storm noted that Awesome was owed significant money by ECW and was justified in leaving. The incident highlighted ECW's financial struggles and the talent raid by larger promotions.

- **Change:** Departed ECW for WCW while still ECW World Heavyweight Champion

- **People involved:** Mike Awesome, ECW, WCW, WWF, Paul Heyman, Eric Bischoff, Vince McMahon
- **Type:** Contract dispute / legal battle
- **What happened:** Mike Awesome departed ECW for WCW on April 10, 2000 while still reigning as ECW World Heavyweight Champion. He had been owed approximately $50,000 in unpaid wages.
