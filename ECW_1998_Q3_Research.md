## 2. Major TV, PPV, Supercard, and Arena Events

### ECW Heat Wave 1998 — August 2, 1998

- **Date:** August 2, 1998
- **Venue:** Hara Arena
- **City:** Dayton, Ohio
- **Attendance:** 4,400
- **Main matches:**
  - **ECW World Tag Team Championship:** Rob Van Dam & Sabu (c) (with Bill Alfonso) defeated Hayabusa & Jinsei Shinzaki to retain the titles (20:51)
  - **FTW Heavyweight Championship Falls Count Anywhere Match:** Taz (c) defeated Bam Bam Bigelow to retain the title (13:21)
  - Street Fight: The Sandman, Tommy Dreamer & Spike Dudley defeated The Dudleys (Buh Buh Ray, D-Von & Big Dick Dudley) (14:26)
  - Justin Credible (with Jason, Nicole Bass, and Chastity) defeated Jerry Lynn (14:36)
  - Chris Candido (with Tammy Lynn Sytch) defeated Lance Storm (11:00)
  - Masato Tanaka defeated Mike Awesome (11:49)
- **Major finishes:** 
  - Rob Van Dam & Sabu retained tag titles vs. Hayabusa & Jinsei Shinzaki
  - Taz retained FTW title vs. Bam Bam Bigelow in Falls Count Anywhere match
  - The Sandman, Tommy Dreamer & Spike Dudley defeated The Dudleys in Street Fight
- **Important debuts/returns/exits:** 
  - **Masato Tanaka ECW appearance** — Tanaka made a notable ECW appearance by defeating Mike Awesome
- **Why it mattered:** This was the first edition of Heat Wave to be broadcast on pay-per-view. The event featured Rob Van Dam & Sabu's successful title defense against the Japanese team of Hayabusa & Jinsei Shinzaki. Taz successfully defended the FTW Championship against Bam Bam Bigelow in a Falls Count Anywhere match. The event showcased ECW's top talent and continued the ongoing storylines of mid-1998 ECW.
