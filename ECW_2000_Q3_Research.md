## 2. Major TV, PPV, Supercard, and Arena Events

### ECW Heat Wave 2000 — July 16, 2000

- **Date:** July 16, 2000
- **Venue:** Grand Olympic Auditorium
- **City:** Los Angeles, California
- **Attendance:** 5,700
- **Network:** PPV
- **Main matches:**
  - Sal E. Graziano (w/ Tony Mamaluke) defeated Balls Mahoney (2:30)
  - Kid Kash, Danny Doring & Roadkill defeated Simon Diamond, CW Anderson & Johnny Swinger (11:01)
  - Jerry Lynn defeated Steve Corino (w/ Jack Victory) (15:23)
  - Chris Chetti & Nova defeated Da Baldies (Angel & Tony DeVito) (5:00)
  - Four-Way Dance: Yoshihiro Tajiri defeated Mikey Whipwreck, Little Guido & Psicosis (9:12)
  - ECW World Television Championship: Rhino (c) defeated The Sandman to retain the title (8:38)
  - Rob Van Dam (w/ Bill Alfonso) defeated Scotty Anton (19:02) — RVD debuted the "Van Terminator"
  - ECW World Heavyweight Championship (Stairway to Hell): Justin Credible (c) (w/ Francine) defeated Tommy Dreamer (w/ Jazz & George) to retain the title (12:20)
- **Major finishes:**
  - Justin Credible retained the World title against Tommy Dreamer in a Stairway to Hell match featuring barbed wire. Dreamer wrapped barbed wire around the top rope and crotched Credible on it, but Credible hit "That's Incredible" on the barbed wire for the win.
  - Rhino retained the TV title against The Sandman with a Gore through the guardrail. After the match, Rhino attempted another Gore on Sandman, but Sandman moved and Spike Dudley took the hit instead.
  - Rob Van Dam defeated Scotty Anton and debuted his new finisher, the "Van Terminator."
  - Jerry Lynn defeated Steve Corino in a highly-rated match (8.5/10 from Slam Wrestling).
- **Post-show incident:**
  - **XPW invasion attempt** — After the PPV ended, wrestlers from the rival California-based Xtreme Pro Wrestling (XPW) promotion attempted an invasion. XPW wrestlers (The Messiah, Kid Kaos, Supreme, Kristi Myst, Homeless Jimmy, and announcer Kris Kloss) sat in the front row wearing XPW logo shirts. They initiated a brawl that spilled into the crowd. ECW performers intervened and ejected the intruders. XPW was not acknowledged by ECW announcer Joey Styles during the broadcast.
- **Important debuts/returns/exits:**
  - **Rob Van Dam's "Van Terminator" debut** — RVD debuted his new finisher against Scotty Anton
  - **Jazz & George** accompanied Tommy Dreamer to the ring
- **Why it mattered:** Heat Wave 2000 was ECW's third PPV of 2000, drawing 5,700 fans in Los Angeles. The event was criticized for being anti-climactic, with the main event Stairway to Hell match not fully utilizing the barbed wire. The XPW invasion attempt was a notable post-show incident. Jerry Lynn vs. Steve Corino was praised as one of the best matches on the card. RVD's debut of the Van Terminator marked a new chapter in his feud with Scotty Anton.
