## 1. Title Changes

### ECW World Heavyweight Championship — The Sandman → Mikey Whipwreck

- **Date:** August 5, 1995
- **Taping date:** August 5, 1995
- **Air date:** Aired on Hardcore TV highlights
- **Championship:** ECW World Heavyweight Championship
- **Winner:** Mikey Whipwreck
- **Loser:** The Sandman (c)
- **Show:** ECW Wrestlepalooza 1995
- **Venue:** ECW Arena, Philadelphia, PA
- **Attendance:** 1,150
- **Finish:** Mikey Whipwreck defeated The Sandman in a "Loser Gets Caned" match (Singapore Cane Match) (5:31)
- **Event type:** Arena supercard
- **Why it mattered:** This was a major upset as Mikey Whipwreck, a young underdog, defeated The Sandman for the ECW World Heavyweight Championship. The stipulation was that if Sandman lost, he would receive 10 lashes with his Singapore cane. This marked the end of Sandman's second ECW World Heavyweight Championship reign (which began April 15, 1995) and the beginning of Whipwreck's first world title reign.

### ECW World Tag Team Championship — The Public Enemy → Raven & Stevie Richards

- **Date:** June 30, 1995 (technically end of Q2, but effects carried into Q3)
- **Taping date:** June 30, 1995
- **Air date:** July 4, 1995 (Hardcore TV)
- **Championship:** ECW World Tag Team Championship
- **Winners:** Raven & Stevie Richards
- **Losers:** The Public Enemy (c)
- **Show:** ECW Mountain Top Madness
- **Venue:** Jim Thorpe, PA – The Flagstaff
- **Attendance:** [UNCONFIRMED — FLAG]
- **Finish:** Raven & Stevie Richards defeated The Public Enemy to win the titles after The Gangstas interfered. However, a second referee informed the referee of the match about the Gangstas' interference, leading to Public Enemy being given the titles back on a reverse decision. Bill Alfonso then overturned the reversal, saying Raven & Richards were the new champions.
- **Event type:** Arena show
- **Why it mattered:** This was a controversial title change with multiple reversals. Raven & Stevie Richards became ECW World Tag Team Champions, marking the first time Raven's Nest held the tag team titles. The controversy involving Bill Alfonso's decision added to the heat of Raven's Nest as heel champions.

**Note:** Raven & Stevie Richards would hold the tag titles until September 16, 1995, when they lost them to The Pitbulls at Gangstas Paradise. They would regain them on October 7, 1995, only to lose them to The Public Enemy later that same evening.

This heel turn was motivated by Cactus Jack's frustration with hardcore wrestling and the physical sacrifices demanded by ECW fans. Chris Benoit's absence due to visa issues marked the beginning of his departure from ECW.

- **Person:** Chris Benoit
- **Change:** Departure from ECW
- **Previous company/status:** ECW
- **New company/status:** WCW (would debut in October 1995)
- **Contract / release / no-compete detail if known:** Benoit was forced to leave ECW after his work visa expired. Paul Heyman was supposed to renew it but failed to make it on time, so Benoit left ECW in August 1995 as a matter of job security and the ability to enter the United States.

- **Why it mattered:** Chris Benoit's departure from ECW marked the loss of one of ECW's top technical wrestlers. Benoit would go on to have a highly successful career in WCW and WWF/WWE, becoming a multiple-time world champion. His departure was part of a larger exodus of ECW talent to WCW in 1995.
