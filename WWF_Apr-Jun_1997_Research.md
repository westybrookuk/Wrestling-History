# WWF April-June 1997 Research Report

**Period Covered:** April 1, 1997 - June 30, 1997

## Executive Summary

The April-June 1997 period marked a crucial transition in WWF, featuring the continuation of the legendary Bret Hart vs. Steve Austin feud, the formation of the new Hart Foundation, and the rise of Hunter Hearst Helmsley (Triple H) as King of the Ring. The period featured two major PPV events: In Your House: Cold Day in Hell (May 11, Buffalo) and King of the Ring (June 8, Providence). The most significant developments were Steve Austin and Shawn Michaels winning the WWF Tag Team Championship (May 26), Hunter Hearst Helmsley winning King of the Ring, and the ongoing Hart Foundation vs. Austin/Michaels feud. The Undertaker remained WWF Champion throughout this period, while Owen Hart held the Intercontinental Championship and Davey Boy Smith held the European Championship. This period set the stage for the summer's major storylines and the continued rise of Steve Austin as the top babyface in WWF.

**Location:** Memorial Auditorium, Buffalo, New York

**Results:**

7. Shawn Michaels fought Stone Cold Steve Austin to a double DQ (22:29)
8. The Undertaker (c) (w/ Paul Bearer) defeated Faarooq (w/ Crush, Savio Vega, D'Lo Brown, Clarence Mason) - WWF Championship (13:43)

**Key Notes:**
- Hunter Hearst Helmsley won the 1997 King of the Ring tournament
- Shawn Michaels and Steve Austin had a blow-up match that ended in double DQ
- The Hart Foundation continued to dominate as a heel faction
- The Undertaker retained the WWF Championship against Faarooq
