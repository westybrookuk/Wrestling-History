# WWF July-September 2003 Research Report

**Period Covered:** July 1, 2003 - September 30, 2003

## Executive Summary

The July-September 2003 period was marked by significant championship changes and the continuation of major storylines from the summer. This quarter featured three major PPV events: **Vengeance** (July 27, Denver, CO), **SummerSlam** (August 24, Phoenix, AZ), and **Unforgiven** (September 21, Hershey, PA).

**Key developments included:**
- **Kurt Angle** won the WWE Championship from Brock Lesnar in a Triple Threat match at Vengeance
- **Eddie Guerrero** won the reactivated United States Championship tournament at Vengeance
- **Goldberg** finally captured the World Heavyweight Championship from Triple H at Unforgiven in a career-threatening match
- **Triple H** retained the World Heavyweight Championship in the second-ever Elimination Chamber match at SummerSlam
- **The Undertaker** defeated John Cena at Vengeance
- **Vince McMahon** defeated Zach Gowen at Vengeance

**PPV Statistics:**
- **Vengeance:** 9,500 attendance, 322,000 buys (0.69 buyrate)
- **SummerSlam:** 16,113 attendance, 415,000 buys
- **Unforgiven:** 10,347 attendance

## Injuries / Backstage Incidents / Controversies

### Triple H's Groin Injury
Triple H suffered a legitimate groin injury leading into SummerSlam 2003. Despite the injury, he competed in the Elimination Chamber match and retained the World Heavyweight Championship. The injury was worked into the storyline and affected his mobility during the match.

### Goldberg's Dominant Push
Goldberg's push continued throughout this period, culminating in his World Heavyweight Championship win at Unforgiven. The Title vs. Career stipulation added significant stakes to the match, as Goldberg would have been forced to retire if he had lost.

### Rhyno's Heel Turn
At Vengeance, Rhyno turned heel by interfering in the United States Championship tournament final, costing his friend Chris Benoit the match and allowing Eddie Guerrero to win the title. This was a surprising turn that established Rhyno as a heel on SmackDown!

### Brock Lesnar's Championship Loss and Regain
Brock Lesnar lost the WWE Championship to Kurt Angle at Vengeance in a Triple Threat match. According to some sources, Lesnar regained the championship from Big Show on the September 18, 2003 episode of SmackDown!, though this needs verification as other sources indicate Angle remained champion.

### Evolution's Continued Dominance
Despite losing the World Heavyweight Championship at Unforgiven, Evolution (Triple H, Ric Flair, Randy Orton) remained a dominant force on Raw throughout this period. Batista was still recovering from his triceps injury.

## Conclusion

The July-September 2003 period was defined by Goldberg's ultimate triumph, capturing the World Heavyweight Championship from Triple H at Unforgiven in a career-threatening match. This culminated a dominant five-month push that established Goldberg as one of WWE's top stars.

Vengeance featured two major championship changes: Kurt Angle winning the WWE Championship from Brock Lesnar, and Eddie Guerrero winning the reactivated United States Championship tournament. The quarter's centerpiece was SummerSlam's Elimination Chamber match, where Triple H retained the World Heavyweight Championship despite Goldberg eliminating three of the other five competitors.

The period also saw the continuation of major feuds on both brands, with Evolution dominating Raw until Goldberg's championship win, and SmackDown! featuring the Lesnar-Angle-Big Show triangle that resulted in Angle's WWE Championship victory.

By the end of September 2003, the championship landscape was: Goldberg held both the World Heavyweight Championship (Raw) and potentially the WWE Championship (SmackDown!) — though the latter needs verification. Kurt Angle was WWE Champion (if Lesnar didn't regain it), Eddie Guerrero was United States Champion, Christian was Intercontinental Champion, the Dudley Boyz were World Tag Team Champions, and Team Angle were WWE Tag Team Champions.

**Note:** There is a discrepancy in sources regarding whether Brock Lesnar regained the WWE Championship from Big Show on September 18, 2003. Some sources indicate he did, while others show Kurt Angle remaining champion through this period. This should be verified before finalizing the title lineage.
