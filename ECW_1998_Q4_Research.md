### FTW Heavyweight Championship — Taz → Sabu

- **Championship:** FTW Heavyweight Championship
- **Winner:** Sabu
- **Loser:** Taz (c)

## 2. Major TV, PPV, Supercard, and Arena Events

### ECW November to Remember 1998 — November 1, 1998

- **Date:** November 1, 1998
- **Venue:** Lakefront Arena
- **City:** New Orleans, Louisiana
- **Attendance:** 5,800
- **Main matches:**
  - Six-man tag team match: The New Triple Threat (Rob Van Dam, Sabu & Taz) (with Bill Alfonso) defeated The Triple Threat (Shane Douglas, Bam Bam Bigelow & Chris Candido) (with Francine) (12:57). Sabu pinned Douglas after hitting the Arabian Facebuster on both Taz and Douglas, as Taz had the Tazmission applied on the champion.
  - ECW World Tag Team Championship: Masato Tanaka & Balls Mahoney (with Axl Rotten) defeated The Dudley Boyz (Buh Buh Ray Dudley & D-Von Dudley) (c) (with Joel Gertner, Big Dick Dudley and Sign Guy Dudley) to win the titles (15:01)
  - Tommy Dreamer & Jake Roberts defeated Justin Credible & Jack Victory (with Jason, Lance Wright, and Nicole Bass) (12:26)
  - Lance Storm (with Tammy Lynn Sytch) defeated Jerry Lynn (16:48)
  - Spike Dudley defeated Mabel (0:05)
  - Tommy Rogers (with Chris Chetti) defeated Tracy Smothers (with Tommy Rich, Little Guido and Ulf Herman) (7:51)
  - The bWo (The Blue Meanie and Super Nova) defeated Danny Doring and Amish Roadkill (10:54)
- **Major finishes:** 
  - The New Triple Threat defeated The Triple Threat in six-man tag
  - Masato Tanaka & Balls Mahoney won tag titles from The Dudley Boyz
  - Tommy Dreamer & Jake Roberts defeated Justin Credible & Jack Victory
- **Important debuts/returns/exits:** 
  - **Bam Bam Bigelow's departure** — Bigelow left ECW for WCW after this event
- **Why it mattered:** This event featured The New Triple Threat's victory over The Triple Threat in a six-man tag team match. Masato Tanaka & Balls Mahoney won the ECW World Tag Team Championship from The Dudley Boyz. The event showcased ECW's top talent and continued the ongoing storylines of late 1998 ECW. Bam Bam Bigelow left ECW for WCW after this event.
