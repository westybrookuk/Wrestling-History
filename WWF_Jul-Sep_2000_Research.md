# WWF July-September 2000 Research Report

**Period Covered:** July 1, 2000 - September 30, 2000

## Executive Summary

The July-September 2000 period was a significant period in WWF history, featuring two major PPV events — SummerSlam (August 27, Raleigh) and Unforgiven (September 24, Philadelphia) — and marked by The Rock's continued WWF Championship reign, the Triple H vs. Kurt Angle feud, Eddie Guerrero winning the Intercontinental Championship, and The Hardy Boyz winning the WWF Tag Team Championship. Key developments included: The Rock retaining the WWF Championship at SummerSlam in a Triple Threat match against Triple H and Kurt Angle; The Rock retaining the WWF Championship at Unforgiven in a Fatal 4-Way match against Chris Benoit, The Undertaker, and Kane; Triple H defeating Kurt Angle in a No Disqualification match at Unforgiven with Mick Foley as special guest referee; Eddie Guerrero winning the Intercontinental Championship from Val Venis on Raw (September 4); The Hardy Boyz winning the WWF Tag Team Championship from Edge & Christian at Unforgiven in a Steel Cage match; and the continued feud between The Rock and Chris Benoit. SummerSlam drew approximately 18,000 attendance at Entertainment & Sports Arena in Raleigh. Unforgiven drew approximately 17,000 attendance at First Union Center in Philadelphia.

## Major PPV Events

### SummerSlam 2000 (August 27, 2000)

**Location:** Entertainment & Sports Arena, Raleigh, North Carolina
**Attendance:** Approximately 18,000
**PPV Buyrate:** Not specified in sources

**PPV Results:**
1. **Right to Censor (Steven Richards, Bull Buchanan & The Goodfather) defeated Too Cool (Scotty 2 Hotty, Grand Master Sexay & Rikishi)** — Six-Man Tag Team Match (5:12)
2. **X-Pac defeated Road Dogg** (4:42)
3. **Eddie Guerrero & Chyna defeated Val Venis (c) & Trish Stratus** — WWF Intercontinental Championship — Mixed Tag Team Match (7:13) [Chyna pinned Trish Stratus; Eddie Guerrero won IC title for Chyna] **Chyna WON IC title**
4. **Jerry "The King" Lawler defeated Tazz** (4:24)
5. **Steve Blackman defeated Shane McMahon (c)** — WWF Hardcore Championship (10:17) **Steve Blackman WON Hardcore title**
6. **Chris Benoit defeated Chris Jericho** — Two-out-of-Three Falls Match (13:02) [Benoit won 2-1]
7. **Edge & Christian (c) defeated The Hardy Boyz (Matt & Jeff Hardy) (with Lita) and The Dudley Boyz (Bubba Ray & D-Von)** — WWF Tag Team Championship — Tables, Ladders, and Chairs Match (15:28) **Edge & Christian retained**
8. **The Kat (with Al Snow) defeated Terri (with Perry Saturn)** — Stinkface Match (3:07)
9. **The Undertaker vs. Kane** — No Contest (7:33)
10. **The Rock (c) defeated Triple H and Kurt Angle** — WWF Championship — Triple Threat Match (20:11) **The Rock retained** [Triple H hit Pedigree on Angle at start; Angle injured (kayfabe concussion); Stephanie McMahon interfered; Triple H accidentally hit McMahon; Rock threw Angle over top rope, hit People's Elbow on Triple H]

**Key Notes:**
- The Rock retained WWF Championship in Triple Threat match
- Chyna won Intercontinental Championship (for Eddie Guerrero)
- Edge & Christian retained Tag Team titles in first TLC match
- Steve Blackman won Hardcore Championship
- Undertaker vs. Kane ended in No Contest
- Triple H and Kurt Angle continued feud
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SummerSlam_(2000)

### Unforgiven 2000 (September 24, 2000)

**Location:** First Union Center, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania
**Attendance:** Approximately 17,000
**PPV Buyrate:** Not specified in sources

**PPV Results:**
1. **Right to Censor (Steven Richards, Val Venis, Bull Buchanan & The Goodfather) defeated The APA (Bradshaw & Faarooq) and The Dudley Boyz (Bubba Ray & D-Von)** — Eight-Man Tag Team Match (6:05)
2. **Tazz defeated Jerry "The King" Lawler** — Strap Match (5:07)
3. **Steve Blackman (c) won** — WWF Hardcore Championship — 10-Minute Open Invitational Hardcore Battle Royal (10:00) [Blackman last defeated Perry Saturn (c); other participants: Crash Holly, Funaki, Test, Al Snow] **Steve Blackman retained**
4. **Chris Jericho defeated X-Pac** — Singles Match (9:05)
5. **The Hardy Boyz (Matt & Jeff Hardy) (with Lita) defeated Edge & Christian (c)** — WWF Tag Team Championship — Steel Cage Match (14:00) **Hardy Boyz WON Tag Team titles**
6. **Eddie Guerrero (c) (with Chyna) defeated Rikishi** — WWF Intercontinental Championship — by disqualification (6:10) **Eddie Guerrero retained**
7. **Triple H (with Stephanie McMahon-Helmsley) defeated Kurt Angle** — No Disqualification Match — Special Guest Referee: Mick Foley (17:28) [Stephanie lowblowed Angle; Triple H hit Pedigree] **Triple H won**
8. **The Rock (c) defeated Chris Benoit, The Undertaker, and Kane** — WWF Championship — Fatal 4-Way Match (16:03) **The Rock retained** [Rock hit Rock Bottom on Benoit]

**Key Notes:**
- The Rock retained WWF Championship in Fatal 4-Way match
- Hardy Boyz won Tag Team titles from Edge & Christian
- Triple H defeated Kurt Angle in No DQ match
- Eddie Guerrero retained Intercontinental Championship
- Steve Blackman retained Hardcore Championship

## Conclusion

The July-September 2000 period was a significant period in WWF history, featuring two major PPV events and the continuation of major storylines. The Rock retained the WWF Championship at SummerSlam in a Triple Threat match against Triple H and Kurt Angle. The Rock retained the WWF Championship at Unforgiven in a Fatal 4-Way match against Chris Benoit, The Undertaker, and Kane. Triple H defeated Kurt Angle in a No Disqualification match at Unforgiven with Mick Foley as special guest referee. Eddie Guerrero won the Intercontinental Championship from Val Venis on Raw (September 4). The Hardy Boyz won the WWF Tag Team Championship from Edge & Christian at Unforgiven in a Steel Cage match. SummerSlam drew approximately 18,000 attendance at Entertainment & Sports Arena in Raleigh. Unforgiven drew approximately 17,000 attendance at First Union Center in Philadelphia.
