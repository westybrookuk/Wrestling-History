# WWF April-June 2002 Research Report

**Period Covered:** April 1, 2002 - June 30, 2002

## Executive Summary

The April-June 2002 period marked the beginning of the Brand Extension era and the transition from WWF to WWE, featuring three major PPV events — Backlash (April 21, Kansas City), Judgment Day (May 19, Nashville), and King of the Ring (June 23, Columbus) — and marked by the Brand Extension announcement, the WWF name change to WWE, and multiple Undisputed WWF Championship changes. Key developments included: the Brand Extension being announced on April 1, 2002, splitting the roster into Raw and SmackDown brands; the WWF being renamed to World Wrestling Entertainment (WWE) on May 5, 2002; Hulk Hogan defeating Triple H to win the Undisputed WWF Championship at Backlash; The Undertaker defeating Hulk Hogan to win the Undisputed WWF Championship at Judgment Day; The Undertaker retaining the Undisputed WWF Championship against Triple H at King of the Ring; and Brock Lesnar winning the 2002 King of the Ring tournament. Backlash drew 15,000+ attendance at the Kemper Arena in Kansas City. Judgment Day drew 15,000+ attendance at the Gaylord Entertainment Center in Nashville. King of the Ring drew 17,000+ attendance at the Nationwide Arena in Columbus.

## Brand Extension Context

**Brand Status:** Brand Extension implemented
- **Brand Extension Announced:** April 1, 2002
- **Brand Split:** WWF roster split into Raw and SmackDown brands
- **Authority Figures:**
  - **Raw:** Ric Flair (General Manager)
  - **SmackDown:** Vince McMahon (General Manager)
- **Title Exclusivity:**
  - **Undisputed WWF Championship:** Shared (became exclusive to SmackDown on August 26, 2002)
  - **WWF Intercontinental Championship:** Raw exclusive
  - **WWF European Championship:** Raw exclusive
  - **WWF Tag Team Championship:** SmackDown exclusive
  - **WWF Women's Championship:** Raw exclusive
  - **WWF Hardcore Championship:** SmackDown exclusive

**WWF Name Change:**
- **Date:** May 5, 2002
- **Change:** WWF (World Wrestling Federation) renamed to WWE (World Wrestling Entertainment)
- **Reason:** Legal dispute with World Wildlife Fund over "WWF" initialism

## Conclusion

The April-June 2002 period marked the beginning of the Brand Extension era and the transition from WWF to WWE, featuring three major PPV events and major storylines. The Brand Extension was announced on April 1, 2002, splitting the WWF roster into Raw and SmackDown brands. The WWF was renamed to World Wrestling Entertainment (WWE) on May 5, 2002. Hulk Hogan defeated Triple H to win the Undisputed WWF Championship at Backlash. The Undertaker defeated Hollywood Hulk Hogan to win the Undisputed WWF Championship at Judgment Day. The Undertaker retained the Undisputed WWF Championship against Triple H at King of the Ring. Brock Lesnar won the 2002 King of the Ring tournament. Eddie Guerrero won the WWF Intercontinental Championship from Rob Van Dam at Backlash. Rikishi & Rico won the WWF Tag Team Championship from Billy & Chuck at Judgment Day. Jamie Noble won the WWF Cruiserweight Championship from The Hurricane at King of the Ring. Molly Holly won the WWF Women's Championship from Jazz at King of the Ring. Vince McMahon became sole owner of WWE on June 10, 2002. Backlash drew 15,000+ attendance at the Kemper Arena in Kansas City. Judgment Day drew 15,000+ attendance at the Gaylord Entertainment Center in Nashville. King of the Ring drew 17,000+ attendance at the Nationwide Arena in Columbus.
