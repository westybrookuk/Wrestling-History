# WWF July-September 1997 Research Report

**Period Covered:** July 1, 1997 - September 30, 1997

## Executive Summary

The July-September 1997 period was one of the most significant in WWF history, marked by Bret Hart's fifth WWF Championship win at SummerSlam, the continued rise of "Stone Cold" Steve Austin, and the beginning of the end for the Hart Foundation. The period featured three major PPV events: SummerSlam (August 3, East Rutherford), In Your House: Ground Zero (September 7, Louisville), and One Night Only (September 20, Birmingham, England). The most significant developments were Bret Hart defeating The Undertaker to win the WWF Championship at SummerSlam (with Shawn Michaels as special guest referee), Steve Austin winning the Intercontinental Championship from Owen Hart at SummerSlam, and the continued dominance of the Hart Foundation. This period also saw the continued rise of Steve Austin as the top babyface in WWF, setting the stage for the Attitude Era. The period ended with the tag team titles being held by Steve Austin & Dude Love, and Shawn Michaels winning the European Championship from Davey Boy Smith at One Night Only in England.

### WWF SummerSlam 1997 (August 3, 1997)

**Location:** Continental Airlines Arena, East Rutherford, New Jersey
**Attendance:** 20,516

**Results:**
1. Mankind defeated Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Chyna) - Steel Cage Match (16:13)

3. The Legion of Doom (Hawk & Animal) defeated The Godwinns (Henry & Phineas) (9:15)
4. The British Bulldog (c) defeated Ken Shamrock by DQ - WWF European Championship (7:29)
5. Los Boricuas (Savio Vega, Miguel Pérez Jr., Jose Estrada Jr., Jesus Castillo) defeated The Disciples of Apocalypse (Crush, Chainz, 8-Ball, Skull) - 8-Man Tag (9:08)
6. **"Stone Cold" Steve Austin defeated Owen Hart (c) - WWF Intercontinental Championship (16:16)** [Austin won with a roll-up; stipulation was Austin would have to kiss Hart's ass if he lost]
7. **Bret Hart defeated The Undertaker (c) - WWF Championship (28:09)** [Shawn Michaels was special guest referee; Hart won by pinfall after Michaels accidentally hit Undertaker with a steel chair after Hart spit in Michaels' face]

## Title Changes

### WWF World Heavyweight Championship

**August 3, 1997 - The Undertaker to Bret Hart**
- **Event:** WWF SummerSlam 1997 (East Rutherford, NJ)
- **Match Type:** Singles Match (Shawn Michaels special guest referee)
- **Result:** Bret Hart defeated The Undertaker (c) (28:09)
- **Notes:** Hart won by pinfall after Michaels accidentally hit Undertaker with a steel chair after Hart spit in Michaels' face. This was Hart's fifth WWF Championship, tying Hulk Hogan's record. The controversial finish set the stage for the Montreal Screwjob at Survivor Series.
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SummerSlam_(1997)

### WWF Intercontinental Championship

**August 3, 1997 - Owen Hart to Steve Austin**
- **Event:** WWF SummerSlam 1997 (East Rutherford, NJ)
- **Match Type:** Singles Match
- **Result:** Steve Austin defeated Owen Hart (c) (16:16)
- **Notes:** Austin won with a roll-up. Stipulation was Austin would have to kiss Hart's ass if he lost. This was Austin's first Intercontinental Championship.
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SummerSlam_(1997)

**September 8, 1997 - Steve Austin to Vacated**
- **Event:** WWF Monday Night Raw (Cincinnati, OH)
- **Result:** Title vacated due to neck injury
- **Notes:** Austin was stripped of the title due to a neck injury he suffered in winning the title. A tournament was announced for the title, with finals at Badd Blood: In Your House on October 5.
- **Source:** https://pdrwrestling.net/2014/07/15/wwf-raw-is-war-09-08-97/

### WWF European Championship

**September 20, 1997 - Davey Boy Smith to Shawn Michaels**
