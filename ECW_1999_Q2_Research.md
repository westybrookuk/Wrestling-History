- **Date:** May 16, 1999

- **Buy rate:** 75,000

The buy rate of 75,000 was moderate for ECW. Taz's dominant defense against both Candido (1:10) and Buh Buh Ray Dudley (12:17) cemented his status as the top champion in the company.

### ECW Hostile City Showdown 1999 — June 26, 1999

- **Date:** June 26, 1999
- **Venue:** ECW Arena (Viking Hall)
- **City:** Philadelphia, Pennsylvania
- **Attendance:** 1,600
- **Main matches:**
  - Rhino (w/ Jack Victory & Steve Corino) defeated Christopher Daniels by pinfall

  - Rhino's ECW debut — Rhino (with Jack Victory and Steve Corino) pinned Christopher Daniels with a Rhino Bomb in his first ECW match

- **Why it mattered:** Hostile City Showdown was ECW's final major event before Heat Wave. It marked Rhino's ECW debut, establishing him as a major new threat. Sabu's return despite his ban was significant, as Jerry Lynn argued that the non-sanctioned match allowed Sabu to compete. The Impact Players' victory over Lynn and Sabu continued their dominant run as ECW's top heel faction.
