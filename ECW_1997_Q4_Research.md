### ECW World Heavyweight Championship — Shane Douglas → Bam Bam Bigelow → Shane Douglas

**Title Change #1: Shane Douglas → Bam Bam Bigelow**

- **Date:** October 16, 1997
- **Taping date:** October 16, 1997
- **Air date:** October 24, 1997 (Hardcore TV)
- **Championship:** ECW World Heavyweight Championship
- **Winner:** Bam Bam Bigelow (with Rick Rude)
- **Loser:** Shane Douglas (c)
- **Show:** ECW live event
- **Venue:** Lost Battalion Hall / Elk's Lodge, Queens, NY
- **Attendance:** 900
- **Finish:** Bam Bam Bigelow pinned Shane Douglas to win the ECW World Heavyweight Championship. Rick Rude had been stalking Douglas and Francine since early 1997 and warned Douglas that he had been training a wrestler to take the title from Douglas. On this episode, Rude revealed Douglas' own Triple Threat teammate Bam Bam Bigelow as his handpicked man, who turned on Triple Threat and proceeded to defeat Douglas for the World Heavyweight Championship.
- **Event type:** House show/live event (aired on Hardcore TV)
- **Why it mattered:** This marked Bam Bam Bigelow winning the ECW World Heavyweight Championship from Shane Douglas in a shocking betrayal that shattered the Triple Threat stable's unity. Bigelow was kicked out of the Triple Threat as a result of this betrayal.

**Title Change #2: Bam Bam Bigelow → Shane Douglas**

- **Date:** November 30, 1997
- **Taping date:** November 30, 1997
- **Air date:** Aired on PPV (November to Remember 1997)
- **Championship:** ECW World Heavyweight Championship
- **Winner:** Shane Douglas (with Francine)
- **Loser:** Bam Bam Bigelow (c)
- **Show:** ECW November to Remember 1997
- **Venue:** Monaca Civic Center, Monaca, PA
- **Attendance:** [UNCONFIRMED — FLAG]
- **Finish:** Shane Douglas defeated Bam Bam Bigelow (25:02) to win the ECW World Heavyweight Championship. Douglas immediately zeroed in on Bigelow's kidneys (which Douglas had injured in a previous attack). Douglas tried everything he could to get the big man off his feet. Bigelow managed to powerbomb Douglas in the middle of the ring, but only got a two-count. Douglas covered him for the win and won the ECW Championship.

- **Why it mattered:** This marked Shane Douglas regaining the ECW World Heavyweight Championship from Bam Bam Bigelow at November to Remember. This win gave Shane Douglas the second-longest world title reign in ECW history (405 days total across his reigns). Both men were so badly injured as a result of the match that they didn't appear on television for over a month.
