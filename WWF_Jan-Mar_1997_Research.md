# WWF January-March 1997 Research Report

**Period Covered:** January 1, 1997 - March 31, 1997

## Executive Summary

The January-March 1997 period marked the beginning of the Attitude Era in WWF, featuring the controversial Royal Rumble victory by "Stone Cold" Steve Austin, Shawn Michaels' second WWF Championship reign and subsequent vacating due to injury, and Bret Hart's fourth WWF Championship win at In Your House 13: Final Four. The period featured two major PPV events: Royal Rumble 1997 (January 19, San Antonio) and In Your House 13: Final Four (February 16, Chattanooga). The most significant developments were Steve Austin's controversial Royal Rumble victory (where he was eliminated by Bret Hart but sneaked back in to win), Shawn Michaels winning the WWF Championship from Sycho Sid at Royal Rumble and then vacating it due to a knee injury, Rocky Maivia (The Rock) winning the Intercontinental Championship from Hunter Hearst Helmsley, and Bret Hart winning the vacant WWF Championship at Final Four. This period also saw the beginning of Bret Hart's heel turn in the United States and the rise of Steve Austin as the top babyface of the Attitude Era.

## Title Changes

### WWF World Heavyweight Championship

**January 19, 1997 - Sycho Sid to Shawn Michaels**
- **Event:** WWF Royal Rumble 1997 (Alamodome, San Antonio, TX)
- **Match Type:** Singles Match
- **Result:** Shawn Michaels (w/ Jose Lothario) defeated Sycho Sid (c) (13:49)
- **Notes:** Shawn Michaels won his second WWF Championship. This was a major title change, as Michaels had been building toward this moment.
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Rumble_(1997)

**February 13, 1997 - Shawn Michaels to Vacated**
- **Event:** WWF Monday Night Raw (Thursday Raw Thursday special)
- **Result:** Shawn Michaels vacated the title due to a knee injury
- **Notes:** Michaels claimed he had "lost his smile" and needed to find it again. This was actually due to a legitimate knee injury. The title was vacated and a Four Corners elimination match was set up for In Your House 13: Final Four.
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1997_in_professional_wrestling

**February 16, 1997 - Vacated to Bret Hart**
- **Event:** WWF In Your House 13: Final Four (UTC Arena, Chattanooga, TN)
- **Match Type:** Four Corners Elimination Match (Bret Hart vs. Steve Austin vs. Undertaker vs. Vader)
- **Result:** Bret Hart defeated Steve Austin, Undertaker, and Vader to win the vacant championship (24:05)
- **Notes:** Hart eliminated Austin by throwing him over the top rope, then eliminated Undertaker, winning his fourth WWF Championship.
- **Source:** https://www.thesmackdownhotel.com/events-results/ppv-special/wwf-in-your-house-13-final-four

### WWF Intercontinental Championship

**February 16, 1997 - Hunter Hearst Helmsley to Rocky Maivia**
- **Event:** WWF In Your House 13: Final Four (UTC Arena, Chattanooga, TN)

## Conclusion

The January-March 1997 period marked the beginning of the Attitude Era in WWF, featuring the controversial Royal Rumble victory by "Stone Cold" Steve Austin, Shawn Michaels' second WWF Championship reign and subsequent vacating due to injury, and Bret Hart's fourth WWF Championship win at In Your House 13: Final Four. The period featured two major PPV events, with the most significant being Royal Rumble 1997, where Steve Austin won controversially and Shawn Michaels won the WWF Championship from Sycho Sid. Rocky Maivia (The Rock) won his first Intercontinental Championship at In Your House 13. This period also saw the beginning of Bret Hart's heel turn in the United States and the rise of Steve Austin as the top babyface of the Attitude Era. The Attitude Era would go on to define WWF programming for the next several years, helping the company compete with WCW in the Monday Night Wars.
