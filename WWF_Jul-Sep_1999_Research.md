# WWF July-September 1999 Research Report

**Period Covered:** July 1, 1999 - September 30, 1999

## Executive Summary

The July-September 1999 period was one of the most chaotic and significant in WWF history, featuring three major PPV events — Fully Loaded (July 25, Buffalo), SummerSlam (August 22, Minneapolis), and Unforgiven (September 26, Charlotte) — and marked by massive title changes including Triple H winning his first WWF Championship and the formation of The Rock 'n' Sock Connection. Key developments included: Steve Austin retaining the WWF Championship from The Undertaker in a First Blood match at Fully Loaded (July 25) with stipulations that Vince McMahon could never appear on WWF television if Austin won; Mankind winning the WWF Championship in a Triple Threat match against Austin and Triple H at SummerSlam (August 22) with Jesse Ventura as special guest referee; Triple H winning his first WWF Championship from Mankind the next night on Raw (August 23); Jeff Jarrett winning the Intercontinental Championship at Fully Loaded; the tag team title situation becoming extremely chaotic with multiple changes; and the WWF Championship being vacated on September 20, with Triple H winning it back in a Six-Pack Challenge at Unforgiven (September 26) with Stone Cold Steve Austin as special enforcer. The period also saw the formation of The Rock 'n' Sock Connection (Rock & Mankind), the return of The British Bulldog, and Triple H's cementing as a main event heel. Fully Loaded drew 16,605 attendance in Buffalo with 334,000 PPV buys. SummerSlam drew 17,370 attendance in Minneapolis with 1.61 buyrate (approximately 600,000 buys). Unforgiven drew 15,779 attendance in Charlotte with 0.86 buyrate (approximately 315,000 buys).

## Major PPV Events

### Fully Loaded (July 25, 1999)

**Location:** Marine Midland Arena, Buffalo, New York
**Attendance:** 16,605
**PPV Buyrate:** 334,000

**Sunday Night Heat (Pre-Show):**
- Val Venis defeated Joey Abs (with Rodney and Pete Gas) (3:16)
- The Godfather defeated Meat (with Jacqueline and Terri Runnels) (2:06)
- Christian defeated Viscera (2:43)

**PPV Results:**
1. **Jeff Jarrett (with Debra) defeated Edge (c)** — WWF Intercontinental Championship (13:23) **Jeff Jarrett WON IC title**
2. **The Acolytes (Bradshaw & Faarooq) defeated The Hardy Boyz (Matt & Jeff Hardy) (c) and Michael Hayes** — WWF Tag Team Championship — Acolyte Rules Match (9:35) **Acolytes WON Tag Team titles**
3. **D'Lo Brown defeated Mideon (c)** — WWF European Championship (7:12) **D'Lo Brown WON European title**
4. **Big Boss Man defeated Al Snow (c)** — WWF Hardcore Championship (10:13) **Big Boss Man WON Hardcore title**
5. **Big Show defeated Kane** — Singles Match — Special Guest Referee: Hardcore Holly (8:13)
6. **Ken Shamrock defeated Steve Blackman** — Iron Circle Match (4:19)
7. **Road Dogg & X-Pac defeated Billy Gunn & Chyna** — Rights to D-Generation X Match (11:44) **Road Dogg & X-Pac WON rights to DX name**
8. **Triple H (with Billy Gunn & Chyna) defeated The Rock** — No Disqualification Falls Count Anywhere Strap Match to Determine #1 Contender WWF Championship (19:23) [Billy Gunn interfered with Billy Club; Rock hit People's Elbow; Triple H surprised Rock with Pedigree for win]
9. **"Stone Cold" Steve Austin (c) defeated The Undertaker (with Paul Bearer & Big Show)** — WWF Championship — First Blood Match (15:58) **Austin retained** [X-Pac interfered and kicked chair into Undertaker's face; Austin hit Undertaker with TV camera; Undertaker bled; Per stipulations, Vince McMahon could never appear on WWF television; Had Austin lost, he would never receive another WWF Championship match]

**Key Notes:**
- Austin vs. Undertaker match stipulated that if Undertaker won, Austin would never wrestle for WWF Championship again; if Austin won, Vince McMahon could never appear on WWF television
- X-Pac interfered in main event, kicked chair into Undertaker's face
- Austin hit Undertaker with TV camera to cause bleeding
- Triple H defeated Rock in strap match to become #1 contender
- DX split: Road Dogg & X-Pac won rights to DX name from Billy Gunn & Chyna
- Jeff Jarrett won IC title from Edge
- Acolytes won Tag Team titles from Hardy Boyz
- D'Lo Brown won European title from Mideon
- Big Boss Man won Hardcore title from Al Snow
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fully_Loaded_(1999)

### SummerSlam 1999 (August 22, 1999)

**Location:** Target Center, Minneapolis, Minnesota
**Attendance:** 17,370
**PPV Buyrate:** 1.61 (approximately 600,000 buys)
**Tagline:** "An Out Of Body Experience"

**Sunday Night Heat (Pre-Show):**
- Val Venis defeated Joey Abs (with Rodney and Pete Gas) (3:16)
- The Godfather defeated Meat (with Jacqueline and Terri Runnels) (2:06)
- Christian defeated Viscera (2:43)

**PPV Results:**
1. **Jeff Jarrett (with Debra) defeated D'Lo Brown (c)** — WWF Intercontinental Championship AND WWF European Championship (7:27) **Jeff Jarrett WON both IC and European titles**
2. **The Acolytes (Bradshaw & Faarooq) defeated Edge & Christian, The New Brood (Matt & Jeff Hardy) (with Gangrel), The Holly Cousins (Hardcore & Crash Holly), Droz & Prince Albert, and Mideon & Viscera** — Tag Team Turmoil Match to Determine #1 Contenders WWF Tag Team Championship (16:13) [Acolytes won by last eliminating Holly Cousins]
3. **Al Snow defeated Big Boss Man (c)** — WWF Hardcore Championship (7:27) **Al Snow WON Hardcore title**
4. **Ivory (c) defeated Tori** — WWF Women's Championship (4:08) **Ivory retained**
5. **Ken Shamrock defeated Steve Blackman** — Lion's Den Weapons Match (9:06)
6. **Test defeated Shane McMahon** — Greenwich Street Fight (12:04) [Since Shane lost, he was forced to give his blessing to Stephanie and Test]
7. **The Unholy Alliance (Big Show & The Undertaker) (with Paul Bearer) defeated Kane & X-Pac (c)** — WWF Tag Team Championship (12:01) **Undertaker & Big Show WON Tag Team titles**
8. **The Rock defeated Billy Gunn** — "Kiss My Ass" Match (10:12) [Rock shoved Gunn's face against fat woman's rear]
9. **Mankind defeated "Stone Cold" Steve Austin (c) and Triple H (with Chyna)** — WWF Championship — Triple Threat Match — Special Guest Referee: Jesse Ventura (16:23) **Mankind WON WWF Championship** [Mankind pinned Austin with double arm DDT after Austin sustained Triple H's Pedigree; Jesse Ventura raised Mankind's hand; This was Mankind's second WWF Championship reign]

**Key Notes:**
- Mankind won WWF Championship from Austin in Triple Threat match
- Jesse "The Body" Ventura (former Minnesota governor and WWF legend) was special guest referee
- Mankind pinned Austin, not Triple H
- Controversy over why Mankind won instead of Triple H (rumors: Austin's neck injury, Ventura wouldn't raise heel's hand in Minnesota)
- Triple H attacked Austin with chair shots to legs and knee after match, putting Austin on shelf
- Undertaker & Big Show won Tag Team titles from Kane & X-Pac
- Al Snow won Hardcore title from Big Boss Man
- Jeff Jarrett won both IC and European titles from D'Lo Brown
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SummerSlam_(1999)

### Unforgiven (September 26, 1999)

**Location:** Charlotte Coliseum, Charlotte, North Carolina
**Attendance:** 15,779
**PPV Buyrate:** 0.86 (approximately 315,000 buys)

**PPV Results:**
1. **Val Venis defeated Steve Blackman** — Singles Match [The Brooklyn Brawler as referee]
2. **D'Lo Brown defeated Mark Henry (c)** — WWF European Championship [Tom Prichard as referee] **D'Lo Brown WON European title**
3. **WWF IC Champion Jeff Jarrett (with Miss Kitty) defeated Chyna** by DQ — WWF Intercontinental Championship [Harvey Wippleman as referee; Chyna pinned Jarrett after Debra hit Jarrett with guitar; Tom Prichard reversed decision and DQ'd Chyna; The Fabulous Moolah & Mae Young interfered] **Chyna WON IC title (but D'Lo Brown later won it back)**
4. **The Acolytes (Bradshaw & Faarooq) defeated The Dudley Boyz (Bubba & D-Von)** — Tag Team Match
5. **Ivory defeated Luna Vachon** — WWF Women's Championship — Hardcore Match [Tori interfered] **Ivory retained**
6. **The New Age Outlaws (Road Dogg & Billy Gunn) (c) defeated Edge & Christian** — WWF Tag Team Championship
7. **Al Snow defeated Big Boss Man** — WWF Hardcore Championship — Kennel From Hell Match (Steel Cage covered with cell, "rabid dogs" circling ring) [Dogs did nothing but wag tails and poop at ringside] **Al Snow retained**
8. **X-Pac (sub. for Ken Shamrock) defeated Chris Jericho (with Curtis Hughes)** by DQ [Curtis Hughes interfered; Road Dogg made save]
9. **Triple H defeated The Rock, Mankind, Big Show, Kane, and The British Bulldog** — Vacant WWF Championship — Six-Pack Challenge — Special Guest Enforcer: Stone Cold Steve Austin (20:34/20:28) **Triple H WON vacant WWF Championship** [Austin made count on Rock; Big Show pulled Austin out; Bulldog hit Rock with chair; Austin gave Bulldog Stunner; Triple H pinned Rock with Pedigree; Austin gave Triple H Stunner after match]

**Key Notes:**
- WWF Championship was vacant (Mr. McMahon vacated it on September 20)
- Six-Pack Challenge with Steve Austin as special enforcer
- Triple H pinned Rock to win vacant WWF Championship
- British Bulldog returned after year-long absence (nearly died in late 1998)
- D'Lo Brown won European title from Mark Henry
- Chyna technically won IC title but was DQ'd; title situation unclear
- Referees were on strike; "scab" referees brought in
- Austin gave Triple H Stunner after match
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unforgiven_(1999)

## Game Effect Suggestions

### Event Timing Corrections
- **July 25:** Fully Loaded (Buffalo) — Austin retains WWF title; Jarrett wins IC; Acolytes win Tag; D'Lo wins European; Boss Man wins Hardcore; DX split
- **August 22:** SummerSlam (Minneapolis) — Mankind wins WWF title; Undertaker & Big Show win Tag; Al Snow wins Hardcore; Jarrett wins IC and European
- **August 23:** Triple H wins first WWF title (Raw); Jeff Jarrett awards Mark Henry European title; Lilian Garcia debuts
- **September 6:** Rock & Mankind win Tag titles (Raw)
- **September 16:** Undertaker & Big Show win Tag titles (SmackDown!)
- **September 20:** WWF Championship vacated (Raw)
- **September 26:** Unforgiven (Charlotte) — Triple H wins vacant WWF title in Six-Pack Challenge; D'Lo wins European; Chyna wins IC (DQ); Al Snow retains Hardcore

## Conclusion

The July-September 1999 period was one of the most chaotic and significant in WWF history, featuring three major PPV events and massive title changes. Steve Austin retained the WWF Championship from The Undertaker at Fully Loaded in a First Blood match with stipulations that Vince McMahon could never appear on WWF television. Mankind won the WWF Championship in a Triple Threat match against Austin and Triple H at SummerSlam with Jesse Ventura as special guest referee, but lost it to Triple H the next night on Raw in Triple H's first WWF Championship reign. The WWF Championship was vacated on September 20, with Triple H winning it back in a Six-Pack Challenge at Unforgiven with Stone Cold Steve Austin as special enforcer. The period also saw Jeff Jarrett winning the Intercontinental Championship, the tag team title situation becoming extremely chaotic with multiple changes, the formation of The Rock 'n' Sock Connection, the return of The British Bulldog, and Triple H's cementing as a main event heel. Fully Loaded drew 16,605 attendance in Buffalo with 334,000 PPV buys. SummerSlam drew 17,370 attendance in Minneapolis with 1.61 buyrate (approximately 600,000 buys). Unforgiven drew 15,779 attendance in Charlotte with 0.86 buyrate (approximately 315,000 buys).
