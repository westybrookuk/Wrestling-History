# WWF October-December 1998 Research Report

**Period Covered:** October 1, 1998 - December 31, 1998

## Executive Summary

The October-December 1998 period was one of the most consequential in WWF history, featuring four major PPV events — Survivor Series (November 15, St. Louis), Judgment Day: In Your House (October 18, Rosemont), Capital Carnage (December 6, London, UK-only), and Rock Bottom: In Your House (December 13, Vancouver) — and marked the culmination of the Attitude Era's first full year with some of the most significant developments in WWF history. Key developments included: the "Deadly Game" tournament at Survivor Series where The Rock won the vacant WWF Championship in controversial fashion (Vince McMahon forced referee to ring bell after applying Sharpshooter to Mankind), the formation of The Corporation as the top heel faction, Shawn Michaels' return as WWF Commissioner, Ken Shamrock winning the vacant IC Championship in a tournament, X-Pac winning the European Championship at Judgment Day, Christian winning the Light Heavyweight Championship, the introduction of the WWF Hardcore Championship, Mankind technically winning the WWF Championship at Rock Bottom only to have the decision reversed by Vince McMahon, the Austin vs. Undertaker feud culminating in a Buried Alive match, and Triple H's return from knee injury. Survivor Series drew 21,179 attendance with approximately 400,000-478,000 PPV buys, the most buys for any Survivor Series in WWF history at that time.

6. **New Age Outlaws (Billy Gunn & Road Dogg) (c) defeated The Corporation (Big Boss Man & Ken Shamrock) (with Shawn Michaels)** — WWF Tag Team Championship (16:10) **NAO retained**

**Key Notes:**
- Mankind technically won the WWF Championship but Vince McMahon immediately reversed the decision
- The Rock remained champion by technicality (hadn't submitted, just passed out)
- The Buried Alive match featured Austin driving a backhoe/bulldozer to bury Undertaker
- Kane interfered in the main event, chokeslamming Undertaker into the grave then turning on him
- The Rock-Mankind feud would continue into 1999, eventually leading to the Rock 'n' Sock Connection
- Debra's striptease was a major crowd moment

## Conclusion

The October-December 1998 period was one of the most consequential in WWF history, featuring the "Deadly Game" tournament at Survivor Series where The Rock won the vacant WWF Championship in controversial fashion, the formation of The Corporation as the top heel faction, Shawn Michaels' return as Commissioner, Ken Shamrock winning the vacant IC Championship, the introduction of the Hardcore Championship, Mankind technically winning the WWF Championship at Rock Bottom only to have the decision reversed by Vince McMahon, and Triple H's return from injury. Survivor Series drew approximately 400,000-478,000 PPV buys, the most for any Survivor Series in WWF history. The Rock's heel turn and Corporation alignment set the stage for 1999 storylines, while the Austin vs. Undertaker feud culminated in the Buried Alive match at Rock Bottom. The period ended with Shawn Michaels being fired as Commissioner and hitting Vince McMahon with a superkick, capping off a year that established WWF's clear dominance over WCW in the Monday Night Wars.
