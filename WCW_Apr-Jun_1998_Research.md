> **Note re: continuity with previous reports:** At the end of March 1998, the titleholders were:
> - WCW World Heavyweight: **Sting** (won vacant title at SuperBrawl VIII, 2/22/1998)
> - WCW US Heavyweight: **Diamond Dallas Page** (held since Starrcade, 12/28/1997)
> - WCW World Tag Team: **The Outsiders** (Scott Hall & Kevin Nash) (won at SuperBrawl VIII, 2/22/1998)
> - WCW World Television: **Booker T** (held since SuperBrawl VIII, 2/22/1998)
> - WCW Cruiserweight: **Chris Jericho** (held from Souled Out, 1/24/1998)
>
> **Goldberg** had won the US Title from Raven on Nitro (before Slamboree).
> **Scott Steiner** had turned on Rick and joined the nWo at SuperBrawl VIII.
> **The nWo** was unified but tensions were building between Hogan, Nash, and Savage.

## 1. Title Changes

### 1A. WCW World Heavyweight Championship — Sting → Randy Savage

- **Date:** April 19, 1998
- **Championship:** WCW World Heavyweight Championship
- **Winner:** Randy Savage (w/ Miss Elizabeth)
- **Loser:** Sting (c)
- **Show:** WCW/nWo Spring Stampede 1998 (PPV)
- **PPV date:** April 19, 1998
- **Venue:** Denver Coliseum, Denver, Colorado
- **Attendance:** 7,428
- **Details:**
  - Savage pinned Sting at approximately 10:08 (some sources say 10:37) in a No Disqualification match.
  - **Kevin Nash interfered** — powerbombed Sting, then dragged Savage on top of Sting for the pin.
  - This was Nash's revenge against Hogan, who had been interfering in matches. Nash cost Sting the title to spite Hogan.
  - Sting's reign lasted 56 days (Feb 22 – Apr 19).
  - Savage's third World Title reign began.
- **Why it mattered:**
  - The nWo was fracturing — Nash interfered against Hogan's wishes.
  - Hogan was furious that "his belt" was now on Savage.
  - This set up the nWo civil war that would officially split the faction.

### 2B. The nWo Officially Splits — April 27, 1998

- **Date:** April 27, 1998 (Nitro)
- **What happened:**
  - The nWo officially split into two factions:
    - **nWo Hollywood** (black and white) — led by Hollywood Hogan, included Scott Hall, Scott Steiner, The Giant, Bret Hart, Curt Hennig, Rick Rude, and others
    - **nWo Wolfpac** (black and red) — led by Kevin Nash, included Randy Savage, Sting, Lex Luger, Konnan, and others
  - Both factions were still technically part of the nWo but were now warring against each other.
  - This was the culmination of months of tensions between Hogan, Nash, and Savage.
- **Why it mattered:**
  - The nWo was no longer a unified force — it was now a civil war.
  - This was one of the most significant developments in WCW history.
  - Both factions claimed to be the "real" nWo.

- **Great American Bash (Jun 14):** Booker T wins TV Title. Jericho wins Cruiserweight. Goldberg retains US. Konnan betrayed by Hennig & Rude. Sting chooses Nash as tag partner.
