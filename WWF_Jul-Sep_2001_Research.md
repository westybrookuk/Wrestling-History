# WWF July-September 2001 Research Report

**Period Covered:** July 1, 2001 - September 30, 2001

## Executive Summary

The July-September 2001 period was dominated by The Invasion storyline, featuring three major PPV events — Invasion (July 22, Buffalo), SummerSlam (August 19, San Jose), and Unforgiven (September 23, Pittsburgh) — and marked by Steve Austin's heel turn and alignment with The Alliance (WCW/ECW), Kurt Angle winning the WWF Championship twice (at SummerSlam by DQ and at Unforgiven by submission), The Rock winning the WCW Championship at SummerSlam, and Chris Jericho winning the WCW Championship at Unforgiven. Key developments included: The Invasion storyline where WCW and ECW joined forces as The Alliance to battle WWF; Steve Austin turning heel and joining The Alliance at Invasion; Kurt Angle defeating Steve Austin by disqualification at SummerSlam to win the WWF Championship; The Rock defeating Booker T at SummerSlam to win the WCW Championship; Kurt Angle forcing Steve Austin to submit at Unforgiven to win the WWF Championship again; and Chris Jericho defeating The Rock at Unforgiven to win the WCW Championship. Invasion drew 21,000+ attendance at the HSBC Arena in Buffalo. SummerSlam drew 15,000+ attendance at the Compaq Center in San Jose. Unforgiven drew 16,000+ attendance at the Mellon Arena in Pittsburgh.

- **July 22:** Invasion (Buffalo) — The Alliance wins 10-man tag; Steve Austin turns heel; Rob Van Dam wins Hardcore title

## Conclusion

The July-September 2001 period was dominated by The Invasion storyline, featuring three major PPV events and major title changes and storylines. The Invasion storyline featured WCW and ECW joining forces as The Alliance to battle WWF. Steve Austin turned heel and joined The Alliance at Invasion. Kurt Angle won the WWF Championship from Steve Austin at SummerSlam by disqualification, then won it again at Unforgiven by submission. The Rock won the WCW Championship from Booker T at SummerSlam. The Brothers of Destruction won both WWF and WCW Tag Team Championships at SummerSlam. Edge won the WWF Intercontinental Championship from Lance Storm at SummerSlam, then lost it to Christian at Unforgiven. Rob Van Dam won the WWF Hardcore Championship from Jeff Hardy at Invasion and retained it at SummerSlam and Unforgiven. Rhyno won the WCW United States Championship from Tajiri at Unforgiven. X-Pac unified the WCW Cruiserweight Championship and WWF Light Heavyweight Championship at SummerSlam. Invasion drew 21,000+ attendance at the HSBC Arena in Buffalo. SummerSlam drew 15,000+ attendance at the Compaq Center in San Jose. Unforgiven drew 16,000+ attendance at the Mellon Arena in Pittsburgh.
