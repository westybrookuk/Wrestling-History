# WWF January-March 2000 Research Report

**Period Covered:** January 1, 2000 - March 31, 2000

## Executive Summary

The January-March 2000 period was one of the most significant in WWF history, featuring two major PPV events — Royal Rumble (January 23, New York) and No Way Out (February 27, Hartford) — and marked by the continuation of the McMahon-Helmsley Era, The Rock's Royal Rumble victory, Triple H's continued dominance as WWF Champion, the retirement of Cactus Jack (Mick Foley), the debut of The Radicalz (Chris Benoit, Eddie Guerrero, Dean Malenko, Perry Saturn) from WCW, and the buildup to WrestleMania 2000. Key developments included: The Rock winning the Royal Rumble match by last eliminating The Big Show to earn a WWF Championship match at WrestleMania 2000; Triple H retaining the WWF Championship throughout the period; Triple H defeating Cactus Jack in a Hell in a Cell Title vs. Career match at No Way Out, forcing Cactus Jack to retire from professional wrestling; Kurt Angle winning the Intercontinental Championship from Chris Jericho at No Way Out and becoming a double champion (also holding European Championship); The Big Show defeating The Rock at No Way Out to become #1 contender for WWF Championship at WrestleMania 2000; The Dudley Boyz defeating New Age Outlaws for WWF Tag Team Championship at No Way Out; and the buildup to the Fatal Four-Way Elimination match at WrestleMania 2000 involving Triple H, The Rock, The Big Show, and Mick Foley. Royal Rumble drew 18,000+ attendance at Madison Square Garden in New York. No Way Out drew 12,000+ attendance at Hartford Civic Center in Connecticut.

- 30 entrants; The Rock entered at #24
- Final four: The Rock, The Big Show, X-Pac, Kane
- Elimination order: X-Pac eliminated Kane; Big Show eliminated X-Pac; Rock eliminated Big Show

## Conclusion

The January-March 2000 period was one of the most significant in WWF history, featuring two major PPV events and the continuation of the McMahon-Helmsley Era. The Rock won the Royal Rumble match to earn a WWF Championship match at WrestleMania 2000. Triple H defeated Cactus Jack in a Hell in a Cell Title vs. Career match at No Way Out, forcing Cactus Jack to retire from professional wrestling. Kurt Angle won the Intercontinental Championship from Chris Jericho at No Way Out and became a double champion (also holding European Championship). The Big Show defeated The Rock at No Way Out to become #1 contender for WWF Championship at WrestleMania 2000. The Dudley Boyz defeated New Age Outlaws for WWF Tag Team Championship at No Way Out. The Radicalz (Chris Benoit, Eddie Guerrero, Dean Malenko, Perry Saturn) debuted in WWF after leaving WCW. The period built to the Fatal Four-Way Elimination match at WrestleMania 2000 involving Triple H, The Rock, The Big Show, and Mick Foley. Royal Rumble drew 18,000+ attendance at Madison Square Garden in New York. No Way Out drew 12,000+ attendance at Hartford Civic Center in Connecticut.
