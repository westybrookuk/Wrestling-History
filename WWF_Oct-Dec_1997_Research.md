# WWF October-December 1997 Research Report

**Period Covered:** October 1, 1997 - December 31, 1997

## Executive Summary

The October-December 1997 period was one of the most significant in WWF history, marked by the infamous Montreal Screwjob at Survivor Series, the formation of D-Generation X, and the continued rise of "Stone Cold" Steve Austin as the top babyface in WWF. The period featured two major PPV events: Survivor Series (November 9, Montreal) and In Your House: D-Generation X (December 7, Boston). The most significant development was the Montreal Screwjob, where Shawn Michaels won the WWF Championship from Bret Hart in controversial fashion, leading to Hart's departure to WCW. D-Generation X was officially formed on October 13, with Shawn Michaels, Triple H, Chyna, and Rick Rude as founding members. Steve Austin returned from injury and continued his feud with Owen Hart, eventually winning the Intercontinental Championship at Survivor Series. The Rock (formerly Rocky Maivia) won the IC Title from Austin on December 8. This period set the stage for the Attitude Era and the continued growth of WWF in the Monday Night Wars.

## Major PPV Events

### WWF Survivor Series 1997 (November 9, 1997)

**Location:** Molson Centre, Montreal, Quebec, Canada
**Attendance:** 20,000
**PPV Buyrate:** Not specified in sources

**Results:**
1. The New Age Outlaws (Road Dogg & Billy Gunn) and The Godwinns (Henry & Phineas) defeated The Headbangers (Mosh & Thrasher) and The New Blackjacks (Blackjack Windham & Blackjack Bradshaw) - 4-on-4 Survivor Series Elimination Match (15:25)
2. The Truth Commission (The Jackyl, The Interrogator, Sniper, Recon) defeated The Disciples of Apocalypse (Crush, Chainz, 8-Ball, Skull) - 4-on-4 Survivor Series Elimination Match (9:59)
3. Team Canada (British Bulldog, Jim Neidhart, Doug Furnas, Phil Lafon) defeated Team USA (Vader, Goldust, Marc Mero, Steve Blackman) (w/ Sable) - 4-on-4 Survivor Series Elimination Match (17:05)
4. Kane (w/ Paul Bearer) defeated Mankind (9:27)
5. Ken Shamrock, Ahmed Johnson, and Legion of Doom (Hawk & Animal) defeated Nation of Domination (Rocky Maivia, Faarooq, Kama, D'Lo Brown) - 4-on-4 Survivor Series Elimination Match (20:28)
6. **"Stone Cold" Steve Austin defeated Owen Hart (c) (w/ British Bulldog, Jim Neidhart, Doug Furnas, Phil Lafon) - WWF Intercontinental Championship (4:03)**
7. **Shawn Michaels defeated Bret Hart (c) - WWF Championship (19:58)** [MONTREAL SCREWJOB - Earl Hebner rang the bell while Michaels had Hart in the Sharpshooter, even though Hart had not submitted]

**The Montreal Screwjob:**
- The match was originally planned to end in disqualification, with Hart retaining the title
- Vince McMahon ordered referee Earl Hebner to end the match while Michaels had Hart in the Sharpshooter
- Hart had not submitted, but was declared the loser
- Hart spat in McMahon's face and smashed equipment in rage
- This was Hart's last WWF match before departing for WCW
- The incident became known as the Montreal Screwjob and is one of the most infamous incidents in wrestling history
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montreal_Screwjob
