# WWF October-December 1995 Research Report

**Period Covered:** October 1, 1995 - December 31, 1995

## Executive Summary

The October-December 1995 period marked the end of Diesel's WWF Championship reign and the beginning of Bret Hart's third WWF Title reign. The period was dominated by the aftermath of the Shawn Michaels Syracuse incident, where Michaels was legitimately attacked outside a bar in Syracuse, New York on October 13, 1995, by several Marines. This incident led to Michaels forfeiting the Intercontinental Championship to Dean Douglas at In Your House 4, who then lost it to Razor Ramon later that same night. The period featured three major PPV events: In Your House 4: Great White North (October 22, Winnipeg), Survivor Series (November 19, Landover), and In Your House 5: Seasons Beatings (December 17, Hershey). The main event of Survivor Series saw Bret Hart defeat Diesel to win his third WWF Championship in a No Disqualification match, ending Diesel's 358-day reign. The period also saw the debut of Goldust, who would become a controversial but popular character.

## Title Changes

### WWF World Heavyweight Championship

**November 19, 1995 - Diesel to Bret Hart**
- **Event:** WWF Survivor Series 1995 (Landover, MD)
- **Match Type:** No Disqualification Match
- **Result:** Bret Hart defeated Diesel (c) (24:54)
- **Notes:** Bret Hart won his third WWF Championship with a small package after playing possum. Diesel hit two jackknife powerbombs on Hart after the match in anger. This ended Diesel's 358-day reign, the longest of the 1990s.
- **Attendance:** 14,500
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Survivor_Series_(1995)

### WWF Intercontinental Championship

**October 22, 1995 - Shawn Michaels to Dean Douglas (forfeit)**
- **Event:** WWF In Your House 4: Great White North (Winnipeg, Manitoba, Canada)
- **Result:** Shawn Michaels forfeited the title to Dean Douglas due to injuries sustained in the Syracuse incident
- **Notes:** Michaels was legitimately attacked by Marines outside Club 37 in Syracuse on October 13, 1995. He suffered severe facial injuries including a laceration on his right eyelid that nearly tore off, two black eyes, and blood coming from his ears. WWF President Gorilla Monsoon forced Michaels to forfeit the title to his scheduled opponent Dean Douglas. Douglas's reign lasted only the length of the match.
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_Your_House_4

**October 22, 1995 - Dean Douglas to Razor Ramon**
- **Event:** WWF In Your House 4: Great White North (Winnipeg, Manitoba, Canada)
- **Match Type:** Singles Match
- **Result:** Razor Ramon defeated Dean Douglas (c) (11:01)
- **Notes:** Immediately after being awarded the title, Douglas was forced to defend against Razor Ramon. Ramon won with a back suplex, making Douglas the shortest-reigning Intercontinental Champion in history (reign lasted only the length of the match). This was Ramon's fourth Intercontinental Championship.

## Injuries / Backstage Incidents / Controversies

### The Syracuse Incident (October 13, 1995)

**Incident:** Shawn Michaels legitimately attacked by Marines outside bar

**Details:**
- Date: October 13, 1995, outside Club 37 in Syracuse, NY
- Michaels, Davey Boy Smith, and 1-2-3 Kid had made an unscheduled appearance at the club
- Michaels was reportedly intoxicated and had been flirting with a woman who was the ex-girlfriend of one of the Marines
- The bouncer asked his girlfriend Donna Jones to drive the wrestlers back to their hotel
- Michaels fell asleep in the passenger seat of Jones's Pontiac Sunbird
- A group of 9-10 men (reportedly Marines) attacked the car
- Michaels was pulled from the front seat and beaten while unconscious
- His head was slammed against the car and he was kicked in the face while face-down on the pavement
- Smith and Waltman were in the back seat of the two-door car and couldn't get out to help
- Jones eventually broke up the fight by running into the club and getting other bouncers

**Injuries:**
- Laceration on right eyelid (nearly torn off, required 20 stitches)
- Two black eyes
- Blood coming from ears
- Severe facial bruising and swelling

**Aftermath:**
- Michaels was taken to St. Joseph's Hospital
- He was stitched up and treated for facial injuries
- Michaels refused to press charges
- The incident led to Michaels forfeiting the IC title at In Your House 4
- The concussion angle on November 20 Raw was used to write Michaels off TV for knee surgery

**Controversy:**
- There are conflicting accounts of how many attackers were involved (ranging from 2 to 10)
- Some accounts say it was just one Marine (Douglas Griffith, 23 years old)
- Shane Douglas questioned the severity of the injuries, suggesting makeup was used to enhance the appearance
- Jim Ross stated backstage personnel were not impressed, saying "a pro wrestler losing to a civilian in a fistfight is unacceptable"
- Bruce Prichard noted the WWF decided to "show the injuries to his face and make a story out of it"

**Source:** https://www.oklafan.com/news/view/11661/; https://prowrestlingstories.com/pro-wrestling-stories/shawn-michaels-syracuse-incident/

### The Kliq and Kayfabe Breaches

**Incident:** Multiple kayfabe breaches involving The Kliq

**Details:**
- The Kliq (Shawn Michaels, Kevin Nash/Diesel, Scott Hall/Razor Ramon, Hunter Hearst Helmsley/Triple H, Sean Waltman/1-2-3 Kid) were a real-life group of friends who had significant influence in WWF
- The November 20, 1995 Raw featured a goodbye hug between Razor Ramon and Shawn Michaels, with Diesel and Hunter Hearst Helmsley joining
- This was a rare breach of kayfabe, as the Kliq members were portrayed as villains on TV but were shown showing affection to each other
- The Syracuse incident and subsequent IC title forfeit were also controversial, with questions about whether Michaels could have competed
- The Kliq's influence was seen in the title changes (Michaels forfeiting to Douglas, who lost to Ramon - all Kliq members)

**Source:** https://bradywwe.fandom.com/wiki/Shawn_Michaels

### Dean Douglas's Short Title Reign

**Incident:** Dean Douglas had shortest Intercontinental Championship reign in history

**Details:**
- Douglas was awarded the IC title when Shawn Michaels forfeited due to Syracuse incident injuries
- Immediately after being awarded the title, Douglas was forced to defend against Razor Ramon
- Ramon defeated Douglas in 11:01, making Douglas's reign last only the length of the match
- This was the shortest IC title reign in WWF history
- Douglas's real name was Shane Douglas (not Dean Douglas as portrayed on TV)
- Douglas was frustrated with WWF's handling of his character and left for ECW shortly after

**Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_Your_House_4

### Diesel's Decline as WWF Champion

**Incident:** Diesel's push as WWF Champion continued to decline

**Details:**
- Diesel main evented 9 straight PPVs, something Hulk Hogan never did
- His matches were consistently criticized as poor
- Vince McMahon was heard saying "horrible" after Diesel's match at In Your House 4
- Diesel's character was described as a "gay-byface champion" and a "milk-toasty sloth"
- After losing the title to Bret Hart at Survivor Series, Diesel's character gained more edge and attitude
- He became a "tweener" saying he would accept support from fans who liked him but didn't care about those who didn't
- Diesel broke ties with Shawn Michaels and remained a heel for the remainder of his WWF run

**Source:** https://wrestlingrecaps.com/2010/08/21/wwf-in-your-house-4-10221995/; https://wrestlingrecaps.com/2010/08/21/wwf-survivor-series-1995-11191995/

## Conclusion

The October-December 1995 period marked the end of Diesel's WWF Championship reign and the beginning of Bret Hart's third WWF Title reign. The period was dominated by the aftermath of the Shawn Michaels Syracuse incident, where Michaels was legitimately attacked by Marines on October 13, leading to his IC title forfeit and the controversial booking of Dean Douglas having the shortest title reign in history. The period featured three PPV events: In Your House 4 (October 22), Survivor Series (November 19), and In Your House 5 (December 17). Survivor Series saw Bret Hart defeat Diesel to win his third WWF Championship, ending Diesel's 358-day reign. The period also saw the debut of Goldust and the continued influence of The Kliq, including rare kayfabe breaches. Razor Ramon left for WCW during this period, marking another significant departure from WWF.
