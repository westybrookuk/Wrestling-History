## 1. Title Changes

### ECW World Heavyweight Championship — Terry Funk → Sabu → Shane Douglas

**Title Change #1: Terry Funk → Sabu**

- **Date:** August 9, 1997
- **Taping date:** August 9, 1997
- **Air date:** Aired on VHS/PPV (Born to be Wired)
- **Championship:** ECW World Heavyweight Championship
- **Winner:** Sabu (with Bill Alfonso)
- **Loser:** Terry Funk (c) (with Dory Funk Jr.)
- **Show:** ECW Born to be Wired
- **Venue:** ECW Arena, Philadelphia, PA
- **Attendance:** 1,400
- **Finish:** Sabu defeated Terry Funk in a barbed wire match (20:38) to win the ECW World Heavyweight Championship. Towards the end of the match, Rob Van Dam interfered on Sabu's behalf until being driven away by Tommy Dreamer. The match ended with both men badly tangled in barbed wire, after which Sabu wrapped barbed wire around his leg and then delivered a leg drop to Funk before pinning him to win the title. Following the match, the two men were so entangled in wire that Sabu's manager Bill Alfonso and the ring crew had to use wire cutters to free them. Sabu's arm required over 100 stitches to close the wound.
- **Event type:** VHS/PPV
- **Why it mattered:** This marked Sabu winning the ECW World Heavyweight Championship from Terry Funk in one of the most brutal matches in ECW history. Paul Heyman stated that the level of violence bothered him so much, he would never again book such a match—and he never did. This was the last-ever barbed-wire match in ECW history.

**Title Change #2: Sabu → Shane Douglas**

- **Date:** August 17, 1997
- **Taping date:** August 17, 1997
- **Air date:** Aired on PPV (Hardcore Heaven 1997)
- **Championship:** ECW World Heavyweight Championship
- **Winner:** Shane Douglas (with Francine)

- **Show:** ECW Hardcore Heaven 1997
- **Venue:** War Memorial Auditorium, Fort Lauderdale, FL

The Sandman was taken out by Sabu and Rob Van Dam earlier in the show but came back to the arena in an ambulance and interfered in the main event by shoving Sabu from the top rope. Francine aided Douglas in attacking Funk until Dory Funk, Jr came to the ring and attacked Douglas and then chased Francine to the backstage. The action continued between Funk and Douglas as Douglas hit a belly-to-belly suplex to win the title.
- **Event type:** PPV (ECW's second ever pay-per-view)
- **Why it mattered:** This marked Shane Douglas winning the ECW World Heavyweight Championship from Sabu in a three-way dance. This was ECW's second ever pay-per-view event (Hardcore Heaven 1997). The match featured The Sandman's dramatic return from injury in an ambulance to interfere in the main event.

### ECW Hardcore Heaven 1997 — August 17, 1997

- **Date:** August 17, 1997
- **Venue:** War Memorial Auditorium
- **City:** Fort Lauderdale, FL
- **Attendance:** 1,800
- **PPV Buy Rate:** 72,000
- **Main matches:**
  - **ECW World Heavyweight Championship Three-Way Dance:** Shane Douglas (with Francine) defeated Sabu (c) (with Bill Alfonso) and Terry Funk (with Dory Funk Jr.) to win the title (26:37). The Sandman was taken out by Sabu and Rob Van Dam earlier in the show but came back to the arena in an ambulance and interfered in the main event by shoving Sabu from the top rope. Sandman delivered a Rolling Rock to Sabu onto a ladder, allowing Funk and Douglas to simultaneously pin him to eliminate him. Francine aided Douglas in attacking Funk until Dory Funk, Jr came to the ring and attacked Douglas and then chased Francine to the backstage. The action continued between Funk and Douglas as Douglas hit a belly-to-belly suplex to win the title.
  - **ECW World Television Championship:** Taz (c) defeated Chris Candido to retain the title (10:52)
  - **ECW World Tag Team Championship:** The Dudley Boyz (Buh Buh Ray Dudley & D-Von Dudley) (c) (with Jenna Jameson, Joel Gertner, Sign Guy Dudley & Big Dick Dudley) defeated PG-13 (J.C. Ice & Wolfie D) to retain the titles (10:58)
  - Bam Bam Bigelow defeated Spike Dudley (5:05)
  - Rob Van Dam (with Bill Alfonso) defeated Al Snow (13:43)
  - Tommy Dreamer (with Beulah McGillicutty) defeated Jerry Lawler (18:57) — interpromotional match between ECW and WWF
- **Major finishes:** 
  - Shane Douglas won ECW World Heavyweight Championship from Sabu and Terry Funk in three-way dance
  - Taz retained TV title vs. Chris Candido
  - The Dudley Boyz retained tag titles vs. PG-13
  - Tommy Dreamer defeated Jerry Lawler (interpromotional match)
- **Important debuts/returns/exits:** 
  - **The Sandman's dramatic return** — Sandman returned from injury in an ambulance to interfere in the main event
  - **Jerry Lawler ECW appearance** — Lawler made a notable appearance in ECW and lost to Tommy Dreamer in an interpromotional match
  - **Dory Funk Jr. ECW appearance** — Dory Funk Jr. accompanied Terry Funk to the ring and attacked Shane Douglas
  - **Jenna Jameson ECW appearance** — Adult film star Jenna Jameson accompanied The Dudley Boyz to the ring
- **Why it mattered:** This was ECW's second ever pay-per-view event (Hardcore Heaven 1997). Shane Douglas won the ECW World Heavyweight Championship from Sabu in a three-way dance. The Sandman's dramatic return from injury in an ambulance to interfere in the main event was a notable moment. Tommy Dreamer's victory over Jerry Lawler was an interpromotional match between ECW and WWF. The event showcased ECW's top talent and continued the ongoing storylines of mid-1997 ECW.
