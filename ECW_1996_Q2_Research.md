### ECW Hardcore Heaven 1996 — June 22, 1996

- **Date:** June 22, 1996
- **Venue:** ECW Arena
- **City:** Philadelphia, PA
- **Attendance:** 1,250
- **Main matches:**
  - **ECW World Heavyweight Championship:** Raven (c) (with Stevie Richards, Super Nova, and The Blue Meanie) defeated Terry Gordy to retain the title (12:45). Stevie Richards hit a "Stevie Kick" to Gordy, allowing Raven to execute a DDT to retain the title. After the match, Raven's Nest attacked Dreamer and Gordy until Sandman made the save, then Peaches came to the ring and brought out her son Tyler along with her, who claimed that he worshipped Raven. Raven took them backstage and Sandman followed.
  - **ECW World Television Championship:** Chris Jericho defeated Pitbull #2 (c) (with Francine) to win the ECW World Television Championship (12:09/12:10)
  - **ECW World Tag Team Championship:** The Eliminators (Kronus and Saturn) (c) vs. The Gangstas (New Jack and Mustafa Saed) ended in a no-contest (8:16)
  - **ECW World Tag Team Championship:** The Eliminators (Kronus and Saturn) (c) vs. The Bruise Brothers (Don Bruise and Ron Bruise) ended in a no-contest
  - Tommy Dreamer (with Beulah McGillicutty and Kimona Wanalaya) defeated Brian Lee in a weapons match (9:02). Beulah McGillicutty prevented Lee from hitting Dreamer with a cinder block and Lee tried to hit a "Primetime Slam" to Beulah until Kimona Wanalaya distracted him enough for Beulah to hit him with a frying pan and Dreamer hit him with a stop sign in the head and executed a DDT on the sign to win the match.
  - Sabu defeated Rob Van Dam (20:00)
  - Taz (with Bill Alfonso, The Eliminators, Rob Van Dam, and Shane Douglas) defeated Paul Varelans by submission (2:24)
  - Shane Douglas defeated Mikey Whipwreck (11:33)
  - The Full Blooded Italians (J.T. Smith and Little Guido) (with Salvatore Bellomo) defeated The Dudleys (Big Dick Dudley and Buh Buh Ray Dudley) (with Chubby Dudley, Sign Guy Dudley, and Dances with Dudley) by disqualification (10:10)
  - Axl Rotten and Hack Meyers vs. The Samoan Gangsta Party (Matty Smalls and Sammy the Silk) ended in a no-contest (2:00)
- **Major finishes:** 
  - Raven retained world title vs. Gordy
  - Chris Jericho won TV title from Pitbull #2
  - The Eliminators retained tag titles (no-contests)
  - Dreamer defeated Brian Lee in weapons match
  - Sabu defeated RVD
- **Important debuts/returns/exits:** 
  - **Chris Jericho's first ECW championship** — Jericho won the ECW Television Championship, marking his first championship win in the United States
- **Why it mattered:** This was a major ECW supercard featuring Chris Jericho's first ECW championship win, Raven's successful title defense, and the continuation of the Sabu vs. Rob Van Dam feud. The event showcased ECW's top talent and continued the ongoing storylines of mid-1996 ECW.
