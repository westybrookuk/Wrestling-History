## Executive Summary

The January-March 2005 period was defined by the continuation of Evolution's dominance, Batista's rise to main event status, and the buildup to WrestleMania 21. This quarter featured three major PPV events: **New Year's Revolution** (January 9, San Juan, Puerto Rico), **Royal Rumble** (January 30, Fresno, California), and **No Way Out** (February 20, Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania), culminating in **WrestleMania 21** (April 3, Los Angeles, California).

**Key developments included:**
- **Triple H** won the vacant World Heavyweight Championship in an Elimination Chamber match at New Year's Revolution
- **Batista** won the Royal Rumble match, earning a World Championship match at WrestleMania 21
- **John Cena** defeated Kurt Angle at No Way Out to become #1 contender for WWE Championship at WrestleMania 21
- **JBL** successfully defended the WWE Championship at Royal Rumble and No Way Out
- **Eddie Guerrero & Rey Mysterio** won the WWE Tag Team Championship at No Way Out
- **Chavo Guerrero** won the Cruiserweight Championship at No Way Out
- **Vince McMahon** legitimately tore both quadriceps during the Royal Rumble match

## Injuries / Backstage Incidents / Controversies

### Vince McMahon's Legitimate Injury
During the Royal Rumble match, Vince McMahon legitimately tore both quadriceps when his knees caught on the apron while angrily rushing to the ring after Batista and John Cena both went over the top rope at the same time. McMahon was in intense pain and could not stand, delivering his lines while sitting on his butt in the ring, leaning against the ropes. Despite the legitimate injury, McMahon ordered the match to be restarted with just the final two participants.

### Randy Orton's Storyline Concussion
At Royal Rumble, Randy Orton suffered a storyline concussion during his World Heavyweight Championship match against Triple H. Orton was unable to recover and was pinned by Triple H, allowing Triple H to retain the title.

### Evolution's Disbandment
After WrestleMania 21, when Batista won the World Heavyweight Championship from Triple H, Evolution effectively disbanded. Batista turned face and began a feud with his former stablemates, marking the end of one of the most dominant stables in WWE history.

### First WWE PPV in Puerto Rico
New Year's Revolution 2005 was the first WWE PPV ever held in Puerto Rico and Latin America. The event sold out in less than a month, marking one of the fastest-selling events in the venue's history. This was a significant milestone for WWE's international expansion.

### First Money in the Bank Ladder Match
WrestleMania 21 featured the first-ever Money in the Bank Ladder Match, which was won by Edge. This match type would become a staple of WWE programming and would eventually lead to the creation of the Money in the Bank PPV.

### Barbed Wire Steel Cage Match
The JBL vs. Big Show match at No Way Out was the first Barbed Wire Steel Cage Match in WWE history. This extreme match type was a significant departure from WWE's more family-friendly product at the time.

## Conclusion

The January-March 2005 period was defined by Triple H's victory in the Elimination Chamber to win the vacant World Heavyweight Championship, Batista's rise to main event status with his Royal Rumble victory, and John Cena's emergence as a top star with his #1 contender spot at No Way Out. The quarter opened with New Year's Revolution, the first WWE PPV ever held in Puerto Rico, where Triple H won the vacant World Heavyweight Championship in an Elimination Chamber match.

Royal Rumble featured Batista's victory in the Royal Rumble match, earning him a World Championship shot at WrestleMania 21. The match had a bizarre finish where both Batista and John Cena went over the top rope at the same time, leading to Vince McMahon legitimately tearing both quadriceps while angrily rushing to the ring. McMahon ordered the match to be restarted, and Batista eliminated Cena to win.

No Way Out was highlighted by JBL's successful defense of the WWE Championship against Big Show in the first Barbed Wire Steel Cage Match in WWE history. John Cena defeated Kurt Angle to become the #1 contender for the WWE Championship at WrestleMania 21, and Eddie Guerrero & Rey Mysterio won the WWE Tag Team Championship from The Basham Brothers.

The quarter culminated with WrestleMania 21 (April 3), where John Cena won the WWE Championship from JBL, ending JBL's 280-day reign, and Batista won the World Heavyweight Championship from Triple H, effectively disbanding Evolution and beginning a new era for WWE. The event also featured the first-ever Money in the Bank Ladder Match, won by Edge.

By the end of March 2005 (and into early April with WrestleMania 21), the championship landscape was: John Cena (WWE Champion, SmackDown!), Batista (World Heavyweight Champion, Raw), Shelton Benjamin (Intercontinental Champion, Raw), Eugene & William Regal (World Tag Team Champions, Raw), Eddie Guerrero & Rey Mysterio (WWE Tag Team Champions, SmackDown!), Trish Stratus (Women's Champion, Raw), Chavo Guerrero (Cruiserweight Champion, SmackDown!), and John Cena (United States Champion, SmackDown! - though he would likely vacate this upon winning the WWE Championship).

The period marked a significant transition in WWE, with the end of Evolution's dominance, the rise of John Cena and Batista as top stars, and the beginning of a new era that would define WWE for the next several years.
