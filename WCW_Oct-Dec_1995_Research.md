# WCW Research Report: October 1995 – December 1995

> **Note re: continuity with previous reports:** At the end of September 1995, the titleholders were:
> - WCW World Heavyweight: **Hulk Hogan** (held since 7/17/1994)
> - WCW US Heavyweight: **Sting** (won at Great American Bash, 6/18/1995)
> - WCW World Tag Team: **Harlem Heat** (Booker T & Stevie Ray) — regained from American Males ~September 27, 1995
> - WCW World Television: **Diamond Dallas Page** (won from Renegade at Fall Brawl, 9/17/1995)
>
> This is the most chaotic period for title changes in WCW's history. The World Title changes hands FOUR times. The US Title leaves the United States for the first time. Monday Nitro is now fully operational and the Monday Night Wars are underway.

### 1B. WCW World Heavyweight Championship — Hogan → The Giant (via DQ, controversial)

- **Date:** October 29, 1995
- **Championship:** WCW World Heavyweight Championship
- **Winner:** The Giant (w/ The Taskmaster / Kevin Sullivan)
- **Loser:** Hulk Hogan (c) (w/ Jimmy Hart)
- **Show:** WCW Halloween Havoc 1995 (PPV)
- **PPV date:** October 29, 1995
- **Venue:** Joe Louis Arena, Detroit, Michigan
- **Details:** The Giant defeated Hogan by DQ at ~14:30 (some sources say 16:57). Jimmy Hart hit the referee with the title belt. Jimmy Hart then hit Hogan with the belt and joined the Dungeon of Doom. The Yeti appeared and double-teamed Hogan with the Giant. Lex Luger turned heel — attacked Savage and put Hogan in the Torture Rack, joining the Dungeon. **However**, a contract clause (written by Hart) stipulated the title could change hands on DQ. The Giant was awarded the belt by the referee. This was immediately controversial.
- **Note:** The Monster Truck Sumo Match was pre-taped on October 28, 1995 (on the roof of Cobo Hall, Detroit). Hogan's truck won. The Giant fell/jumped off the roof after the match — appeared in the ring later that night as if nothing happened.

### 1H. WCW United States Heavyweight Championship — Kensuke Sasaki → One Man Gang (controversial, later reversed)

- **Date:** December 27, 1995
- **Championship:** WCW United States Heavyweight Championship
- **Winner:** One Man Gang (initially announced)
- **Loser:** Kensuke Sasaki (c)
- **Show:** WCW Starrcade '95 (dark match, taped for later broadcast)
- **Details:** One Man Gang pinned Sasaki after a splash. The referee (Randy Eller) made the 3-count even though Sasaki had kicked out. OMG celebrated with the belt. Then another official pointed out the error and the match was restarted. Sasaki pinned OMG to retain. WCW showed the OMG pin on Saturday Night but **never acknowledged the restart**, choosing to recognize One Man Gang as champion.
- **Air date:** Parts shown on WCW Saturday Night (taped for later broadcast)
- **[FLAG]:** This is one of the most bizarre title situations in wrestling history. Wikipedia says "WCW never acknowledged that the match was restarted, choosing to recognize the One Man Gang as champion instead." However, at World War 3 (November 26), Sasaki was still presented as US Champion (defeated Chris Benoit). So the OMG "title change" happened at Starrcade (December 27), which is technically AFTER the November 26 defense. This needs careful handling.
- **Confidence:** HIGH that the botched pin and restart both happened. MEDIUM on whether WCW officially recognized OMG as champion immediately or if this was a slow build.
- **Source:**
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starrcade_(1995) ("After the main event WCW held one additional match... While Sasaki kicked out of the pinfall, referee Randy Eller still made the three-count... the match was restarted. Sasaki then pinned Gang to retain the title... WCW never acknowledged that the match was restarted, choosing to recognize the One Man Gang as champion instead")

## 3. Roster Moves / Debuts / Exits

### 3A. The Yeti — Debuts (October 23, 1995)

- **Date:** October 23, 1995 (Nitro #8, Huntsville, AL)
- **Person:** Ron Reis (The Yeti)
- **Change:** Debuted as Dungeon of Doom's "insurance policy" — encased in a giant block of ice. A mummy-like creature. Attacked Hogan alongside Giant at Halloween Havoc.
- **Impact:** Immediately one of the most ridiculed gimmicks in wrestling history. Made only a handful of appearances.

- **Date:** October-November 1995 (gradual)

### 3G. Steve Austin — Fired (September 15, 1995)

- **Date:** September 15, 1995
- **Person:** "Stunning" Steve Austin
- **Change:** Fired by Eric Bischoff while recovering from torn triceps (suffered May 27, 1995 in Japan). Bischoff did not see Austin as marketable. Austin went to ECW, then WWF (December 1995).
- **Why this falls in this period:** While the firing technically falls in the July–September window, Austin's last televised WCW match aired on WCW Pro July 1, 1995. He was effectively gone from WCW by October 1995.

## 4. Injuries / Backstage Incidents / Controversies

### 4A. The Giant "Falls Off the Roof" (October 28, 1995)

- **Date:** October 28, 1995 (taped); aired October 29 (PPV)
- **People:** The Giant, Hulk Hogan
- **What happened:** After losing the Monster Truck Sumo Match on the roof of Cobo Hall, The Giant chased Hogan toward the roof edge. Hogan pushed/accidentally sent the Giant off the building. The Giant then appeared in the ring later that same night for their World Title match as if nothing happened.
- **Why it mattered:** One of the most infamous no-sells in wrestling history. Heenan tried to explain it by saying Giant "may have fallen on a car or truck."
- **Confidence:** High (it happened on PPV).
- **Source:**
  - https://www.thesportster.com/strange-confusing-weird-moments-big-show-wcw-career-forgotten-the-giant/

### 4C. Hogan Suspended for Rest of 1995

- **Date:** December 18, 1995 (aired on Nitro)
- **People:** Hulk Hogan
- **What happened:** Hogan attacked The Giant and several referees during Giant's title shot against Savage, resulting in Hogan being "suspended" for the rest of 1995 (kayfabe).
- **Why it mattered:** Removed Hogan from the title picture heading into 1996. Cleared the way for Flair's reign.
- **Confidence:** High.

### 4D. World Title Chaos — 4 Changes in 2 Months

- **Date:** October 29 – December 27, 1995
- **What happened:** The WCW World Title went: Hogan → Giant (DQ) → Vacated → Savage (battle royal) → Flair (Starrcade interference). Four title changes/handling events in ~2 months.
- **Why it mattered:** This rapid turnover severely damaged the title's prestige. Fans couldn't track who the champion was.
- **Confidence:** High.
- **Source:** Multiple sources above.

## 7. Potential Implementation Corrections

If a booking simulator is running this period, the following corrections may be needed:

### Event Timing Corrections
- **Halloween Havoc:** The Monster Truck Match was pre-taped October 28 (not live on October 29). The in-ring match was live.
- **Tag title changes in September–October:** The American Males won on Sept 18 (Nitro #3 dark match or house show), lost back to Harlem Heat at a Saturday Night taping ~Oct 11 (aired Oct 28). These rapid changes need precise date tracking.

### Missing Roster Additions
- **The Yeti (Ron Reis)** — must be added to roster from October 23, 1995.
- **Kurasawa (Manabu Nakanishi)** — must be added under Col. Parker's management. Debuted at Halloween Havoc (or earlier — needs verification).
- **One Man Gang** — active on roster. Phantom US Champion after Starrcade dark match botch.
- **King Curtis Iaukea (The Master)** — active as Dungeon of Doom behind-the-scenes leader.

### Title Lineage Adjustments
- **World Title:** Must track the Giant's brief "reign" (Oct 29 – Nov 6) as a special case — recognized at the time but later stripped. Some implementations may want to skip it entirely.
- **US Title:** The OMG/Sasaki Starrcade controversy creates a fork. WCW apparently recognized OMG as champion on TV, but Sasaki was still defended as champion at World War 3 (Nov 26). This suggests the "OMG as champion" recognition came AFTER Starrcade (Dec 27) and was a retroactive decision. Game should handle this as either: (a) Sasaki remained champion and the OMG "win" was never recognized, OR (b) OMG won at Starrcade dark match and was recognized going forward.
- **Tag Title:** American Males' reign (Sept 18–27) was only 9 days and at a house show/Nitro. May need to be tracked or consolidated.

### Faction/Team Addition
- **Four Horsemen (1995 version):** Flair, Anderson, Pillman, Benoit. Fully reformed by Halloween Havoc/Starrcade. Must be added as a faction.
- **Dungeon of Doom (expanded):** Now includes Lex Luger, Jimmy Hart (as manager), The Yeti, in addition to Giant, Sullivan, Kamala, Zodiac, Shark, Meng, Big Bubba.

### Injury/Morale/Business Effects
- **Hogan's mustache shave:** Visual change needed — Hogan in all black, no mustache, from ~mid-October onward.
- **Hogan's neckbrace:** From Fall Brawl (Sept 17) through at least November.
- **Hogan suspended (Dec 18):** Must be removed from active roster for rest of 1995.
- **Savage's 31-day reign:** Shortest in the game so far. Morale hit.
- **The Yeti's reception:** Significant morale/fan trust penalty. One of the worst-received gimmicks in company history.
- **4 World Title changes in 2 months:** Major prestige penalty for World Title.
- **Sting losing US Title in Japan:** Prestige hit — title left the country for the first time. May need special handling for international defenses.
- **Eddie Guerrero / Otani at Starrcade:** Major morale boost — match of the year contender. Push both up.
