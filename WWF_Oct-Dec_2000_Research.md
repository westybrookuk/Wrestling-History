# WWF October-December 2000 Research Report

**Period Covered:** October 1, 2000 - December 31, 2000

## Executive Summary

The October-December 2000 period was one of the most significant in WWF history, featuring three major PPV events — No Mercy (October 22, Albany), Survivor Series (November 21, Tampa), and Armageddon (December 10, Birmingham) — and marked by Kurt Angle winning his first WWF Championship, the revelation of Rikishi as the one who hit Steve Austin with a car, Triple H being revealed as the mastermind behind Austin's attack, and Kurt Angle retaining the WWF Championship in a six-man Hell in a Cell match at Armageddon. Key developments included: Kurt Angle defeating The Rock in a No Disqualification match at No Mercy to win his first WWF Championship; The Rock defeating Rikishi at Survivor Series; Triple H being revealed as the mastermind behind Steve Austin's attack at Survivor Series; Mick Foley announcing a six-man Hell in a Cell match for the WWF Championship at Armageddon; and Kurt Angle defeating The Rock, Steve Austin, Triple H, The Undertaker, and Rikishi in the six-man Hell in a Cell match at Armageddon to retain the WWF Championship. The period also saw Chris Benoit winning the Intercontinental Championship from Billy Gunn at Armageddon, and Edge & Christian winning the WWF Tag Team Championship from The Hardy Boyz at No Mercy (disguised as Los Conquistadores). No Mercy drew 14,342 attendance at Pepsi Arena in Albany with 550,000 buys. Survivor Series drew approximately 15,000 attendance at Ice Palace in Tampa. Armageddon drew approximately 15,000 attendance at Birmingham-Jefferson Civic Center in Birmingham.

## Major PPV Events

### No Mercy 2000 (October 22, 2000)

**Location:** Pepsi Arena, Albany, New York
**Attendance:** 14,342
**PPV Buyrate:** 550,000

**PPV Results:**
1. **Dudley Boyz (Bubba Ray & D-Von) won** — Dudley Boyz Invitational Tables Match (12:18) [Last eliminated Right to Censor (The Goodfather & Bull Buchanan)]
2. **Chris Jericho defeated X-Pac** — Steel Cage Match (10:40)
3. **Right to Censor (Steven Richards & Val Venis) defeated Chyna & Billy Gunn** — Tag Team Match (7:10)
4. **"Stone Cold" Steve Austin vs. Rikishi** — No Holds Barred Match — No Contest (9:21/10:30)
5. **William Regal (c) defeated Naked Mideon** — WWF European Championship (6:02/6:10) **William Regal retained**
6. **Los Conquistadores (Uno & Dos) defeated The Hardy Boyz (Matt & Jeff Hardy) (c)** — WWF Tag Team Championship (10:52/10:58) **Los Conquistadores WON Tag Team titles** [Los Conquistadores were Edge & Christian in masks]
7. **Triple H defeated Chris Benoit** — Singles Match (18:43/18:44)
8. **Kurt Angle (with Stephanie McMahon-Helmsley) defeated The Rock (c)** — WWF Championship — No Disqualification Match (21:01/21:45) **Kurt Angle WON WWF Championship** [Stephanie interfered; Triple H interfered; Rikishi interfered but accidentally hit Rock; Angle hit Angle Slam on Rock]

**Key Notes:**
- Kurt Angle won his first WWF Championship from The Rock
- Rikishi interfered but accidentally hit The Rock, allowing Angle to win
- Triple H interfered to help Angle (and to protect his WWF Championship storyline)
- Los Conquistadores (Edge & Christian in masks) won Tag Team titles from Hardy Boyz
- Steve Austin vs. Rikishi ended in No Contest
- Triple H defeated Chris Benoit
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_Mercy_(2000)

### Survivor Series 2000 (November 21, 2000)

**Location:** Ice Palace, Tampa, Florida
**Attendance:** Approximately 15,000
**PPV Buyrate:** Not specified in sources

**PPV Results:**
1. **Val Venis (with Bull Buchanan, Christian, Edge & The Goodfather) defeated Jeff Hardy (with Bubba Ray, D-Von, Lita & Matt Hardy)** — Singles Match (8:00)
2. **Steve Blackman, Crash Holly & Molly Holly defeated T & A (Albert & Test) and Trish Stratus** — Six-Person Intergender Tag Team Match (5:06)
3. **The Radicalz (Eddie Guerrero, Chris Benoit, Dean Malenko & Perry Saturn) (with Terri) defeated Billy Gunn, Chyna, K-Kwik & Road Dogg** — 4-on-4 Survivor Series Elimination Match (12:41)
4. **Kane defeated Chris Jericho** — Singles Match (12:35)

## Conclusion

The October-December 2000 period was one of the most significant in WWF history, featuring three major PPV events and major title changes and storylines. Kurt Angle defeated The Rock in a No Disqualification match at No Mercy to win his first WWF Championship. The Rock defeated Rikishi at Survivor Series. Triple H was revealed as the mastermind behind Steve Austin's attack at Survivor Series. Mick Foley announced a six-man Hell in a Cell match for the WWF Championship at Armageddon. Kurt Angle defeated The Rock, Steve Austin, Triple H, The Undertaker, and Rikishi in the six-man Hell in a Cell match at Armageddon to retain the WWF Championship. Chris Benoit won the Intercontinental Championship from Billy Gunn at Armageddon. Edge & Christian won the WWF Tag Team Championship from The Hardy Boyz at No Mercy (disguised as Los Conquistadores). No Mercy drew 14,342 attendance at Pepsi Arena in Albany with 550,000 buys. Survivor Series drew approximately 15,000 attendance at Ice Palace in Tampa. Armageddon drew approximately 15,000 attendance at Birmingham-Jefferson Civic Center in Birmingham.
