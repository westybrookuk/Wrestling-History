## 1. Title Changes

### ECW World Heavyweight Championship — Shane Douglas → Taz

**Title Change: Shane Douglas → Taz**

- **Date:** January 10, 1999
- **Championship:** ECW World Heavyweight Championship
- **Winner:** Taz
- **Loser:** Shane Douglas (c) (w/ Francine)
- **Show:** ECW Guilty as Charged 1999
- **Venue:** Millennium Theatre
- **City:** Kissimmee, Florida
- **Attendance:** 2,600
- **Finish:** Taz defeated Shane Douglas (c) by submission (Tazmission) to win the ECW World Heavyweight Championship (22:15). Douglas was attacked by former Triple Threat teammate Chris Candido during the match, allowing Taz to lock in the Tazmission for the victory.
- **Event type:** Pay-per-view
- **Why it mattered:** Taz won the ECW World Heavyweight Championship, ending Shane Douglas's historic 406-day reign (the longest in the title's history). This was Taz's first ECW World Heavyweight Championship reign and marked a major shift in ECW's power structure. Douglas's fourth reign as champion was the longest in the title's history, and Taz's victory signaled the rise of a new era in ECW.

### FTW Heavyweight Championship — Sabu → Unified with ECW World Title

**Title Change: Sabu → Taz (Unification)**

- **Date:** March 21, 1999
- **Championship:** FTW Heavyweight Championship (unified with ECW World Heavyweight Championship)
- **Winner:** Taz (ECW World Heavyweight Champion)
- **Loser:** Sabu (FTW Champion) (w/ Bill Alfonso)
- **Show:** ECW Living Dangerously 1999
- **Venue:** Asbury Park Convention Center
- **City:** Asbury Park, New Jersey
- **Attendance:** 3,900
- **Finish:** Taz (ECW World Heavyweight Champion) defeated Sabu (FTW Champion) in a Falls Count Anywhere title unification match to win the FTW Heavyweight Championship (18:28). Taz forced Sabu to submit to the Tazmission following a Dragon Tazplex through a table.
- **Event type:** Pay-per-view
- **Why it mattered:** Taz unified the FTW Heavyweight Championship with the ECW World Heavyweight Championship, effectively retiring the FTW title. This was Taz's second FTW Championship reign (though it lasted less than a day). The unification match was the culmination of a convoluted storyline where Taz had intentionally lost the FTW title to Sabu in December 1998 so they could do a unification match after Taz won the ECW World title. Sabu, entering the match with a pre-existing fractured jaw, sustained further damage during the brutal encounter. This was the last time the FTW Championship appeared on an ECW show until Taz revived it in AEW in 2020.

- **Date:** January 10, 1999

- **People involved:** Shane Douglas
- **Type:** Legitimate injuries
- **What happened:** Shane Douglas suffered from a slew of legitimate injuries in late 1998 and early 1999, including a serious sinus infection and elbow injury. These injuries contributed to ECW's decision to delay the title switch to Taz, as Douglas was unable to compete at full capacity. Douglas wore a cast on his right forearm during his title loss to Taz at Guilty as Charged.

- **What is disputed:** The extent of the injuries and their impact on booking decisions
