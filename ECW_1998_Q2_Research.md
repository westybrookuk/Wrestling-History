## 2. Major TV, PPV, Supercard, and Arena Events

### ECW Wrestlepalooza 1998 — May 3, 1998

- **Date:** May 3, 1998
- **Venue:** Cobb County Civic Center
- **City:** Marietta, Georgia
- **Attendance:** 3,407
- **Main matches:**
  - **ECW World Heavyweight Championship:** Shane Douglas (c) (with Francine) defeated Al Snow (with Head) to retain the title (13:05). This was Al Snow's farewell appearance before departing the promotion. Snow targeted Douglas's injured elbow early, incorporating his signature mannequin head prop to taunt and strike the champion, while Douglas retaliated with chair-assisted powerbombs and a rare plancha over the guardrail.
  - **ECW World Television Championship:** Rob Van Dam (c) (with Bill Alfonso) vs. Sabu (with Bill Alfonso) ended in a Time Limit Draw (30:00). Van Dam retained the title.
  - **ECW World Tag Team Championship:** Chris Candido & Lance Storm (c) (with Tammy Lynn Sytch) defeated The Hardcore Chair Swingin' Freaks (Axl Rotten and Balls Mahoney) to retain the titles (12:04/12:06)
  - Bam Bam Bigelow defeated New Jack (8:48/8:53)
  - Tommy Dreamer and The Sandman defeated The Dudley Boyz (Buh Buh Ray Dudley and D-Von Dudley) (11:20)
  - The bWo (The Blue Meanie and Super Nova) defeated The F.B.I. (Little Guido and Tracy Smothers) (with Tommy Rich) (9:28)
  - Justin Credible (with Jason and Chastity) defeated Mikey Whipwreck (9:54)
- **Major finishes:** 
  - Shane Douglas retained world title vs. Al Snow
  - RVD retained TV title vs. Sabu (time limit draw)
  - Chris Candido & Lance Storm retained tag titles
- **Important debuts/returns/exits:** 
  - **Al Snow's farewell appearance** — Snow departed the promotion after this match
- **Why it mattered:** This event featured Shane Douglas' successful title defense against Al Snow in Snow's farewell appearance. Rob Van Dam retained the ECW World Television Championship in a 30-minute time limit draw against Sabu. The event showcased ECW's top talent and continued the ongoing storylines of mid-1998 ECW.

### ECW It Ain't Seinfeld — May 14, 1998

- **Date:** May 14, 1998
- **Venue:** Elks Lodge
- **City:** Elmhurst, Queens, NY
- **Attendance:** 1,000
- **Main matches:**
  - **FTW Heavyweight Championship:** Taz introduced the FTW Heavyweight Championship during a promotional segment, declaring himself the inaugural champion. He presented a repainted version of an old ECW Television Championship belt, proclaiming himself the inaugural champion and dubbing it the FTW title—officially standing for "For The World," though it carried the provocative double meaning of "Fuck The World" to underscore its rebellious nature.
  - **ECW World Television Championship:** Rob Van Dam (c) vs. Mikey Whipwreck ended in a no-contest after approximately 10 minutes of action. This outcome preserved Van Dam's reign, avoiding a title change while building anticipation for subsequent defenses.
  - The Dudley Boyz (Buh Buh Ray Dudley, D-Von Dudley and Big Dick Dudley) defeated Tommy Dreamer, The Sandman and Spike Dudley in a six-man tag team match
  - Various other matches
- **Major finishes:** 
  - Taz introduced FTW Championship and declared himself inaugural champion
  - RVD vs. Mikey Whipwreck ended in no-contest
  - The Dudley Boyz defeated Dreamer, Sandman & Spike Dudley
- **Important debuts/returns/exits:** 
  - **FTW Championship creation** — Taz introduced the unsanctioned FTW Heavyweight Championship
- **Why it mattered:** This event was notable for Taz introducing the FTW Heavyweight Championship, an unsanctioned "rebel" title created out of frustration with ECW World Heavyweight Champion Shane Douglas's repeated avoidance of a title match due to claimed injuries. The event's title, "It Ain't Seinfeld," was a playful reference to the popular NBC sitcom Seinfeld, whose series finale aired on the same evening, positioning the show as an alternative entertainment option for wrestling enthusiasts.
