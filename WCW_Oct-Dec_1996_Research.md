# WCW Research Report: October 1996 – December 1996

> **Note re: continuity with previous reports:** At the end of September 1996, the titleholders were:
> - WCW World Heavyweight: **"Hollywood" Hogan** (nWo) (won from Giant at Hog Wild, 8/10/1996)
> - WCW US Heavyweight: **Ric Flair** (won from Konnan at Bash at the Beach, 7/7/1996)
> - WCW World Tag Team: **Harlem Heat** (Booker T & Stevie Ray)
> - WCW World Television: **Lex Luger**
> - WCW Cruiserweight: **Rey Mysterio Jr.** (won from Malenko, 7/8/1996)
>
> The nWo had won WarGames at Fall Brawl. Sting was evolving into the Crow. The Giant had joined the nWo at Fall Brawl.

## 1. Title Changes

### 1A. WCW Cruiserweight Championship — Rey Mysterio Jr. → Dean Malenko

- **Date:** October 27, 1996
- **Championship:** WCW World Cruiserweight Championship
- **Winner:** Dean Malenko
- **Loser:** Rey Mysterio Jr. (c)
- **Show:** WCW Halloween Havoc 1996 (PPV)
- **PPV date:** October 27, 1996
- **Venue:** MGM Grand Garden Arena, Las Vegas, Nevada
- **Attendance:** 10,000
- **Details:** Malenko pinned Mysterio at 18:32 with a gutwrench powerbomb off the top rope. Prior to the match, Malenko had stolen Mysterio's mask. During the match, Mysterio got his mask back and switched masks, but Malenko still won. Mysterio's reign lasted 111 days (Jul 8 – Oct 27).

### 1B. WCW World Tag Team Championship — Harlem Heat → The Outsiders (Hall & Nash)

- **Date:** October 27, 1996
- **Championship:** WCW World Tag Team Championship
- **Winners:** The Outsiders (Kevin Nash & Scott Hall) (nWo)
- **Losers:** Harlem Heat (Booker T & Stevie Ray) (c) (w/ Sister Sherri & Col. Robert Parker)

### 1C. WCW United States Heavyweight Championship — VACATED → Eddie Guerrero

- **Date:** December 29, 1996
- **Championship:** WCW United States Heavyweight Championship (VACANT)
- **Winner:** Eddie Guerrero
- **Loser:** Diamond Dallas Page
- **Show:** WCW Starrcade '96 (PPV) — Tournament Final
- **PPV date:** December 29, 1996
- **Venue:** Nashville Municipal Auditorium, Nashville, Tennessee
- **Attendance:** 9,030
- **Details:** Guerrero pinned Page at 15:20 with a frog splash after nWo interference (Hall, Nash, Syxx attacked Page, then Guerrero hit the splash). The title had been vacated sometime between Halloween Havoc and Starrcade. A tournament was held.
- **[FLAG — uncertain date of vacation]:** Ric Flair was recognized as US Champion through September house shows. The Giant was parading with the US Title belt at Halloween Havoc (Oct 27) despite never having won it. Flair was originally scheduled to face Giant at Halloween Havoc but was "beaten up" by nWo. Jeff Jarrett replaced Flair. The exact date Flair lost/was stripped of the title, and when it was officially vacated, is uncertain — likely late October or November 1996. The Giant had possession of the belt for a period without being recognized as champion.
- **Confidence:** High that Eddie won at Starrcade. Medium on exact vacation date of the title.

## 2. Major TV/PPV Events

### 2A. Sting's Crow Gimmick Begins — October 21 & 28, 1996

- **Date:** October 21, 1996 (first appearance with white face paint); October 28, 1996 (full Crow look)
- **Show:** WCW Monday Nitro
- **What happened:**
  - **October 21:** Sting appeared on Nitro wearing white face paint (reminiscent of his Surfer look but in black and white). He attacked nWo Sting and said "The only thing that's for sure about Sting is nothing's for sure." This was the **last time Sting spoke on camera for over a year.**
  - **October 28:** Sting appeared in the rafters with a black trench coat and the full black-and-white Crow face paint — the iconic look he would maintain for years. He carried a baseball bat.
- **Why it mattered:** The Crow Sting was born. Inspired by the movie "The Crow" (and reportedly suggested by Scott Hall), Sting went silent, appeared in rafters, and became the most mysterious figure in wrestling. He wouldn't wrestle regularly for months.

## 4. Injuries / Backstage Incidents / Controversies

- **Date:** October–November 1996
- **What happened:** Flair was supposed to face the Giant at Halloween Havoc but was "beaten up" by nWo. Jeff Jarrett replaced him. The Giant brought the US Title belt to the ring despite never having won it. After Halloween Havoc, the title situation became unclear. By Starrcade, it was vacated.
- **Why it mattered:** The US Title lineage became murky. The Giant had possession without being champion. Flair's reign just... ended. The title was vacated and a tournament held.

- **Date:** December 29, 1996

- **nWo** remains dominant. The Giant is now a confirmed member (joined at Fall Brawl). Internal tensions beginning (Giant vs. Luger at Starrcade).

### Injury/Morale/Business Effect
- **Sting's Crow gimmick:** Maximum intrigue. He's not wrestling but is must-see TV. Massive positive morale/intrigue effect.
- **Eddie Guerrero US Title win:** Massive positive for Mexican division and cruiserweight fans.
- **Starrcade main event non-title:** NEGATIVE fan trust. Critics and fans angry.
- **J-Crown unification:** POSITIVE. Best match of the year. Cruiserweight division at peak.
- **nWo infighting (Giant vs. Luger):** Early sign of faction fracture. Could be implemented as a slow-burn tension.
