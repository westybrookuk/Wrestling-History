## Executive Summary

The October-December 2004 period was dominated by the ongoing Randy Orton vs. Evolution rivalry, Triple H's continued World Heavyweight Championship reign, and JBL's successful defense of the WWE Championship. This quarter featured three major PPV events: **Taboo Tuesday** (October 19, Milwaukee, Wisconsin), **Survivor Series** (November 14, Charlotte, North Carolina), and **Armageddon** (December 12, Duluth, Georgia).

**Key developments included:**
- **Triple H** retained the World Heavyweight Championship at Taboo Tuesday against Shawn Michaels (fan-voted opponent) and continued his dominance
- **Randy Orton** defeated his mentor Ric Flair in a Steel Cage match at Taboo Tuesday, continuing his feud with Evolution
- **Team Orton** (Randy Orton, Chris Benoit, Chris Jericho, Maven) defeated **Team Triple H** (Triple H, Edge, Batista, Gene Snitsky) at Survivor Series in a traditional elimination match
- **Chris Benoit & Edge** won the World Tag Team Championship from La Résistance at Taboo Tuesday
- **Shelton Benjamin** won the Intercontinental Championship from Chris Jericho at Taboo Tuesday
- **JBL** successfully defended the WWE Championship at both Survivor Series (vs. Booker T) and Armageddon (in a Fatal 4-Way)
- **John Cena** successfully defended the United States Championship at Armageddon in a Street Fight against Jesús
- **Funaki** won the Cruiserweight Championship from Spike Dudley at Armageddon

**PPV Statistics:**

## Injuries / Backstage Incidents / Controversies

### Eric Bischoff's Return and World Heavyweight Championship Vacated
After Team Orton's month-long control of Raw ended, Eric Bischoff returned as General Manager on November 22. On November 29, Randy Orton (as acting GM) scheduled a World Heavyweight Championship Battle Royal. Vince McMahon overruled this and instead booked a Triple Threat match between Chris Benoit, Edge, and Triple H. The match ended in confusion when Benoit applied the Crossface on Edge, but Edge rolled Benoit over, causing Benoit's shoulders to be down. Edge submitted but the referee began counting. The following week, Chris Jericho (as acting GM) had Vince McMahon vacate the World Heavyweight Championship, concluding the match ended in a tie.

### Team Orton's Control of Raw
As a result of winning the Survivor Series elimination match, Team Orton (Randy Orton, Chris Benoit, Chris Jericho, and Maven) were given control of Raw for 4 weeks. Each member had one week as "GM":
- Week 1: Randy Orton
- Week 2: Chris Benoit
- Week 3: Chris Jericho
- Week 4: Maven

This led to various booking decisions and storylines, culminating in the confusion that led to the World Heavyweight Championship being vacated.

### Taboo Tuesday Fan Voting
Taboo Tuesday was unique as the first PPV where fans voted on multiple aspects of the matches via phone and online. Fans voted on:
- Triple H's opponent (Shawn Michaels won with 38.72%)
- Randy Orton vs. Ric Flair stipulation (Steel Cage won with 68%)
- Weapon of Choice match weapon (Chain won with 40.84%)
- Eugene vs. Bischoff stipulation (Loser has head shaved won with 58.73%)
- Christy Hemme vs. Carmella stipulation (Lingerie Pillow Fight won with 56.48%)
- Chris Jericho's opponent (Shelton Benjamin won with 37.48%)

### JBL's Continued WWE Championship Reign
JBL successfully defended the WWE Championship at both Survivor Series (vs. Booker T) and Armageddon (in a Fatal 4-Way). At Survivor Series, the stipulation was that JBL would retire if he lost. At Armageddon, the stipulation was that if The Cabinet (JBL's allies) interfered, JBL would lose the title. Heidenreich interfered and attacked The Undertaker, allowing JBL to retain.

### Randy Orton vs. Evolution Feud
The Randy Orton vs. Evolution feud continued throughout this period. At Taboo Tuesday, Orton defeated his mentor Ric Flair in a Steel Cage match. This victory earned Orton a World Heavyweight Championship shot at Survivor Series. At Survivor Series, Orton led Team Orton to victory over Team Triple H, with Orton eliminating Triple H last to be the sole survivor.

### Low PPV Buyrates
Taboo Tuesday had a very poor buyrate of 174,000, considered "dreadful" and attributed to it being a "filler PPV." SmackDown! brand-only PPVs were suffering in terms of buyrates. Survivor Series (325,000-373,000) and Armageddon (230,000-242,000) also had lower buyrates compared to previous years.

## Conclusion

The October-December 2004 period was defined by the ongoing Randy Orton vs. Evolution rivalry, Triple H's continued World Heavyweight Championship reign, and JBL's successful defense of the WWE Championship. The quarter opened with Taboo Tuesday, the first PPV where fans voted on multiple aspects of the matches. Triple H retained the World Heavyweight Championship against Shawn Michaels (fan-voted opponent), and Randy Orton defeated his mentor Ric Flair in a Steel Cage match. Chris Benoit & Edge won the World Tag Team Championship from La Résistance, and Shelton Benjamin won the Intercontinental Championship from Chris Jericho.

Survivor Series featured the traditional elimination matches, with Team Orton (Randy Orton, Chris Benoit, Chris Jericho, Maven) defeating Team Triple H (Triple H, Edge, Batista, Gene Snitsky). Randy Orton was the sole survivor, eliminating Triple H last with an RKO. As a result, Team Orton was given control of Raw for 4 weeks. JBL successfully defended the WWE Championship vs. Booker T (with the stipulation that JBL would retire if he lost).

Armageddon was a SmackDown! brand-exclusive PPV where JBL successfully defended the WWE Championship in a Fatal 4-Way match vs. Eddie Guerrero, Booker T, and The Undertaker. John Cena retained the United States Championship in a Street Fight vs. Jesús, and Funaki won the Cruiserweight Championship from Spike Dudley.

The period also saw the World Heavyweight Championship being vacated after confusion at the November 29 Raw episode, where Chris Benoit applied the Crossface on Edge, but Edge rolled Benoit over, causing Benoit's shoulders to be down. Edge submitted but the referee began counting. Chris Jericho (as acting GM) had Vince McMahon vacate the title, concluding the match ended in a tie.

By the end of December 2004, the championship landscape was: JBL (WWE Champion, SmackDown!), World Heavyweight Championship (vacated, to be determined at New Year's Revolution), Shelton Benjamin (Intercontinental Champion, Raw), Chris Benoit & Edge (World Tag Team Champions, Raw), Rob Van Dam & Rey Mysterio (WWE Tag Team Champions, SmackDown!), Trish Stratus (Women's Champion, Raw), Funaki (Cruiserweight Champion, SmackDown!), and John Cena (United States Champion, SmackDown!).

The low PPV buyrates (Taboo Tuesday: 174,000; Survivor Series: 325,000-373,000; Armageddon: 230,000-242,000) continued to be a concern, with Taboo Tuesday's poor performance attributed to it being a "filler PPV" and SmackDown! brand-only PPVs suffering.
