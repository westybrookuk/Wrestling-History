# WWF October-December 2003 Research Report

**Period Covered:** October 1, 2003 - December 31, 2003

## Executive Summary

The October-December 2003 period marked the culmination of Goldberg's dominant push and Evolution's complete takeover of Raw. This quarter featured three major PPV events: **No Mercy** (October 19, Baltimore, MD), **Survivor Series** (November 16, Dallas, TX), and **Armageddon** (December 14, Orlando, FL).

**Key developments included:**
- **Brock Lesnar** retained the WWE Championship in two consecutive PPV matches
- **Big Show** won the United States Championship from Eddie Guerrero at No Mercy
- **Goldberg** retained the World Heavyweight Championship at Survivor Series despite Evolution's interference
- **Triple H** regained the World Heavyweight Championship at Armageddon in a Triple Threat match
- **Randy Orton** won the Intercontinental Championship, completing Evolution's hold on all Raw titles
- **Batista** returned from injury on October 20, claiming the $100,000 bounty on Goldberg
- **Steve Austin** forced to step down as Raw Co-General Manager after Team Austin lost at Survivor Series

**PPV Statistics:**
- **No Mercy:** 8,500 attendance, 254,000 buys (0.50 buyrate), $500,000 gate
- **Survivor Series:** 13,487 attendance, 450,000 buys
- **Armageddon:** 12,672 attendance, 200,000 buys

## Injuries / Backstage Incidents / Controversies

### Evolution's $100,000 Bounty on Goldberg
On September 29, 2003, Triple H issued a $100,000 bounty to anybody who could "take Bill Goldberg out of the game." This bounty was claimed by Batista on October 20 when he returned from injury and attacked Goldberg during a match, stomping on a steel chair with Goldberg's ankle sandwiched in it.

### Steve Austin Forced to Step Down
At Survivor Series, Team Austin (Shawn Michaels, Rob Van Dam, Booker T, Bubba Ray Dudley, D-Von Dudley) lost to Team Bischoff (Chris Jericho, Christian, Randy Orton, Scott Steiner, Mark Henry). The stipulation was that if Team Austin lost, Steve Austin would be forced to step down as Raw Co-General Manager. Austin was forced to relinquish his position, making Eric Bischoff the sole Raw General Manager.

### Evolution's Complete Title Hold
At Armageddon, Evolution achieved complete dominance of Raw by holding all male championships:
- Triple H: World Heavyweight Championship
- Randy Orton: Intercontinental Championship
- Ric Flair & Batista: World Tag Team Championship

This marked the first time a single stable held all male titles on one brand.

### Batista's Injury Return
Batista returned from a legitimate triceps tear (suffered March 1, 2003) on October 20, 2003. His return was timed perfectly with the Goldberg bounty, allowing him to immediately impact the main event scene.

### Vince McMahon's "I Quit" Match Victory
At No Mercy, Vince McMahon defeated his daughter Stephanie McMahon in an "I Quit" Loser Gets Fired match. This was part of the ongoing McMahon family feud and resulted in Stephanie being "fired" from her SmackDown! General Manager position (though she would return).

## Conclusion

The October-December 2003 period was defined by Evolution's complete takeover of Raw and Goldberg's resilient title defense at Survivor Series. The quarter opened with No Mercy, where Brock Lesnar retained the WWE Championship and Big Show won the United States Championship. Survivor Series featured Goldberg's successful retention of the World Heavyweight Championship despite Evolution's constant interference, and Steve Austin being forced to step down as Co-General Manager.

The period culminated with Armageddon, where Evolution achieved unprecedented dominance by holding all male championships on Raw: Triple H (World Heavyweight), Randy Orton (Intercontinental), and Ric Flair & Batista (World Tag Team). This marked the first time a single stable held all male titles on one brand.

Batista's return from injury on October 20 was perfectly timed with the $100,000 bounty on Goldberg, allowing him to immediately impact the main event scene. The McMahon family feud continued with Vince defeating Stephanie in an "I Quit" match at No Mercy and defeating The Undertaker in a Buried Alive match at Survivor Series.

By the end of December 2003, the championship landscape was: Brock Lesnar (WWE Champion, SmackDown!), Triple H (World Heavyweight Champion, Raw), Randy Orton (Intercontinental Champion, Raw), Evolution/Flair & Batista (World Tag Team Champions, Raw), Basham Brothers (WWE Tag Team Champions, SmackDown!), Molly Holly (Women's Champion, Raw), Tajiri (Cruiserweight Champion, SmackDown!), and Big Show (United States Champion, SmackDown!). Evolution's complete dominance of Raw set the stage for 2004, where they would face challenges from various opponents trying to break their stranglehold on the brand.
