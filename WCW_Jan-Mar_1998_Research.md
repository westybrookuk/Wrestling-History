# WCW Research Report: January 1998 – March 1998

> **Note re: continuity with previous reports:** At the end of December 1997, the titleholders were:
> - WCW World Heavyweight: **VACATED** (after Starrcade controversy)
> - WCW US Heavyweight: **Diamond Dallas Page** (won from Hennig at Starrcade, 12/28/1997)
> - WCW World Tag Team: **The Steiner Brothers** (Rick & Scott) (won from Outsiders, 10/13/1997)
> - WCW World Television: **Booker T** (won from Disco Inferno, 12/29/1997)
> - WCW Cruiserweight: **[UNCERTAIN — needs research]**
> - WCW Women's: **Akira Hokuto** (inaugural, 12/29/1996)
>
> **Sting** was recognized as champion from Starrcade but the title was vacated in early January 1998 programming.
> **Goldberg** continued his undefeated squash streak.
> **Bret Hart** debuted in December 1997 and was establishing himself.
> **The nWo** was still unified but showing signs of internal division.

## 1. Title Changes

### 1A. WCW World Heavyweight Championship — Vacant → Sting

- **Date:** February 22, 1998
- **Championship:** WCW World Heavyweight Championship (VACANT)
- **Winner:** Sting
- **Loser:** "Hollywood" Hogan
- **Show:** WCW/nWo SuperBrawl VIII (PPV)
- **PPV date:** February 22, 1998
- **Venue:** Cow Palace, Daly City (San Francisco), California
- **Attendance:** 12,620
- **Buyrate:** 415,000
- **Details:**
  - Sting pinned Hogan after a Scorpion Death Drop at approximately 16:33.
  - **Randy Savage interfered** — hit Hogan with a spray can, allowing Sting to capitalize.
  - This was Sting's **second recognized World Title reign** (after the controversial Starrcade win in December 1997, which was vacated).
  - After the match, Sting spray-painted "WCW" on Hogan's torso. JJ Dillon presented Sting with the World Title belt.
  - This was the resolution to the Starrcade controversy — a clean(ish) win for Sting.
- **Why it mattered:**
  - Sting finally had a clean(ish) World Title win after the Starrcade debacle.
  - Savage's interference signaled his growing tension with Hogan and the nWo.
  - The nWo was beginning to fracture internally.
  - Sting's reign would last 56 days (Feb 22 – Apr 19, when Savage won it at Spring Stampede).
- **Source:**

### 1B. WCW World Tag Team Championship — Steiner Brothers → The Outsiders

- **Date:** February 22, 1998
- **Championship:** WCW World Tag Team Championship
- **Winners:** The Outsiders (Scott Hall & Kevin Nash) (w/ Dusty Rhodes)
- **Losers:** The Steiner Brothers (Rick & Scott Steiner) (c) (w/ Ted DiBiase)
- **Show:** WCW/nWo SuperBrawl VIII (PPV)
- **PPV date:** February 22, 1998
- **Venue:** Cow Palace, Daly City, California
- **Details:**
  - The Outsiders won the tag titles in just 4:18.
  - **SCOTT STEINER TURNED ON RICK** during the match and joined the nWo.
  - This was a massive betrayal — the Steiner Brothers were one of WCW's top babyface teams.
  - Scott Steiner's turn was one of the biggest shocks of 1998.
  - Dusty Rhodes was associated with the Outsiders at this point.
- **Why it mattered:**
  - The Steiner Brothers were destroyed from within.
  - Scott Steiner's nWo membership gave the faction a massive boost.
  - This was the beginning of "Big Poppa Pump" Scott Steiner.
  - The Outsiders now had the tag titles again.
- **Source:**
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SuperBrawl_VIII
  - https://www.onlineworldofwrestling.com/profile/nwo/
  - https://thehistoryofwwe.com/wcw-results-1998/

**[FLAG — DATE CONFLICT]:** Some Nitro results archives show "February 9, 1998: The Outsiders defeated The Steiner Brothers to win the World Tag Team Titles." However, multiple sources confirm the Steiners were champions going INTO SuperBrawl VIII (Feb 22) and lost them there when Scott turned. The Feb 9 result may be incorrect or may have been a non-title match. The SuperBrawl VIII title change is the confirmed, documented change.

- **Venue:** Hara Arena, Dayton, Ohio
- **Attendance:** (not precisely reported)
- **Full results:**

    - **Sting def. Bret Hart and Hollywood Hogan** — Sting pinned Hogan with the Scorpion Death Drop at 12:39.

  - Sting was officially recognized as World Champion after the Starrcade controversy.
  - The nWo was still dominant but tensions were building (Savage interference).

## 4. Injuries / Backstage Incidents / Controversies

### 4A. Starrcade Controversy Resolution (January 1998)

- **Date:** Early January 1998 (programming)
- **What happened:** After the controversial Starrcade finish (Dec 28, 1997) where Sting won the World Title but the finish was botched, and the rematch on Nitro (Dec 29) which was also controversial, the title was vacated in early January 1998 programming. At Souled Out (Jan 24), Sting, Hogan, and Bret Hart competed in a triangle match, with Sting winning to be officially recognized as champion. Then at SuperBrawl VIII (Feb 22), Sting defeated Hogan for the vacant title.
- **Why it mattered:** The Starrcade controversy took two PPVs and multiple weeks of programming to resolve. It damaged fan trust and showed WCW's booking instability.
- **Confidence:** High.
- **Source:**
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starrcade_(1997)
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SuperBrawl_VIII

### 4B. Scott Steiner's Betrayal of Rick

- **Date:** February 22, 1998 (SuperBrawl VIII)
- **What happened:** Scott Steiner turned on his brother Rick during their tag team title defense, joining the nWo.
- **Why it mattered:** One of the biggest betrayals in WCW history. The Steiner Brothers were one of WCW's top babyface teams. Scott's turn shocked fans and gave the nWo a massive boost.
- **Confidence:** High.
- **Source:**
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SuperBrawl_VIII
  - https://www.onlineworldofwrestling.com/profile/nwo/

### 4C. nWo Internal Tensions

- **Date:** January–March 1998 (ongoing)
- **What happened:** The nWo was showing signs of internal division:
  - Savage was frustrated with Hogan's leadership.
  - Nash was questioning Hogan's decisions.
  - Savage interfered against Hogan at SuperBrawl VIII.
- **Why it mattered:** The nWo was at its peak in terms of membership but was beginning to fracture. This would lead to the official split into nWo Hollywood and nWo Wolfpac in April/May 1998.
- **Confidence:** High.
