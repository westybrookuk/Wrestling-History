# WWF October-December 2001 Research Report

**Period Covered:** October 1, 2001 - December 31, 2001

## Executive Summary

The October-December 2001 period marked the end of The Invasion storyline and the beginning of title unifications, featuring three major PPV events — No Mercy (October 21, St. Louis), Survivor Series (November 18, Greensboro), and Vengeance (December 9, San Diego) — and marked by the end of The Invasion storyline at Survivor Series when Team WWF defeated The Alliance, and Chris Jericho becoming the first-ever Undisputed WWF Champion at Vengeance by unifying the WWF Championship and World Championship (formerly WCW Championship). Key developments included: Steve Austin retaining the WWF Championship at No Mercy in a Triple Threat match against Kurt Angle and Rob Van Dam; The Rock defeating Chris Jericho at No Mercy to win the World Championship; Team WWF (The Rock, Chris Jericho, The Undertaker, Kane, and Big Show) defeating The Alliance (Stone Cold Steve Austin, Kurt Angle, Rob Van Dam, Booker T, and Shane McMahon) in a Winner Takes All Survivor Series elimination match at Survivor Series, ending The Invasion storyline; The Dudley Boyz defeating The Hardy Boyz at Survivor Series to unify the WWF Tag Team Championship and WCW Tag Team Championship; Chris Jericho defeating The Rock at Vengeance to win the World Championship; Chris Jericho defeating Steve Austin at Vengeance to win the WWF Championship and become the first-ever Undisputed WWF Champion; and The Undertaker defeating Rob Van Dam at Vengeance to win the WWF Hardcore Championship. No Mercy drew 15,000+ attendance at the Savvis Center in St. Louis. Survivor Series drew 15,000+ attendance at the Greensboro Coliseum in Greensboro. Vengeance drew 12,000+ attendance at the San Diego Sports Arena in San Diego.

## Conclusion

The October-December 2001 period marked the end of The Invasion storyline and the beginning of title unifications, featuring three major PPV events and major title changes and storylines. The Invasion storyline ended at Survivor Series when Team WWF defeated The Alliance in a Winner Takes All Survivor Series elimination match. Chris Jericho became the first-ever Undisputed WWF Champion at Vengeance by unifying the WWF Championship and World Championship (formerly WCW Championship). Steve Austin retained the WWF Championship at No Mercy in a Triple Threat match against Kurt Angle and Rob Van Dam. The Rock defeated Chris Jericho at No Mercy to win the World Championship, won it back on Raw, then lost it back to Jericho at Vengeance. Edge won the WWF Intercontinental Championship from Christian at No Mercy and unified it with the WCW United States Championship at Survivor Series. The Dudley Boyz won the WWF Tag Team Championship on Raw and unified it with the WCW Tag Team Championship at Survivor Series. Trish Stratus won the vacant WWF Women's Championship at Survivor Series. The Undertaker won the WWF Hardcore Championship from Rob Van Dam at Vengeance. William Regal turned on WWF and joined The Alliance on Raw (Oct 8), and Mick Foley replaced Regal as Commissioner on SmackDown (Oct 11). No Mercy drew 15,000+ attendance at the Savvis Center in St. Louis. Survivor Series drew 15,000+ attendance at the Greensboro Coliseum in Greensboro. Vengeance drew 12,000+ attendance at the San Diego Sports Arena in San Diego.
