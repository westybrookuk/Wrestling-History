# WWF January-March 1998 Research Report

**Period Covered:** January 1, 1998 - March 31, 1998

## Executive Summary

The January-March 1998 period was a watershed moment in WWF history, widely regarded as the beginning of the "Attitude Era." The period featured three major PPV events: Royal Rumble (January 18, San Jose), No Way Out of Texas: In Your House (February 15, Houston), and WrestleMania XIV (March 29, Boston). The most significant development was "Stone Cold" Steve Austin winning the WWF Championship from Shawn Michaels at WrestleMania XIV, with Mike Tyson as special enforcer — a moment that signalled the changing of the guard in the WWF. Austin won the Royal Rumble for the second consecutive year, Shawn Michaels suffered a legitimate back injury at Royal Rumble that would force him out of wrestling for four years, Mike Tyson made his famous WWF appearance aligning with (and ultimately betraying) D-Generation X, and the new DX Army was formed the night after WrestleMania with Triple H, X-Pac, and the New Age Outlaws. Other key developments included The Rock retaining the Intercontinental Championship throughout the period, Owen Hart winning the European Championship in a controversial Goldust-disguise match, and the Undertaker vs. Kane feud continuing from the aftermath of the Montreal Screwjob. WrestleMania XIV drew 19,028 attendance and approximately 730,000 PPV buys (2.3 buyrate), the highest in WWF history at that point.

### WrestleMania XIV (March 29, 1998)

**Location:** FleetCenter, Boston, Massachusetts
**Attendance:** 19,028 (sell-out)
**Gate Revenue:** $1.03 million
**PPV Buyrate:** 2.3 (approximately 730,000 domestic buys) — highest in WWF history at that point
**Tagline:** "WrestleMania dX raided"
**Sponsor:** M&M's
**Commentary:** Jim Ross & Jerry Lawler

**Results:**
1. LOD 2000 (Hawk & Animal) (with Sunny) won by last eliminating The Midnight Express (Bodacious Bart & Bombastic Bob) (with Jim Cornette) — 15-Team Tag Team Battle Royal for #1 Contendership to WWF Tag Team Championship (8:19) [Pre-show/dark match]
2. Taka Michinoku (c) defeated Aguila — WWF Light Heavyweight Championship (5:57)
3. **Triple H (c) (with Chyna) defeated Owen Hart** — WWF European Championship (11:29) [Chyna was handcuffed to Sgt. Slaughter]
4. Marc Mero & Sable defeated The Artist Formerly Known as Goldust & Luna Vachon — Mixed Tag Team Match (9:11) [NOTE: Some sources list Mero & Sable as winners; other sources list Goldust & Luna as winners. Wikipedia main card lists Mero & Sable winning (9:11). Grokipedia lists Goldust & Luna winning. Wikipedia article lists Mero & Sable. [FLAG]]
5. **The Rock (c) (with Nation of Domination) defeated Ken Shamrock by disqualification** — WWF Intercontinental Championship (4:49) [Shamrock applied ankle lock, Rock submitted, but Shamrock refused to release hold; referee reversed decision to DQ; Rock retains. Had Rock been DQ'd, he would have lost the title. Gennifer Flowers was special guest ring announcer.]
6. **Cactus Jack & Chainsaw Charlie defeated The New Age Outlaws (Road Dogg & Billy Gunn) (c)** — WWF Tag Team Championship — Dumpster Match (10:01)
7. **The Undertaker defeated Kane (with Paul Bearer)** (17:05) [Undertaker won with Tombstone Piledriver; extended WrestleMania streak to 6-0]
8. **"Stone Cold" Steve Austin defeated Shawn Michaels (c) (with Triple H & Chyna)** — WWF Championship — with Mike Tyson as Special Outside Enforcer (20:02/20:08) [Mike Tyson turned on DX, punched Michaels, counted the pin after Austin hit the Stunner]

**WrestleMania XIV Main Event Details:**
- Austin won by pinfall after Stone Cold Stunner
- Referee Mike Chioda was knocked unconscious
- Mike Tyson made the three-count
- After the match, Tyson punched Shawn Michaels and raised Austin's hand
- This signalled the changing of the guard and the beginning of the Attitude Era
- Shawn Michaels wrestled the entire match with legitimate torn discs in his back
- This was Michaels' last match for four years (returned at SummerSlam 2002)
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WrestleMania_XIV

## Injuries / Backstage Incidents / Controversies

### Shawn Michaels' Legitimate Back Injury

**Incident:** Shawn Michaels suffered serious back injury during casket match at Royal Rumble

**Details:**
- Date: January 18, 1998 (Royal Rumble, San Jose)
- During the casket match with The Undertaker, Undertaker hit Michaels with a back-body drop to the outside
- Michaels landed on his lower back against the casket
- Suffered two herniated discs and crushed a third disc completely
- Despite the injury, Michaels continued to wrestle, including WrestleMania XIV (March 29)
- After WrestleMania, Michaels went into retirement due to the injury (legitimate)
- Underwent back surgery in January 1999
- Also struggled with pain pill addiction during this period
- Did not return to in-ring competition until SummerSlam 2002 (four years later)
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WrestleMania_XIV

### Cactus Jack & Chainsaw Charlie Dumpster Incident

**Incident:** New Age Outlaws pushed dumpster with Cactus Jack and Chainsaw Charlie inside off the stage

**Details:**
- Date: February 2, 1998 (Raw #245, Indianapolis)
- After their hardcore "King of Hardcore" match, Cactus Jack piledrived Chainsaw Charlie inside a dumpster
- New Age Outlaws locked the lid, tied it shut, and pushed the dumpster off the stage (approximately 10 feet)
- Both Foley and Funk sold the injury convincingly; the rest of the show was restructured around their "hospitalization"
- Both returned later in the show in hospital gowns
- This was one of the most memorable angles of early 1998
- **Source:** https://www.wrestlecrap.com/art-is-war/wwf-raw-245/

### Mike Tyson / Steve Austin Brawl

**Incident:** Steve Austin and Mike Tyson engaged in a massive physical confrontation on Raw

**Details:**
- Date: January 19, 1998 (Raw #243, Fresno, CA)
- Vince McMahon introduced Mike Tyson as a major WWF celebrity attraction
- Steve Austin interrupted, gave Tyson the middle finger
- Tyson shoved Austin; massive brawl ensued
- Vince McMahon was furious at Austin for "ruining" his announcement
- The altercation generated worldwide headlines
- This set up Tyson's role as special enforcer at WrestleMania XIV
- **Source:** https://uproxx.com/prowrestling/wwe-raw-best-and-worst-of-january-19-1998/

### Kane Chokeslamming Undertaker into Casket / Setting Fire

**Incident:** Kane betrayed The Undertaker at Royal Rumble

**Details:**
- Date: January 18, 1998 (Royal Rumble)
- During the casket match between Undertaker and Shawn Michaels, Kane chokeslammed Undertaker into the casket
- After the match, Kane and Paul Bearer locked Undertaker in the casket and set it on fire
- This was the culmination of months of Kane terrorizing Undertaker
- Set up the Undertaker vs. Kane match at WrestleMania XIV
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Rumble_(1998)

### Owen Hart European Title Win via Goldust Disguise

**Incident:** Owen Hart won European Championship from a disguised Goldust posing as Triple H

**Details:**
- Date: January 26, 1998 (Raw #244, Davis, CA)
- Triple H was "injured" (kayfabe fractured kneecap, which was actually legitimate)
- Goldust disguised himself as Triple H and came to the ring with the belt and Luna Vachon as Chyna
- Hart won the match, and Commissioner Sgt. Slaughter ruled the disguise was convincing enough to uphold the title change
- This was one of the most unusual and controversial title changes in WWF history
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Owen_Hart

### Triple H Regaining European Title via Chyna Interference

**Incident:** Triple H won back European Championship from Owen Hart with help from Chyna

**Details:**
- Date: March 16, 1998 (Raw #251, Phoenix, AZ)
- Hart had a kayfabe ankle injury from a match with Barry Windham
- While Hart was at commentary, Triple H lured him into an impromptu title match
- Chyna (who was supposed to be handcuffed to Sgt. Slaughter) struck Hart's knee with a baseball bat
- Triple H applied a reverse ankle lock to the injured ankle to win
- Highly controversial finish

## Game Effect Suggestions

### Event Timing Corrections
- **January 5:** NWA stable formed; Jeff Jarrett wins NWA North American title (Raw)
- **January 18:** Royal Rumble (San Jose) — Austin wins RR; Michaels retains WWF title in casket match
- **January 19:** Mike Tyson debuts; Austin/Tyson brawl (Raw)
- **January 26:** Owen Hart wins European title via Goldust disguise (Raw)
- **February 2:** Dumpster angle — Cactus Jack & Chainsaw Charlie pushed off stage (Raw)
- **February 15:** No Way Out of Texas (Houston)
- **March 2:** Mike Tyson joins DX (Raw)
- **March 16:** Triple H regains European title from Owen Hart (Raw)
- **March 29:** WrestleMania XIV (Boston) — Austin wins WWF Championship; multiple title changes
- **March 30:** New DX formed; tag titles restored to NAO (Raw, Albany)

### Title Lineage Adjustments
- **World Title:** Shawn Michaels → **Steve Austin** (Mar 29, WrestleMania XIV)
- **IC Title:** The Rock retains throughout (Jan 1 RR DQ, Mar 29 WM14 DQ)
- **European Title:** Triple H → **Owen Hart** (Jan 26, Raw) → **Triple H** (Mar 16, Raw)
- **Tag Titles:** New Age Outlaws → **Cactus Jack & Chainsaw Charlie** (Mar 29, WM14) → **New Age Outlaws** (Mar 30, Raw)
- **Light Heavyweight:** Taka Michinoku (c) throughout
- **NWA North American:** Vacated → **Jeff Jarrett** (Jan 5, Raw)

### Faction/Team Changes
- **D-Generation X (First Incarnation):** Ended at WrestleMania XIV when Tyson betrayed DX; Shawn Michaels expelled March 30
- **D-Generation X (Second Incarnation / "DX Army"):** Formed March 30 (Triple H, Chyna, X-Pac, New Age Outlaws)
- **NWA (Jim Cornette's faction):** Formed January 5; Jeff Jarrett departed March 2; Midnight Express joined
- **Nation of Domination:** Breaking up during this period; effectively dissolved March 30 when Faarooq walked on Rock
- **LOD 2000:** Reunited with Sunny as manager; won tag team battle royal at WrestleMania XIV
- **Brothers of Destruction:** Undertaker vs. Kane feud; Undertaker wins at WrestleMania XIV

### Injury/Morale/Business Effect
- **Shawn Michaels back injury:** CRITICAL — legitimate, forced retirement for 4 years; wrestled WrestleMania XIV while injured
- **Mike Tyson appearance:** MASSIVE POSITIVE — drew huge mainstream attention; WrestleMania XIV had highest buys ever (730,000)

## Conclusion

The January-March 1998 period marked the definitive beginning of the Attitude Era and the changing of the guard in WWF. Steve Austin's victory over Shawn Michaels at WrestleMania XIV, aided by Mike Tyson's betrayal of DX, signalled the transition from the New Generation to the Attitude Era. Shawn Michaels' legitimate back injury would keep him out of action for four years. The period also saw the formation of the new DX Army, the continued rise of The Rock, the breakdown of the Nation of Domination, and the establishment of Austin as the company's top star. WrestleMania XIV was a massive commercial success, drawing the highest PPV buys in WWF history. The Monday Night Wars were shifting in WWF's favour, with Raw beginning to close the gap with WCW Nitro.
