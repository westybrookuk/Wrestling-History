# WWF April-June 1998 Research Report

**Period Covered:** April 1, 1998 - June 30, 1998

## Executive Summary

The April-June 1998 period was one of the most iconic in WWF history, featuring some of the most legendary moments in professional wrestling. The period featured three major PPV events: Unforgiven: In Your House (April 26, Greensboro), Over the Edge: In Your House (May 31, Milwaukee), and King of the Ring (June 28, Pittsburgh). The defining moments included: the debut of Dude Love as Vince McMahon's "corporate champion," the legendary Hell in a Cell match between The Undertaker and Mankind at King of the Ring (in which Undertaker threw Mankind off the 16-foot cell and chokeslammed him through the roof — widely considered the most famous match in wrestling history), Kane winning the WWF Championship from Steve Austin in a First Blood match at King of the Ring (only to lose it back the next night on Raw), Ken Shamrock winning the 1998 King of the Ring tournament, Kane unmasking Vader at Over the Edge, and WWF Raw beating WCW Nitro in the ratings for the first time in 83 weeks on April 13, 1998. The period also saw the continued DX vs. Nation of Domination feud, the Austin vs. Dude Love rivalry, and the formation of the Brothers of Destruction tag team (Kane & Mankind).

## Major PPV Events

### Unforgiven: In Your House (April 26, 1998)

**Location:** Greensboro Coliseum, Greensboro, North Carolina
**Attendance:** 21,427 (new arena record)
**PPV Buyrate:** 300,000 (0.85 buyrate)
**Notes:** First WWF PPV to use the new "scratch" logo

**Results:**
1. Faarooq, Ken Shamrock & Steve Blackman defeated The Nation of Domination (The Rock, D'Lo Brown & Mark Henry) (with Kama Mustafa) (13:07/13:32) [Faarooq pinned The Rock; post-match, The Rock's Nation allies turned on Faarooq, and The Rock took over leadership of the Nation]
2. Triple H (c) (with Chyna) defeated Owen Hart — WWF European Championship (12:26/12:38) [Chyna was suspended above the ring in a cage; Chyna escaped, X-Pac interfered with fire extinguisher, Triple H retained]
3. The New Midnight Express (Bodacious Bart & Bombastic Bob) (c) (with Jim Cornette) defeated The Rock 'n' Roll Express (Ricky Morton & Robert Gibson) — NWA World Tag Team Championship (7:12/7:20)
4. Luna Vachon (with The Artist Formerly Known as Goldust) defeated Sable — Evening Gown Match (2:35/2:50) [Post-match, Sable disrobed Luna under the ring]
5. The New Age Outlaws (Road Dogg & Billy Gunn) (c) defeated LOD 2000 (Hawk & Animal) (with Sunny) — WWF Tag Team Championship (12:13/12:14) [Controversial finish — referee counted both wrestlers' shoulders]
6. **The Undertaker defeated Kane (with Paul Bearer) — Inferno Match** (15:57/16:00) [Undertaker set Kane's arm on fire; Vader interfered post-match, attacking Kane]
7. **Dude Love defeated Steve Austin (c) by disqualification** — WWF Championship (18:49/18:52) [Austin hit Vince McMahon with a chair; Gerald Brisco informed Finkel that Austin was disqualified; title does NOT change hands]

**Key Notes:**
- The Nation of Domination effectively broke up after this event when The Rock's allies turned on Faarooq
- The main event finish saw Austin count his own pinfall after stunning Dude Love and moving McMahon's unconscious hand to count three, but Gerald Brisco ruled the DQ
- Jeff Jarrett performed a song with Sawyer Brown on the card
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unforgiven:_In_Your_House

### Over the Edge: In Your House (May 31, 1998)

**Location:** Wisconsin Center Arena, Milwaukee, Wisconsin
**Attendance:** 9,822
**PPV Buyrate:** Not specified in sources

**Results:**
1. LOD 2000 (Animal & Hawk) (with Droz & Sunny) defeated The Disciples of Apocalypse (8-Ball & Skull) (with Chainz) (9:57)
2. Jeff Jarrett (with Tennessee Lee) defeated Steve Blackman (10:15/10:18) [Tennessee Lee interfered with arnis stick]
3. **Marc Mero defeated Sable** (0:30) [Stipulation: Loser leaves WWF television; Mero won with low blow]
4. Kaientai (Dick Togo, Men's Teioh & Sho Funaki) (with Yamaguchi-san) defeated Justin Bradshaw & Taka Michinoku — Handicap Match (9:52/9:54)
5. **The Rock (c) defeated Faarooq** — WWF Intercontinental Championship (5:02/5:07) [Rock debuted his first solo entrance theme at this event]
6. **Kane (with Paul Bearer) defeated Vader** — Mask vs. Mask Match (7:13/7:20) [Kane won with Tombstone Piledriver; Vader unmasked, revealing scarred face; post-match interview, Vader called himself "a big fat piece of s**t" and said "Vader time may be over"]
7. The Nation of Domination (D'Lo Brown, Kama Mustafa & Owen Hart) (with Mark Henry) defeated D-Generation X (Billy Gunn, Road Dogg & Triple H) (with Chyna & X-Pac) — Six-Man Tag Team Match (18:33/18:40) [Owen pinned Triple H with Pedigree]
8. **"Stone Cold" Steve Austin (c) defeated Dude Love** — WWF Championship — Falls Count Anywhere Match with Mr. McMahon as Special Guest Referee (22:17/22:27) [Pat Patterson was special guest ring announcer; Gerald Brisco was special guest timekeeper; Undertaker was special guest enforcer; Austin pinned Love after Stunner, then moved McMahon's unconscious hand to count the pin; Undertaker ordered the bell to ring]

**Key Notes:**
- This was Vader's last WWF TV appearance for 14 years (until 2012)
- The Rock debuted his first solo entrance theme at this event
- Sable was written off television after losing to Mero
- Austin vs. McMahon stipulation: If Austin put his hands on McMahon, he would be stripped of the title
- After this event, Dude Love formed an alliance with McMahon
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Over_the_Edge:_In_Your_House

### King of the Ring (June 28, 1998)

**Location:** Civic Arena, Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania
**Attendance:** 17,087
**PPV Buyrate:** Not specified in sources

**Results:**
1. The Headbangers (Mosh & Thrasher) & Taka Michinoku defeated Kaientai (Funaki, Men's Teioh & Dick Togo) (6:41/6:44)
2. **Ken Shamrock defeated Jeff Jarrett (with Tennessee Lee)** — KOTR Semi-Final (5:29) [Submission]
3. **The Rock defeated Dan Severn** — KOTR Semi-Final (4:25) [Pinfall]
4. Too Much (Brian Christopher & Scott Taylor) defeated Al Snow & Head — Tag Team Match with Jerry Lawler as Special Guest Referee (8:26/8:27)
5. X-Pac (with Chyna) defeated Owen Hart (8:30)
6. **The New Age Outlaws (Road Dogg & Billy Gunn) (c) (with Chyna) defeated The New Midnight Express (Bodacious Bart & Bombastic Bob) (with Jim Cornette)** — WWF Tag Team Championship (9:34/9:54)
7. **Ken Shamrock defeated The Rock** — KOTR Final (14:09) [Submission with Ankle Lock; Triple H on commentary made famous "Testes…Testes…1…2…3?" comment]
8. **The Undertaker defeated Mankind — Hell in a Cell Match** (16:00/17:38) [THE MOST FAMOUS HELL IN A CELL MATCH IN HISTORY — Undertaker threw Mankind off the 16-foot cell through the Spanish announce table; later chokeslammed Mankind through the roof of the cell; Mankind legitimately knocked unconscious; Mankind spread thumbtacks but Undertaker won with Chokeslam and Tombstone onto thumbtacks]
9. **Kane (with Paul Bearer) defeated "Stone Cold" Steve Austin (c)** — WWF Championship — First Blood Match (14:52/15:58) [Had Kane lost, he would have set himself on fire. Mankind interfered, Undertaker made save but hit Austin with chair instead (supposedly accidental). Kane won when referee saw Austin bleeding.]

**King of the Ring Tournament Bracket:**
- Qualifiers (TV): Ken Shamrock def. Kama Mustafa; Jeff Jarrett def. Faarooq; The Rock def. Triple H; Dan Severn def. Owen Hart
- Quarterfinals (TV, Jun 22): Ken Shamrock def. Mark Henry; Jeff Jarrett def. Marc Mero
- Semifinals (PPV): Ken Shamrock def. Jeff Jarrett; The Rock def. Dan Severn
- Final (PPV): Ken Shamrock def. The Rock (14:09, submission)

**Key Notes:**
- The Hell in a Cell match between Undertaker and Mankind is widely considered the most famous match in professional wrestling history
- Mankind (Mick Foley) was thrown off the 16-foot cell through the Spanish announce table, then chokeslammed through the roof of the cell, landing legitimately unconscious
- The footage of the fall remains one of the most viewed videos in wrestling history
- Kane won his FIRST AND ONLY WWF Championship (reigned less than one day)
- Undertaker accidentally hit Austin with a chair during the main event interference, causing Austin to bleed and Kane to win
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_of_the_Ring_(1998)

## Injuries / Backstage Incidents / Controversies

### The Undertaker Throws Mankind Off Hell in a Cell

**Incident:** Undertaker threw Mankind off the 16-foot Hell in a Cell structure

**Details:**
- Date: June 28, 1998 (King of the Ring, Pittsburgh)
- Less than two minutes into the match, Undertaker threw Mankind from the top of the cell through the Spanish announce table
- Midway through, Undertaker chokeslammed Mankind through the ROOF of the cell
- Mankind was legitimately knocked unconscious from the chokeslam through the roof
- Mankind spread thumbtacks in the ring but was eventually defeated
- This is widely considered the most famous match in professional wrestling history
- Mick Foley has stated he doesn't remember most of the match, having watched it on video
- The footage remains one of the most viewed videos in wrestling history
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_of_the_Ring_(1998)

### Kane Unmasks Vader

**Incident:** Kane defeated Vader in Mask vs. Mask match, forcing unmasking

**Details:**
- Date: May 31, 1998 (Over the Edge, Milwaukee)
- Kane won with Tombstone Piledriver
- Vader was forced to unmask, revealing his scarred face
- Post-match ringside interview: Vader called himself "a big fat piece of s**t" and said "Vader time may be over"
- This was Vader's last WWF TV appearance for 14 years
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Over_the_Edge:_In_Your_House

### Kane Wins and Immediately Loses WWF Championship

**Incident:** Kane won WWF Title at King of the Ring, lost it back next night

**Details:**
- Won: June 28, 1998 (King of the Ring) — First Blood match
- Lost: June 29, 1998 (Raw) — defeated by Austin
- Kane's reign lasted less than one day
- This remains the shortest WWF Championship reign in history
- Undertaker's interference (accidental chair shot on Austin) cost Austin the title
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_of_the_Ring_(1998)

### WWF Raw Beats WCW Nitro in Ratings

**Incident:** WWF Raw beat WCW Nitro in the ratings for the first time in 83 weeks

**Details:**
- Date: April 13, 1998 (Raw)
- This was the first time Raw had beaten Nitro since before WrestleMania XIV
- The momentum from WrestleMania XIV and the Attitude Era content was paying off
- After this, WWF would steadily gain on WCW and eventually dominate
- **Source:** https://wrestlingrecaps.com/2010/08/31/wwf-unforgiven-4261998/

### Austin vs. Dude Love Feud with McMahon Stipulations

**Incident:** Vince McMahon created stipulations to prevent Austin from hitting him

**Details:**
- At Over the Edge (May 31): If Austin put his hands on McMahon, he would be automatically stripped of the championship
- McMahon was special guest referee, Pat Patterson was ring announcer, Gerald Brisco was timekeeper
- Undertaker was special guest enforcer
- Despite these restrictions, Austin won when he stunned Love, moved McMahon's unconscious hand to count the pin, and Undertaker ordered the bell to ring

## Game Effect Suggestions

### Event Timing Corrections
- **April 13:** Raw beats Nitro for first time in 83 weeks; Dude Love debuts
- **April 26:** Unforgiven (Greensboro) — 21,427 attendance, 300,000 buys
- **May 4:** Dude Love defeats Terry Funk, forms alliance with McMahon (Raw)
- **May 31:** Over the Edge (Milwaukee) — 9,822 attendance; Kane unmasking Vader; Rock debuts solo theme
- **June 1:** Kane defeats Undertaker to become #1 contender (Raw)
- **June 22:** KOTR quarterfinals on Raw
- **June 28:** King of the Ring (Pittsburgh) — 17,087 attendance; Hell in a Cell; Kane wins WWF Title
- **June 29:** Austin wins WWF Title back from Kane (Raw)

### Title Lineage Adjustments
- **World Title:** Austin (c) → **Kane** (Jun 28, KOTR) → **Austin** (Jun 29, Raw) — Kane's reign lasted less than one day; title renamed "WWF Championship"
- **IC Title:** The Rock retains throughout — defeated Faarooq at Over the Edge (May 31)
- **European Title:** Triple H retains throughout — defeated Owen Hart at Unforgiven (Apr 26)
- **Tag Titles:** New Age Outlaws retain throughout — defeated LOD 2000 at Unforgiven, New Midnight Express at KOTR
- **Light Heavyweight:** Taka Michinoku (c) throughout

### Faction/Team Changes
- **Nation of Domination:** Faarooq ousted as leader; The Rock takes over (April 26, Unforgiven)
- **D-Generation X:** Continued as top heel stable (Triple H, Chyna, X-Pac, New Age Outlaws)
- **DX vs. Nation:** Major faction war throughout this period, escalating to Over the Edge and beyond
- **Dude Love:** Debuted April 13 as Vince McMahon's "corporate champion"; aligned with McMahon
- **Kane & Mankind:** Tag team formed after King of the Ring (June 28); would win tag titles in July
- **NWA (Cornette's faction):** Continued with New Midnight Express; lost NWA World Tag Team titles at King of the Ring

### Injury/Morale/Business Effect
- **Hell in a Cell match:** ICONIC — Undertaker vs. Mankind; one of the most famous moments in wrestling history
- **Kane wins WWF Title:** SHOCKING — first and only WWF Championship; lasted less than one day
- **Vader unmasking/departure:** END OF AN ERA — last WWF TV appearance for 14 years
- **WWF beats Nitro:** TRANSFORMATIVE — first time in 83 weeks; beginning of WWF's ratings dominance
- **Unforgiven attendance:** 21,427 — new Greensboro Coliseum record; 300,000 buys (0.85 buyrate)
- **Nation breakup:** POSITIVE for Rock — took over leadership, began path to megastardom
- **Dude Love debut:** MIXED — memorable character but mostly used as McMahon's pawn
- **Ken Shamrock wins KOTR:** POSITIVE — solidified as top babyface; set up major Rock/Shamrock feud

### Potential Booking Issues
- Hell in a Cell match is MUST-PRESERVE as one of wrestling's most iconic moments
- Kane's one-day WWF Title reign should be tracked accurately (Jun 28 → Jun 29)
- Dude Love's alliance with McMahon is important for the Corporation storyline that follows
- The Nation of Domination breakup under Faarooq and The Rock's takeover should be gradual
- Vader's departure after unmasking should be noted as effectively permanent (14 years)
- The Rock's solo entrance theme debut at Over the Edge is a character milestone

## Conflicts with Previous Reports

### WWF January-March 1998 Report Context
- **World Title:** The Jan-Mar 1998 report showed Austin winning from Michaels at WrestleMania XIV. This report shows Austin retaining at Unforgiven (Apr 26, DQ loss to Dude Love — title doesn't change) and Over the Edge (May 31), then losing to Kane at KOTR (Jun 28) and winning it back on Raw (Jun 29).
- **IC Title:** The Jan-Mar 1998 report showed The Rock retaining throughout. This report confirms Rock retains at Over the Edge (May 31) against Faarooq.
- **European Title:** The Jan-Mar 1998 report showed Triple H winning from Owen Hart on March 16. This report shows Triple H retaining at Unforgiven (Apr 26) against Owen Hart.
- **Tag Titles:** The Jan-Mar 1998 report showed NAO winning on March 30. This report shows NAO retaining at Unforgiven (Apr 26) against LOD 2000, and at KOTR (Jun 28) against New Midnight Express.
- **Nation of Domination:** The Jan-Mar 1998 report showed Nation breaking up at WrestleMania aftermath. This report shows the formal breakup at Unforgiven (Apr 26) when Faarooq was ousted.
- **Kane:** The Jan-Mar 1998 report showed Kane losing to Undertaker at WrestleMania XIV. This report shows Kane losing the Inferno Match at Unforgiven (Apr 26), defeating Vader at Over the Edge (May 31), and winning the WWF Title at KOTR (Jun 28).

## Conclusion

The April-June 1998 period was arguably the most iconic quarter in WWF history. The legendary Hell in a Cell match between Undertaker and Mankind at King of the Ring produced the most famous single moment in professional wrestling. Kane's shock WWF Title win (lasting less than one day) and Ken Shamrock's King of the Ring victory were major highlights. Dude Love's debut as Vince McMahon's "corporate champion" set up months of Austin vs. McMahon storyline. The Nation of Domination formally broke up at Unforgiven when The Rock took over leadership, setting the stage for his megastardom. Kane's unmasking of Vader effectively ended Vader's WWF career for 14 years. WWF Raw beat WCW Nitro in the ratings for the first time in 83 weeks on April 13, marking the beginning of WWF's ratings dominance. Unforgiven drew 21,427 fans in Greensboro and 300,000 buys, confirming WWF's growing momentum in the Monday Night Wars.
