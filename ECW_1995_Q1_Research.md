### Eddie Gilbert Death — February 18, 1995

- **Date:** February 18, 1995
- **Type:** Death
- **People involved:** Eddie Gilbert ("Hot Stuff" Eddie Gilbert)
- **What happened:** Eddie Gilbert was found dead in his apartment in Isla Verde, Puerto Rico. He was 33 years old. The official cause of death was a heart attack. His father Tommy Gilbert stated that injuries to Eddie's chest and heart muscle from a serious car crash in 1983 may have been a factor, and Eddie's alleged use of painkillers since the accident could also have contributed to his heart condition.
- **What was confirmed:** Gilbert died of a heart attack on February 18, 1995, in Puerto Rico. He had wrestled his last match on February 17, 1995, in Humacao, Puerto Rico.
- **What is disputed:** The exact contributing factors to his heart condition (painkiller use vs. prior injuries).
- **Immediate creative/business impact:** ECW ran a tribute to Eddie Gilbert on the February 21, 1995 episode of Hardcore TV (Episode #95).
- **Long-term impact:** Gilbert was remembered as one of the most innovative and influential wrestlers/bookers in professional wrestling history. The Eddie Gilbert Memorial Brawl was held annually from 1996 to 1999.

### Triple Threat — Formed Early 1995

- **Members:** Shane Douglas (leader), Chris Benoit, Dean Malenko
- **Alignment:** Heel
- **Manager/valet:** None initially
- **Formation:** Early 1995
- **Storyline trigger:** Shane Douglas, as ECW World Heavyweight Champion, recruited technical specialists Chris Benoit and Dean Malenko to form ECW's elite force.
