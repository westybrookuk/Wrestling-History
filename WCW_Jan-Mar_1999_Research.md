# WCW January-March 1999 Research Report

**Date Range:** January 1, 1999 - March 31, 1999

## Executive Summary

The first quarter of 1999 marked one of the most infamous periods in WCW history, beginning with the infamous "Fingerpoke of Doom" at the Georgia Dome on January 4, 1999, when Hulk Hogan defeated Kevin Nash with a single finger poke to win the WCW World Heavyweight Championship. This match, which was meant to reunite the nWo factions, instead alienated fans and led to a significant ratings drop. The period also saw Ric Flair win the World Title and become WCW President for life at Uncensored, multiple title changes, and the continued decline of WCW's creative direction. The Four Horsemen reformation, the nWo's internal conflicts, and the mishandling of top talent like Goldberg characterized this chaotic quarter.

## Title Changes

### WCW World Heavyweight Championship

**January 4, 1999 - Kevin Nash to Hollywood Hogan**
- **Event:** WCW Monday Nitro (Georgia Dome, Atlanta, GA)
- **Match Type:** Singles match
- **Result:** Hollywood Hogan defeated Kevin Nash
- **Notes:** The infamous "Fingerpoke of Doom" - Nash lay down after one finger poke, leading to widespread fan outrage. This reunited the nWo factions but was widely criticized as one of the worst booking decisions in wrestling history.
- **Attendance:** 38,809 (34,788 paid)
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fingerpoke_of_Doom

**March 14, 1999 - Hollywood Hogan to Ric Flair**
- **Event:** WCW Uncensored 1999
- **Match Type:** First Blood Barbed Wire Steel Cage match
- **Stipulation:** Winner gains permanent control of WCW
- **Result:** Ric Flair defeated Hollywood Hogan (14:19)
- **Notes:** Flair won both the championship and the presidency of WCW in this extreme match
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uncensored_(1999)

## Major PPV Events

### SuperBrawl IX (February 21, 1999)

**Location:** Oakland Arena, Oakland, California  
**Attendance:** 15,880

**Results:**
1. Booker T defeated Disco Inferno (9:19)
2. Chris Jericho (w/ Ralphus) defeated Perry Saturn by countout (11:17)
3. Billy Kidman (c) defeated Chavo Guerrero Jr. to retain WCW Cruiserweight Championship (8:26)
4. Chris Benoit & Dean Malenko defeated Barry Windham & Curt Hennig by submission (20:00) - Tag Team Tournament Final
5. Barry Windham & Curt Hennig defeated Chris Benoit & Dean Malenko (0:37) - Won WCW World Tag Team Championship in sudden death rematch
6. The Outsiders (Kevin Nash & Scott Hall) (w/ Lex Luger & Miss Elizabeth) defeated Konnan & Rey Misterio Jr. in Hair vs. Mask match (11:00) - Rey lost his mask
7. Scott Steiner (c) defeated Diamond Dallas Page to retain WCW World Television Championship (13:53)
8. Scott Hall (w/ Disco Inferno) defeated Roddy Piper (c) to win WCW United States Heavyweight Championship (8:19)
9. Goldberg defeated Bam Bam Bigelow (11:39)
10. Hollywood Hogan (c) defeated Ric Flair to retain WCW World Heavyweight Championship (12:00)

**Key Notes:**
- Rey Mysterio Jr. lost his mask in the Hair vs. Mask match
- The tag team title situation was controversial - Benoit & Malenko won the tournament but lost the titles in an immediate rematch
- David Flair was revealed as the masked man who interfered, stunning his father Ric Flair
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SuperBrawl_IX

### Uncensored 1999 (March 14, 1999)

**Location:** Louisville, Kentucky (likely Freedom Hall)

**Results:**
1. Billy Kidman (c) defeated Mikey Whipwreck to retain WCW Cruiserweight Championship (14:57)
2. Stevie Ray defeated Vincent in Harlem Street Fight for leadership of nWo Black and White (6:30)
3. Kevin Nash (w/ Lex Luger & Miss Elizabeth) defeated Rey Misterio Jr. (6:19)
4. Jerry Flynn defeated Ernest Miller & Sonny Onoo in Handicap match (7:08)
5. Hak defeated Bam Bam Bigelow & Raven in Falls Count Anywhere match (14:29)
6. Chris Benoit & Dean Malenko defeated Curt Hennig & Barry Windham (c) in Lumberjack match to win WCW World Tag Team Championship (15:58)
7. Perry Saturn defeated Chris Jericho in Dog Collar match (11:50)
8. Booker T defeated Scott Steiner (c) (w/ Buff Bagwell) to win WCW World Television Championship (13:30)
9. Ric Flair defeated Hollywood Hogan (c) in First Blood Barbed Wire Steel Cage match to win WCW World Heavyweight Championship and permanent control of WCW (14:19)

**Key Notes:**
- Ric Flair won both the World Championship and the presidency of WCW
- Stevie Ray won leadership of the nWo Black and White faction
- Multiple titles changed hands on one card
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uncensored_(1999)

## Injuries / Backstage Incidents / Controversies

### The Fingerpoke of Doom (January 4, 1999)

**Incident:** Hulk Hogan defeated Kevin Nash with a single finger poke in a match that was meant to reunite the nWo factions

**Controversy:**
- The match was widely criticized as one of the worst booking decisions in wrestling history
- Fans felt cheated out of the expected Goldberg vs. Hogan main event
- Goldberg was legitimately injured or was being kept off TV, leading to fan outrage
- The match led to a significant ratings drop
- WWE refers to it as "one of the most scandalous title changes of all time"

**Impact:**
- Nearly 40,000 fans were cheated out of the expected Goldberg main event
- The "We want Sting!" chant dominated the crowd reaction
- Direct link between the title change and drop in ratings
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fingerpoke_of_Doom

### Rick Steiner's Injury

**Incident:** Rick Steiner sustained a legitimate injury in early January 1999

**Impact:**
- The WCW World Tag Team Championship was vacated on January 4, 1999
- This led to a tournament for the titles
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1999_in_professional_wrestling

### Scott Hall Stripped of US Title

**Incident:** Scott Hall was stripped of the WCW United States Heavyweight Championship on March 18, 1999

**Reason:** WCW President Ric Flair stripped Hall of the title due to injury

## Conclusion

The January-March 1999 period was marked by some of WCW's worst creative decisions, beginning with the infamous Fingerpoke of Doom. While there were some positive moments like the Four Horsemen reformation and Booker T winning the TV title, the overall direction was chaotic and alienating to fans. The mishandling of Goldberg, the Fingerpoke of Doom, and the confusing booking at SuperBrawl IX exemplified WCW's creative problems. Ric Flair winning both the World Title and the presidency at Uncensored was a significant moment, but the booking that followed was erratic. This period set the stage for WCW's continued decline in 1999.
