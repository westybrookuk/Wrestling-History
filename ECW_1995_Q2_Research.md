## 1. Title Changes

### ECW World Heavyweight Championship — Shane Douglas → The Sandman

- **Date:** April 15, 1995
- **Taping date:** April 15, 1995
- **Air date:** April 18, 1995 (Hardcore TV)
- **Championship:** ECW World Heavyweight Championship
- **Winner:** The Sandman
- **Loser:** Shane Douglas (c)
- **Show:** ECW Hostile City Showdown 1995
- **Venue:** ECW Arena, Philadelphia, PA
- **Attendance:** 1,150
- **Finish:** Woman struck Douglas in the knee with a Singapore cane while he was applying a crossface chickenwing to Sandman, enabling Sandman to pin Douglas and end his year-long title reign. Woman then reunited with Sandman, revealing her alliance with Douglas as a ruse.
- **Event type:** Arena supercard
- **Why it mattered:** This ended Shane Douglas's 385-day reign (the longest ECW World Heavyweight Championship reign at that time) and began The Sandman's second ECW World Heavyweight Championship reign. The double-cross reveal was one of ECW's most memorable storyline twists, as Woman had appeared to betray Sandman at Three Way Dance just one week earlier.

## 4. Factions, Tag Teams, and Major Character Changes

### Woman's Double-Cross / Reunion with Sandman — April 8-15, 1995

- **Members:** Woman (Nancy Benoit/Sullivan), The Sandman, Shane Douglas
- **Alignment:** Heel (apparently), then revealed as face ruse
- **Manager/valet:** Woman
- **Formation/breakup:** Woman appeared to betray The Sandman at Three Way Dance (April 8) by aligning with Shane Douglas, then revealed it was a ruse at Hostile City Showdown (April 15) by helping Sandman win the ECW World Heavyweight Championship
- **Storyline trigger:** Woman's apparent betrayal of Sandman at Three Way Dance was revealed to be a con job to help Sandman win the title
- **Real-life context:** Woman was married to Kevin Sullivan at the time, who was also The Sandman's storyline brother. The double-cross was a brilliant piece of storytelling that confused both the audience and Shane Douglas.
- **Why it mattered:** This was one of ECW's most memorable storyline twists. The audience and Shane Douglas were completely fooled into thinking Woman had betrayed The Sandman, only for her to help Sandman win the championship at Hostile City Showdown. This began The Sandman's second ECW World Heavyweight Championship reign and solidified Woman as his loyal valet.

### Cactus Jack vs. The Sandman Feud — April-June 1995

- **Members:** Cactus Jack vs. The Sandman (with Woman)
- **Alignment:** Cactus Jack (face), The Sandman (face with heel manager valet)
- **Manager/valet:** Woman (Sandman), Bill Alfonso (referee, turned heel June 17)
- **Formation or breakup:** Ongoing feud from early 1995
