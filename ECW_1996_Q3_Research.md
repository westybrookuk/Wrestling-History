### ECW World Tag Team Championship — The Eliminators → The Gangstas

**Title Change: The Eliminators (Perry Saturn & John Kronus) → The Gangstas (New Jack & Mustafa Saed)**

- **Date:** August 3, 1996
- **Taping date:** August 3, 1996
- **Air date:** Aired on Hardcore TV
- **Championship:** ECW World Tag Team Championship
- **Winners:** The Gangstas (New Jack & Mustafa Saed)
- **Losers:** The Eliminators (Perry Saturn & John Kronus) (c), The Bruise Brothers (Ron & Don Harris), Samoan Gangsta Party (Mack Daddy Kane & Sammy Silk)
- **Show:** ECW The Doctor Is In
- **Venue:** ECW Arena, Philadelphia, PA
- **Attendance:** 1,400-1,500
- **Finish:** The Gangstas won in a Four-Way Elimination Match (11:01). The Samoan Gangsta Party were the first team eliminated after New Jack performed a "187" on Sammy Silk and pinned him. The Bruise Brothers were the second team eliminated after The Eliminators performed Total Elimination on one of the Brothers and pinned him. The Gangstas won the match by pinfall after another "187" from New Jack to John Kronus.
- **Event type:** Arena supercard
- **Why it mattered:** This marked The Gangstas winning the ECW World Tag Team Championship from The Eliminators, beginning their reign as tag team champions. This was a major title change that shifted the tag team division landscape.

### ECW The Doctor Is In — August 3, 1996

- **Date:** August 3, 1996
- **Venue:** ECW Arena
- **City:** Philadelphia, PA
- **Attendance:** 1,400-1,500
- **Main matches:**
  - **ECW World Heavyweight Championship:** Stevie Richards (with The Blue Meanie, Don E. Allen, Lori Fullington, Raven, Super Nova, and Tyler Fullington) defeated The Sandman (with Missy Hyatt) by pinfall (7:12). Stevie Richards was defending the ECW World Heavyweight Championship for Raven. Raven came in the last seconds of the match and made the pin.
  - **ECW World Television Championship:** Shane Douglas (with Francine) (c) defeated Pitbull #2 (with Pitbull #1) by pinfall (15:26). Pitbull #1 was wearing a neck brace after suffering a cervical fracture of his spine at Heat Wave when Douglas gave him a DDT onto the ECW World Television Championship. The match saw Pitbull #2 repeatedly target Douglas' neck in an attempt to avenge Pitbull #1. The match ended when a heavily bleeding Pitbull #2 attempted to give Douglas a Superbomb but was unable to deliver the move due to weakness from blood loss, enabling Douglas to counter and give him two belly-to-belly suplexes for a pinfall victory.
  - **ECW World Tag Team Championship:** The Gangstas (Mustafa and New Jack) defeated The Bruise Brothers (Don Bruise and Ron Bruise), The Eliminators (Kronus and Saturn) (c), and Samoan Gangsta Party (Mack Daddy Kane and Sammy Silk) in a Four-Way Elimination Match to win the ECW World Tag Team Championship (11:01)
  - Sabu defeated Rob Van Dam in a Stretcher Match (23:22)
  - 2 Cold Scorpio defeated Chris Jericho by pinfall (19:56). This was Jericho's final appearance with ECW before departing to join World Championship Wrestling.
  - Brian Lee and Taz (with Bill Alfonso and Team Taz) defeated Steve Williams and Tommy Dreamer (with Beulah McGillicutty) by pinfall (6:57)
  - Johnny Smith defeated Louie Spicolli by pinfall (5:39). After the match, the audience gave both men a standing ovation.
  - Mikey Whipwreck defeated Devon Storm (with Damien Kane and Lady Alexandra) by pinfall (12:25)
  - Axl Rotten vs. D-Von Dudley ended in a no contest. The match was never started after the Dudley Brothers interfered, leading Axl Rotten and D-Von Dudley to join forces against them.
- **Major finishes:** 
  - Stevie Richards retained world title for Raven vs. Sandman
  - Shane Douglas retained TV title vs. Pitbull #2
  - The Gangstas won tag titles from The Eliminators
  - Sabu defeated RVD in stretcher match
  - 2 Cold Scorpio defeated Chris Jericho (Jericho's final ECW match)
- **Important debuts/returns/exits:** 
  - **Chris Jericho's final ECW appearance** — Jericho departed for WCW after this event
  - **Steve Williams ECW appearance** — Williams made a notable appearance in ECW
- **Why it mattered:** This was a major ECW supercard featuring Chris Jericho's final ECW appearance before departing for WCW, The Gangstas winning the tag team titles, and the continuation of the Sabu vs. Rob Van Dam feud. The event showcased ECW's top talent and continued the ongoing storylines of mid-1996 ECW.
