# WWF July-September 1998 Research Report

**Period Covered:** July 1, 1998 - September 30, 1998

## Executive Summary

The July-September 1998 period was a transformative quarter in WWF history, featuring three major PPV events — Fully Loaded: In Your House (July 26, Fresno), SummerSlam (August 30, Madison Square Garden), and Breakdown: In Your House (September 27, Hamilton, Ontario) — and marked some of the most significant title changes and storyline developments of the Attitude Era. Key developments included: Triple H winning the Intercontinental Championship from The Rock in a legendary ladder match at SummerSlam, D'Lo Brown winning the European Championship from Triple H on Raw (July 20), Kane & Mankind winning the WWF Tag Team Championship twice (July 13 and August 10), the Austin vs. Undertaker rivalry culminating at SummerSlam, the WWF Championship being declared vacant after Kane and Undertaker simultaneously pinned Steve Austin at Breakdown, Mankind being fired by Vince McMahon and Steve Austin's zamboni incident, the early formation of the Corporation, and X-Pac winning the European title from D'Lo Brown (September 21). SummerSlam drew 21,588 at MSG with approximately 600,000 PPV buys (1.50-1.63 buyrate), confirming WWF's dominance over WCW in the Monday Night Wars. The period also saw the continued rise of Edge and Sable, the Owen Hart face turn, and Triple H's knee injury after SummerSlam leading to the IC title being vacated.

## Major PPV Events

### Fully Loaded: In Your House (July 26, 1998)

**Location:** Selland Arena, Fresno, California
**Attendance:** 9,855
**PPV Buyrate:** 305,000 (0.9 buyrate)

**Results:**
1. Val Venis defeated Jeff Jarrett (with Tennessee Lee) (7:45/7:50)
2. D'Lo Brown (with The Godfather) defeated X-Pac (with Chyna) (8:26)
3. Faarooq & Too Cold Scorpio defeated Justin Bradshaw & Terry Funk (6:49/6:51)
4. Mark Henry defeated Vader (5:03)
5. Disciples of Apocalypse (8-Ball & Skull) (with Paul Ellering) defeated LOD 2000 (Hawk & Animal) (8:50)
6. **Owen Hart defeated Ken Shamrock** — Dungeon Match with Dan Severn as Special Guest Referee (4:46) [Hart won by submission; Severn initially awarded victory to Shamrock but recovered and saw Shamrock was still in the hold, reversing the decision]
7. **The Rock (c) vs. Triple H (with Chyna) — DRAW** — WWF Intercontinental Championship — Two-out-of-Three-Falls Match (30:00) [1-0: Rock pins Triple H; 1-1: Triple H pins Rock (Chyna DDT on Rock onto chair); final fall: time limit expired after Triple H hit Pedigree; Rock retains via no contest]
8. Jacqueline defeated Sable by disqualification — Bikini Contest (Jerry Lawler as MC) [Sable won by audience applause after removing her top, revealing painted handprints on her breasts; Jacqueline attacked, leading to Sable's DQ retaliation]
9. **"Stone Cold" Steve Austin & The Undertaker defeated Kane & Mankind (c) (with Paul Bearer)** — WWF Tag Team Championship (18:08) [Undertaker pinned Kane after Tombstone Piledriver; Austin & Undertaker WON the tag titles]

**Key Notes:**
- The Rock vs. Triple H IC title match was originally supposed to be title vs. title, but Triple H had lost the European Championship to D'Lo Brown on Raw (July 20)
- The main event tag team match saw Austin and Undertaker win the WWF Tag Team Championship
- The Dungeon match featured early "cinematic wrestling" vibes with multiple camera angles
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fully_Loaded:_In_Your_House

### SummerSlam 1998 (August 30, 1998)

**Location:** Madison Square Garden, New York City, New York
**Attendance:** 21,588
**PPV Buyrate:** ~600,000 buys (1.50-1.63 buyrate)
**Tagline:** "Highway to Hell"
**Sponsor:** Stridex
**Theme Song:** "Highway to Hell" by AC/DC
**Commentary:** Jim Ross & Jerry Lawler

**Sunday Night Heat (Pre-Show):**
- Too Much (Brian Christopher & Scott Taylor) defeated LOD 2000 (Hawk & Animal) (with Droz) (2:14)
- Gangrel defeated Dustin Runnels (2:32)
- Disciples of Apocalypse (8-Ball & Skull) (with Paul Ellering) defeated Bradshaw & Vader (2:56)

**PPV Results:**
1. D'Lo Brown (c) defeated Val Venis by disqualification — WWF European Championship (15:24/15:31)
2. The Oddities (Giant Silva, Golga & Kurrgan) (with Luna, ICP) defeated Kaientai (Taka Michinoku, Dick Togo, Men's Teioh & Sho Funaki) (with Yamaguchi-san) — Handicap Match (10:10)
3. **X-Pac (with Howard Finkel) defeated Jeff Jarrett** — Hair vs. Hair Match (11:11) [Jarrett lost his hair]
4. Edge & Sable defeated Marc Mero & Jacqueline — Mixed Tag Team Match (8:26/8:24)
5. **Ken Shamrock defeated Owen Hart (with Dan Severn)** — Lion's Den Match (9:15/9:16) [Submission]
6. **New Age Outlaws (Road Dogg & Billy Gunn) defeated Mankind (c)** — Falls Count Anywhere Handicap Match — WWF Tag Team Championship (5:17/7:30) [NAO WON tag titles back]
7. **Triple H (with Chyna) defeated The Rock (c) (with Mark Henry)** — Ladder Match — WWF Intercontinental Championship (25:58/26:01) [Triple H WON IC title — legendary match]
8. **"Stone Cold" Steve Austin (c) defeated The Undertaker** — WWF Championship (20:52/20:55) [Austin retained with Stunner; Undertaker handed Austin the title after the match — show of respect]

**Key Notes:**
- The ladder match between Triple H and The Rock is widely considered one of the best ladder matches in wrestling history
- Triple H suffered a legitimate knee injury after this match, requiring surgery
- The Austin vs. Undertaker main event was a classic, with Undertaker showing respect by handing Austin the title
- X-Pac shaving Jeff Jarrett's hair was a major moment
- Edge & Sable's victory began Sable's massive popularity rise
- SummerSlam was one of the most successful WWF PPVs of the year
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SummerSlam_(1998)

**Incident:** Vince McMahon fired Mankind; Steve Austin drove a zamboni into the arena and attacked McMahon

**Details:**
- Date: July 27, 1998 (Mankind fired on Raw); September 28, 1998 (zamboni incident on Raw)

## Conflicts with Previous Reports

### WWF April-June 1998 Report Context
- **World Title:** The Apr-Jun 1998 report showed Austin winning from Kane on June 29. This report shows Austin retaining through the period until losing at Breakdown (Sep 27), title vacated.
- **IC Title:** The Apr-Jun 1998 report showed Rock retaining throughout. This report shows Rock vs. Triple H draw at Fully Loaded (Jul 26), then Triple H winning at SummerSlam (Aug 30).
- **European Title:** The Apr-Jun 1998 report showed Triple H as champion. This report shows Triple H losing to D'Lo Brown (Jul 20), D'Lo retaining at Fully Loaded and SummerSlam, then losing to X-Pac (Sep 21).
- **Tag Titles:** The Apr-Jun 1998 report showed NAO as champions. This report shows four title changes: NAO → Kane & Mankind (Jul 13) → Austin & Undertaker (Jul 26) → Kane & Mankind (Aug 10) → NAO (Aug 30).

## Conclusion

The July-September 1998 period was one of the most dynamic in WWF history, featuring multiple major title changes across all championships. Triple H's legendary ladder match victory over The Rock at SummerSlam for the IC title, D'Lo Brown's rise as European champion, Kane & Mankind's tag team dominance, and the WWF Championship being vacated at Breakdown were the headline stories. The period also saw the Austin vs. McMahon feud escalate (zamboni incident, McMahon's broken ankle), the early formation of the Corporation, Mankind's firing and return, Christian's debut, Sable's massive popularity rise, and Triple H's knee injury requiring surgery. SummerSlam at MSG drew 21,588 fans and ~600,000 PPV buys, confirming WWF's clear dominance in the Monday Night Wars. The WWF Championship vacancy set the stage for the Survivor Series tournament that would determine the new champion.
