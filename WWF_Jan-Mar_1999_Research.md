# WWF January-March 1999 Research Report

**Period Covered:** January 1, 1999 - March 31, 1999

## Executive Summary

The January-March 1999 period was one of the most eventful in WWF history, featuring the Rock-Mankind title feud that saw the WWF Championship change hands four times in less than two months, the debut of The Big Show (Paul Wight), the formation of The Ministry of Darkness led by The Undertaker, the first-ever Empty Arena Match during Super Bowl halftime (Halftime Heat), the legendary "I Quit" match at Royal Rumble, and WrestleMania XV where Steve Austin won the WWF Championship from The Rock. The period also featured the St. Valentine's Day Massacre PPV in Memphis with the Big Show's debut and the Austin-McMahon steel cage match, Mr. McMahon's shocking Royal Rumble victory, and Triple H's heel turn against X-Pac at WrestleMania XV. The WWF Championship changed hands four times between The Rock and Mankind (Jan 4, Jan 24, Jan 31, Feb 14), the Intercontinental Championship changed hands twice (Val Venis won at StVD, Road Dogg won at WrestleMania XV), and the European Championship changed hands when Shane McMahon won at WrestleMania XV. The Ministry of Darkness was formed in January/February 1999 with Undertaker as leader and members including The Acolytes, The Brood, Viscera, and Mideon. WrestleMania XV drew a sell-out crowd at the First Union Center in Philadelphia.

## Conclusion

The January-March 1999 period was one of the most chaotic and eventful in WWF history, featuring four WWF Championship changes in less than two months, the debut of The Big Show, the formation of The Ministry of Darkness, the first-ever Empty Arena Match during Super Bowl halftime, the legendary "I Quit" match at Royal Rumble, and WrestleMania XV where Steve Austin won the WWF Championship. The Rock-Mankind feud dominated the period with brutal matches including the "I Quit" match (featured in "Beyond the Mat"), the Empty Arena Match (6.6 TV rating during Super Bowl), and the ladder match at St. Valentine's Day Massacre. Vince McMahon's legitimate injury at StVD Massacre and his toughness in continuing the match became legendary. The Big Show's debut as McMahon's "insurance policy" set up his long-term run in WWF. The Ministry of Darkness formation gave Undertaker a new faction and goal. Triple H's heel turn on X-Pac at WrestleMania XV set up DX's breakup. The period ended with Austin winning the WWF Championship at WrestleMania XV, setting up the next phase of the Austin-McMahon-Rock storyline.
