## 1. Title Changes

### ECW World Heavyweight Championship — Raven → Terry Funk

**Title Change: Raven → Terry Funk**

- **Date:** April 13, 1997
- **Taping date:** April 13, 1997
- **Air date:** Aired on PPV (Barely Legal)
- **Championship:** ECW World Heavyweight Championship
- **Winner:** Terry Funk
- **Loser:** Raven (c)
- **Show:** ECW Barely Legal 1997
- **Venue:** ECW Arena, Philadelphia, PA
- **Attendance:** [UNCONFIRMED — FLAG]
- **Finish:** Terry Funk defeated Raven to win the ECW World Heavyweight Championship. Earlier in the night, Funk had defeated The Sandman and Stevie Richards in a triple threat match to earn the title shot against Raven.
- **Event type:** PPV (ECW's first ever pay-per-view)
- **Why it mattered:** This marked Terry Funk winning the ECW World Heavyweight Championship from Raven, headlining ECW's first ever pay-per-view event. This was Terry Funk's second ECW World Heavyweight Championship reign. Before the match, ECW owner Paul Heyman presented Terry with a belt, paid for through a collection taken up by wrestlers on the ECW roster, that declared him the "Lifetime ECW World Heavyweight Champion."
