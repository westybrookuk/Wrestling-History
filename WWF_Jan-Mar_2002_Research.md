# WWF January-March 2002 Research Report

**Period Covered:** January 1, 2002 - March 31, 2002

## Executive Summary

The January-March 2002 period marked the end of the WWF (World Wrestling Federation) name and the beginning of the WWE (World Wrestling Entertainment) era, featuring three major PPV events — Royal Rumble (January 20, Atlanta), No Way Out (February 17, Milwaukee), and WrestleMania X8 (March 17, Toronto) — and marked by the return of the nWo, the Brand Extension announcement, and Triple H winning the Undisputed WWF Championship at WrestleMania X8. Key developments included: Triple H winning the 2002 Royal Rumble match by last eliminating Kurt Angle; Chris Jericho retaining the Undisputed WWF Championship against Steve Austin at No Way Out; the nWo (Hulk Hogan, Scott Hall, Kevin Nash) returning at No Way Out and attacking Austin; Triple H defeating Chris Jericho to win the Undisputed WWF Championship at WrestleMania X8; and the Brand Extension being announced after WrestleMania X8, splitting the roster into Raw and SmackDown brands. Royal Rumble drew 12,915 attendance at the Philips Arena in Atlanta with $859,690 gate and $142,615 merchandise sales. No Way Out drew 15,235 attendance at the Bradley Center in Milwaukee with $966,150 gate and $139,591 merchandise sales. WrestleMania X8 drew 68,237 attendance at the SkyDome in Toronto, setting a new attendance record.

4. **Ric Flair defeated Vince McMahon** — Street Fight (14:55)
5. **Chris Jericho (c) defeated The Rock** — Undisputed WWF Championship (18:48/18:50) **Chris Jericho retained**

### WrestleMania X8 (March 17, 2002)

**Location:** SkyDome, Toronto, Ontario, Canada
**Attendance:** 68,237 (sellout, new attendance record)

**Sunday Night Heat (Pre-Show):**
- **Rikishi, Scotty 2 Hotty & Albert defeated Lance Storm, Test & Mr. Perfect** — Six-Man Tag Team Match — Special Guest Referee: Jacqueline (3:06/7:19)

**PPV Results:**
1. **Rob Van Dam defeated William Regal (c)** — WWF Intercontinental Championship (6:19/6:22) **Rob Van Dam WON Intercontinental title**
2. **Diamond Dallas Page (c) defeated Christian** — WWF European Championship (6:08/6:10) **DDP retained**
3. **Maven (c) vs. Goldust** — WWF Hardcore Championship — ended in No Contest (3:15/3:17) [Spike Dudley pinned Maven to win Hardcore title under 24/7 rules]
4. **Kurt Angle defeated Kane** — Singles Match (10:45)
5. **The Undertaker defeated Ric Flair** — No Disqualification Match (18:47) [Undertaker's WrestleMania streak improved to 10-0]
6. **Edge defeated Booker T** — Singles Match (6:32)
7. **"Stone Cold" Steve Austin defeated Scott Hall (with Kevin Nash)** — Singles Match (9:51)
8. **Billy & Chuck (Billy Gunn & Chuck Palumbo) (c) defeated The APA (Faarooq & Bradshaw), The Dudley Boyz (Bubba Ray & D-Von) (with Stacy Keibler), and The Hardy Boyz (Matt & Jeff)** — Four Corners Elimination Match — WWF Tag Team Championship (13:50/13:52) **Billy & Chuck retained**
9. **The Rock defeated Hollywood Hulk Hogan** — Singles Match (16:23/16:24) [Hogan's first WrestleMania since WrestleMania IX in 1993; Nash and Hall attacked Hogan after match]
10. **Jazz (c) defeated Trish Stratus and Lita** — Triple Threat Match — WWF Women's Championship (6:16) **Jazz retained**
11. **Triple H defeated Chris Jericho (c) (with Stephanie McMahon)** — Undisputed WWF Championship (18:41) **Triple H WON Undisputed WWF Championship**

**Key Notes:**
- Triple H won Undisputed WWF Championship from Chris Jericho
- Rob Van Dam won WWF Intercontinental Championship from William Regal
- Undertaker's WrestleMania streak improved to 10-0
- The Rock defeated Hollywood Hulk Hogan in "Icon vs. Icon" match
- nWo (Hogan, Hall, Nash) storyline continued
- Brand Extension announced after WrestleMania X8
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WrestleMania_X8

## Conclusion

The January-March 2002 period marked the end of the WWF name and the beginning of the WWE era, featuring three major PPV events and major storylines. Triple H won the 2002 Royal Rumble match by last eliminating Kurt Angle. Chris Jericho retained the Undisputed WWF Championship against Steve Austin at No Way Out. The nWo (Hulk Hogan, Scott Hall, Kevin Nash) returned at No Way Out and attacked Austin, with Hogan turning on Hall and Nash to end nWo involvement. Triple H defeated Chris Jericho to win the Undisputed WWF Championship at WrestleMania X8. Rob Van Dam won the WWF Intercontinental Championship from William Regal at WrestleMania X8. The Brand Extension was announced on March 25, 2002, splitting the WWF roster into Raw and SmackDown brands, with Vince McMahon taking control of SmackDown and Ric Flair taking control of Raw. Royal Rumble drew 12,915 attendance at the Philips Arena in Atlanta with $859,690 gate and $142,615 merchandise sales. No Way Out drew 15,235 attendance at the Bradley Center in Milwaukee with $966,150 gate and $139,591 merchandise sales. WrestleMania X8 drew 68,237 attendance at the SkyDome in Toronto, setting a new attendance record.
