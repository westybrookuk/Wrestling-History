# WCW July-September 1999 Research Report

**Period Covered:** July 1, 1999 - September 30, 1999

## Executive Summary

The July-September 1999 period was marked by continued chaos in WCW, with rapid title changes and the dissolution of the nWo faction. The period saw three World Title changes in just three months, as the title passed from Kevin Nash to Randy Savage to Hulk Hogan to Sting. The infamous "Fingerpoke of Doom" controversy at Bash at the Beach 2000 was building toward its climax. The period also saw the rise of new factions like The Filthy Animals and The Revolution, while the nWo continued to fragment. Fall Brawl 1999 abandoned the traditional WarGames match for the first time in the event's history.

### WCW World Heavyweight Championship

**July 11, 1999 - Kevin Nash to Randy Savage**
- **Event:** WCW Bash at the Beach 1999 (National Car Rental Center, Ft. Lauderdale, FL)
- **Match Type:** Tag Team Match for WCW World Heavyweight Championship
- **Result:** Randy Savage & Sid Vicious defeated Kevin Nash (c) & Sting
- **Notes:** Per stipulation, Savage became the new WCW World Heavyweight Champion. This was part of the ongoing nWo vs. Team Madness storyline.

- **Event:** WCW Road Wild 1999 (Sturgis Rally & Races, Sturgis, SD)
- **Match Type:** Retirement Match
- **Result:** Hulk Hogan defeated Kevin Nash (c) to retain the title
- **Notes:** Wait - this needs correction. Hogan defeated Nash in a retirement match, but Nash had already won the title from Savage at Bash at the Beach. So the correct sequence is:

## Major PPV Events

### WCW Bash at the Beach 1999 (July 11, 1999)

**Location:** National Car Rental Center, Ft. Lauderdale, Florida  
**Attendance:** 13,624  
**Buyrate:** 0.4

**Key Matches:**
1. CJ Afi & Jeremy Lopez beat Jamie Howard & Jet Jaguar
2. Konnan pinned Kendall (10:52)
3. Misterio pinned Hennig (15:00)
4. David Finlay won a "junkyard battle royal" (13:50)
5. Bryan Adams & Bryan Clark beat Sean Stasiak & Chuck Palumbo (13:36) to win the WCW Tag Title
6. Kanyon pinned Booker T (10:05)
7. Mike Awesome beat Scott Steiner (9:11) via DQ
8. Vampiro beat The Kiss Demon (6:40) in a "graveyard" match
9. Shane Douglas pinned Buff Bagwell (7:53)
10. **Hulk Hogan pinned Jeff Jarrett to win the WCW World Title**

**Key Notes:**
- This was building toward the infamous "Fingerpoke of Doom" controversy
- The nWo was in a state of civil war
- Multiple factions were in conflict
- **Source:** http://www.prowrestlinghistory.com/supercards/usa/wcw/beach.html

### WCW Road Wild 1999 (August 14, 1999)

**Location:** Sturgis Rally & Races, Sturgis, South Dakota

**Results:**
1. The Filthy Animals (Rey Mysterio Jr., Eddie Guerrero & Billy Kidman) defeated The Deadpool (Vampiro, Violent J & Shaggy 2 Dope)
2. **Harlem Heat (Booker T & Stevie Ray) defeated The Jersey Triad (Kanyon & Bam Bam Bigelow) (c) to retain the WCW World Tag Team Championship** (13:06)
3. The Revolution (Dean Malenko, Perry Saturn & Shane Douglas) defeated The West Texas Rednecks (Curt Hennig, Barry Windham & Bobby Duncum Jr.) (10:57)
4. Buff Bagwell defeated Ernest Miller (w/ Sonny Onoo) (7:24)
5. **Chris Benoit (c) defeated Diamond Dallas Page in a No Disqualification match to retain the WCW United States Heavyweight Championship** (12:14)
6. Sid Vicious defeated Sting (10:40)
7. **Goldberg defeated Rick Steiner** (5:39)
8. **Randy Savage defeated Dennis Rodman** (11:30)
9. **Hulk Hogan (c) defeated Kevin Nash in a Retirement match to retain the WCW World Heavyweight Championship** (12:16)

**Key Notes:**
- Goldberg continued his undefeated streak
- Dennis Rodman was involved in a match (celebrity involvement)
- Multiple title changes occurred
- The Revolution vs. West Texas Rednecks feud continued
- **Source:** https://prowrestling.fandom.com/wiki/Road_Wild_1999

### WCW Fall Brawl 1999 (September 12, 1999)

**Location:** Lawrence Joel Veterans Memorial Coliseum, Winston-Salem, North Carolina  
**Attendance:** 7,491  
**Buyrate:** 0.29

**Results:**
1. The Filthy Animals (Rey Mysterio Jr., Eddie Guerrero & Billy Kidman) defeated The Deadpool (Vampiro, Violent J & Shaggy 2 Dope) (14:14)
2. **WCW Cruiserweight Championship:** Lenny Lane (c) pinned Kaz Hayashi (12:09)
3. Hugh Morrus & Brian Knobbs beat Dean Malenko & Shane Douglas (9:26) in a "no DQ" match
4. **WCW TV Championship:** Rick Steiner (c) pinned Perry Saturn (9:23)
5. Berlyn pinned Jim Duggan (7:58)
6. **WCW World Tag Team Championship:** Harlem Heat beat Barry & Kendall Windham (13:05) to win the WCW Tag Title when Booker pinned Kendall
7. **WCW United States Heavyweight Championship:** Sid Vicious pinned Chris Benoit (11:48) to win the U.S. Title
8. Bill Goldberg pinned Diamond Dallas Page (9:04)
9. **WCW World Heavyweight Championship:** Sting pinned Hulk Hogan (c) at 13:56 with the Scorpion Death Lock to win the title

**Key Notes:**
- **First Fall Brawl without a WarGames match** - a significant departure from tradition
- Three title changes on one card (Tag Titles, US Title, World Title)
- Sting won his sixth World Title
- Sid's undefeated streak reached 80-0
- The low buyrate (0.29) reflected WCW's declining fortunes
- **Source:** https://www.prowrestlinghistory.com/supercards/usa/wcw/fall.html

## Conclusion

The July-September 1999 period was marked by continued chaos in WCW, with three World Title changes in three months and the continued fragmentation of the nWo. The period saw the rise of new factions like The Filthy Animals and The Revolution, but also the decline of WCW's traditional events (Fall Brawl without WarGames). The period set up the infamous "Fingerpoke of Doom" controversy that would occur at Bash at the Beach 2000. The low buyrates and creative decisions reflected WCW's declining fortunes as the promotion struggled to compete with WWF. The period exemplified WCW's creative problems and contributed to the promotion's eventual decline.
