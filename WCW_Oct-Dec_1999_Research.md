# WCW October-December 1999 Research Report

**Period Covered:** October 1, 1999 - December 31, 1999

## Executive Summary

The October-December 1999 period was marked by one of the most controversial finishes in wrestling history at Starrcade 1999, where Bret Hart retained the World Title from Goldberg in a Montreal Screwjob-style finish. The period saw Bret Hart win the World Title in a tournament at Mayhem, only to suffer a career-altering concussion from Goldberg at Starrcade. The nWo 2000 faction was formed with Hart, Hall, Nash, and Jarrett. The period also saw multiple title changes and the continued decline of WCW's creative direction. Halloween Havoc 1999 featured a botched finish between Sting and Hogan, and Mayhem 1999 was held in Toronto, Canada, where Hart won the title in his home country.

## Title Changes

### WCW World Heavyweight Championship

**October 24, 1999 - Sting to Vacated**
- **Event:** WCW Halloween Havoc 1999 (MGM Grand Garden Arena, Las Vegas, NV)
- **Match Type:** Singles match (Sting (c) vs. Hulk Hogan)
- **Result:** Sting defeated Hulk Hogan in a botched 3-minute match
- **Notes:** After the match, Goldberg defeated Sting in an unsanctioned match and was awarded the title. Sting attacked referee Charles Robinson, leading to J.J. Dillon vacating the title.
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halloween_Havoc_(1999)

**November 21, 1999 - Vacated to Bret Hart**
- **Event:** WCW Mayhem 1999 (Air Canada Centre, Toronto, Ontario, Canada)
- **Match Type:** Tournament Final (32-man tournament)
- **Result:** Bret Hart defeated Chris Benoit (17:44)
- **Notes:** Hart won the tournament to become the new WCW World Heavyweight Champion. He won the title in his home country of Canada.
- **Attendance:** 13,839
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayhem_(1999)

**December 19, 1999 - Bret Hart (c) retained from Goldberg**
- **Event:** WCW Starrcade 1999 (MCI Center, Washington, D.C.)
- **Match Type:** No Disqualification match
- **Result:** Bret Hart (c) defeated Goldberg (12:30)
- **Notes:** Controversial Montreal Screwjob-style finish. Roddy Piper called for the bell while Hart had Goldberg in the Sharpshooter, even though Goldberg never submitted. Hart suffered a severe concussion from Goldberg's mule kick.
- **Attendance:** 8,582
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starrcade_(1999)

## Major PPV Events

### WCW Halloween Havoc 1999 (October 24, 1999)

**Location:** MGM Grand Garden Arena, Las Vegas, Nevada  
**Attendance:** 8,464  
**Buyrate:** 230,000

**Results:**
1. Disco Inferno (c) defeated Lash LeRoux to retain WCW Cruiserweight Championship (7:35)
2. Harlem Heat (Booker T & Stevie Ray) defeated The Filthy Animals (Billy Kidman & Konnan) and The First Family (Brian Knobbs & Hugh Morrus) in a three-way tag team street fight to win the vacant WCW World Tag Team Championship (5:02)
3. Eddie Guerrero defeated Perry Saturn by DQ (11:12)
4. Brad Armstrong defeated Berlyn (4:23)
5. Rick Steiner defeated Chris Benoit (c) to win the WCW World Television Championship (12:50)
6. The Total Package (w/ Miss Elizabeth) defeated Bret Hart (7:46)
7. **Sting (c) defeated Hulk Hogan to retain WCW World Heavyweight Championship (0:03)** - Botched finish
8. **Goldberg defeated Sid Vicious (c) to win WCW United States Championship** (7:11)
9. Diamond Dallas Page (w/ Kimberly) defeated Ric Flair in a Strap Match (12:49)
10. **Goldberg defeated Sting (c) to win WCW World Heavyweight Championship** (3:08) - Unsanctioned match

**Key Notes:**
- The main event was botched - Sting laid down for Hogan, who came out in street clothes
- Goldberg defeated Sting in an unsanctioned match and was awarded the title
- Sting attacked referee Charles Robinson, leading to the title being vacated
- Multiple title changes on one card
- The event was heavily criticized for its booking
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halloween_Havoc_(1999)

### WCW Mayhem 1999 (November 21, 1999)

**Location:** Air Canada Centre, Toronto, Ontario, Canada  
**Attendance:** 13,839

**Results:**
1. Chris Benoit defeated Jeff Jarrett in Tournament Semi-Final match (9:27)
2. Evan Karagias (w/ Madusa) defeated Disco Inferno (c) to win WCW Cruiserweight Championship (8:28)
3. Norman Smiley defeated Brian Knobbs (w/ Jimmy Hart) to win inaugural WCW Hardcore Championship (7:29)
4. The Revolution (Perry Saturn, Dean Malenko and Asya) (w/ Shane Douglas) defeated The Filthy Animals (Eddie Guerrero, Billy Kidman and Torrie Wilson) (w/ Konnan) in Elimination match (11:15)
5. Buff Bagwell defeated Curt Hennig in Retirement match (7:47)
6. **Bret Hart defeated Sting in Tournament Semi-Final match** (9:27)
7. Vampiro (w/ Jerry Only) defeated Berlyn (w/ The Wall) in Dog Collar match (4:57)
8. Meng defeated The Total Package (w/ Elizabeth) (5:23)
9. Scott Hall (c) defeated Booker T to retain WCW United States and World Television Championships (6:04)
10. David Flair vs. Kimberly Page ended in a no contest (4:55)
11. Goldberg defeated Sid Vicious by Technical Submission in "I Quit" match (5:30)
12. **Bret Hart defeated Chris Benoit in Tournament Final to win vacant WCW World Heavyweight Championship** (17:44)

**Key Notes:**
- Bret Hart won the World Title in his home country of Canada
- Hart also won the US Title in his semi-final match against Goldberg
- The Revolution vs. Filthy Animals elimination match was a highlight
- Multiple title changes and tournament finals
- Curt Hennig was "retired" by Buff Bagwell
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayhem_(1999)

### WCW Starrcade 1999 (December 19, 1999)

**Location:** MCI Center, Washington, D.C.  
**Attendance:** 8,582

**Results:**
1. The Mamalukes (Big Vito and Johnny the Bull) (w/ Tony Marinara) defeated Disco Inferno and Lash LeRoux (9:40)
2. Madusa defeated Evan Karagias (c) (w/ Spice) to win WCW Cruiserweight Championship (3:32)
3. Norman Smiley (c) defeated Meng to retain WCW Hardcore Championship (4:29)
4. The Revolution (Shane Douglas, Dean Malenko, Perry Saturn and Asya) defeated Jim Duggan and The Varsity Club (Kevin Sullivan, Mike Rotunda and Rick Steiner) (w/ Leia Meow) (4:53)
5. Vampiro (w/ Misfits) defeated Steve Williams by DQ (5:02)
6. Vampiro (w/ Misfits) defeated Oklahoma (2:52)
7. Creative Control (Gerald and Patrick) and Curt Hennig (w/ Shane) defeated Harlem Heat (Booker T and Stevie Ray) and Midnight (7:52)
8. Jeff Jarrett defeated Dustin Rhodes in Bunkhouse Brawl (11:18)
9. Diamond Dallas Page defeated David Flair in Crowbar on a Pole match (3:53)
10. Sting (w/ Elizabeth) defeated The Total Package by DQ (5:31)
11. Kevin Nash defeated Sid Vicious in Powerbomb match (6:58)
12. Chris Benoit (c) defeated Jeff Jarrett in Ladder match to retain WCW United States Heavyweight Championship (10:14)
13. **Bret Hart (c) defeated Goldberg in No Disqualification match to retain WCW World Heavyweight Championship** (12:30)

**Key Notes:**
- **Controversial Montreal Screwjob-style finish** - Roddy Piper called for the bell while Hart had Goldberg in the Sharpshooter
- **Hart suffered severe concussion** from Goldberg's mule kick, which ultimately led to his retirement
- The nWo 2000 faction was formed with Hart, Hall, Nash, and Jarrett
- The ending resembled the Montreal Screwjob from 1997
- The show ended abruptly at 10:42 PM, more than 15 minutes before the top of the hour
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starrcade_(1999)

## Injuries / Backstage Incidents / Controversies

### Bret Hart's Career-Altering Concussion

**Incident:** Bret Hart suffered a severe concussion from Goldberg's mule kick at Starrcade 1999

**Details:**
- The concussion occurred during the main event at Starrcade (December 19, 1999)
- Hart was diagnosed with post-concussion syndrome
- He continued to wrestle for a few weeks but the injury worsened
- Hart eventually retired from professional wrestling in 2000
- This was one of the most significant injuries in WCW history
