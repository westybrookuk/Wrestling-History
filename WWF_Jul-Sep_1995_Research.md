# WWF Research Report: July 1995 – End of September 1995

---

## 1. Title Changes

### 1A. WWF Intercontinental Championship — Jeff Jarrett → Shawn Michaels

- **Date:** July 23, 1995
- **Championship:** WWF Intercontinental Championship
- **Event:** In Your House 2: The Lumberjacks (Nashville Municipal Auditorium, Nashville, TN)
- **Winner:** Shawn Michaels
- **Loser:** Jeff Jarrett (c) (w/ The Roadie)
- **Details:** Shawn Michaels defeated Jeff Jarrett to win the Intercontinental Championship at 19:59 with a superkick. The Roadie accidentally tripped Jarrett during an Irish whip (Jarrett had asked Roadie to trip Shawn, but Shawn reversed it and Roadie, not looking, tripped Jarrett instead). When Jarrett got back up, he was greeted with Sweet Chin Music. This was Michaels' third Intercontinental Championship reign.
- **Note:** Following this event, Jeff Jarrett left the WWF for several months and returned to the United States Wrestling Association. Jarrett would return to WWF in late 1995.
- **Taping vs. Air Date:** This was a live PPV event on July 23, 1995.
- **Source:** WWE.com title history; wrestlingrecaps.com; wwe.com/classics/titlehistory/intercontinental; prowrestling.fandom.com (Jeff Jarrett); bradywwe.fandom.com (Jeff Jarrett).

### 1B. WWF Women's Championship — Alundra Blayze → Bertha Faye

- **Date:** August 27, 1995
- **Championship:** WWF Women's Championship
- **Event:** SummerSlam 1995 (Pittsburgh Civic Arena, Pittsburgh, PA)
- **Winner:** Bertha Faye (w/ Harvey Wippleman)
- **Loser:** Alundra Blayze (c)
- **Details:** Bertha Faye defeated Alundra Blayze to win the Women's Championship at 4:37-4:38 with a sit-down powerbomb after Blayze missed a dropkick off the top. This was a shock upset, as Blayze was dominant in the women's division.
- **Note:** Bertha Faye had debuted on April 3, 1995, attacking Alundra Blayze right after Blayze regained the title from Bull Nakano. The attack broke Blayze's nose and she was forced to have reconstructive surgery. Blayze would regain the title from Faye on October 23, 1995.
- **Taping vs. Air Date:** This was a live PPV event on August 27, 1995.
- **Source:** WWE.com SummerSlam 1995 results; thehistoryofwwe.com WWE Women's Title History; wrestlingrecaps.com SummerSlam 1995; bwwe.fandom.com (Alundra Blayze); en.wikipedia.org (Rhonda Sing/Bertha Faye).

### 1C. WWF Tag Team Championship — Controversial Finish at In Your House 3, Then Smoking Gunns Win

- **Date:** September 24, 1995 (In Your House 3); September 25, 1995 (Raw)
- **Championship:** WWF Tag Team Championship
- **Events:** 
  - In Your House 3: Triple Header (Saginaw Civic Center, Saginaw, MI) - September 24
  - Monday Night Raw (Grand Center, Grand Rapids, MI) - September 25

**In Your House 3 Finish (Controversial):**
- Diesel (WWF Champion) & Shawn Michaels (IC Champion) defeated The British Bulldog & Yokozuna (Tag Team Champions)
- British Bulldog had replaced Owen Hart, who was with his wife at the hospital (kayfabe: she was giving birth to their second child)
- Diesel pinned Owen Hart (who interfered late in the match) with a Jackknife Powerbomb at 15:42-15:43
- **Controversy:** Owen Hart was not the legal man in the match (British Bulldog was his substitute), so the title change was not recognized
- The next night on Raw, WWF President Gorilla Monsoon announced that Clarence Mason (Jim Cornette's attorney) had regained the tag team titles for Owen Hart & Yokozuna since Owen was not legally in the match

**Raw - September 25, 1995:**
- The Smoking Gunns (Billy & Bart Gunn) defeated WWF Tag Team Champions Owen Hart & Yokozuna (w/ Jim Cornette & Mr. Fuji) to win the titles at 10:09
- Bart pinned Owen after Owen sustained the Sidewinder (from Bart) and an accidental slash from Yokozuna
- After the bout, Diesel and Shawn Michaels came out to celebrate with the new champions
- **Note:** This was the Smoking Gunns' second tag team title reign

- **Taping vs. Air Date:** In Your House 3 was live PPV on September 24. Raw was taped on September 25 in Grand Rapids, MI.
- **Source:** en.wikipedia.org (In Your House 3); grokipedia.com (In Your House 3); prowrestling.fandom.com (In Your House 3); wrestlingrecaps.com (In Your House 3); thehistoryofwwe.com (Raw 1995 results); onlineworldofwrestling.com (Owen Hart, Smoking Gunns); darrenswinloss.wordpress.com (Raw 1995).

## 3. Roster Movements / Debuts / Exits

### 3A. Lex Luger — Departure to WCW (September 4, 1995)

- **Date:** September 4, 1995 (WCW Monday Nitro debut); September 3, 1995 (last WWF match)
- **Person:** Lex Luger
- **Change:** Lex Luger left WWF and debuted in WCW on the premiere episode of WCW Monday Nitro on September 4, 1995
- **Details:** 
  - Luger's last WWF match was on September 3, 1995, at a house show in Saint John, New Brunswick, teaming with Shawn Michaels and defeating Owen Hart & Yokozuna by disqualification
  - At SummerSlam (August 27), Luger prevented Sir Mo from interfering in the Diesel vs. King Mabel main event
  - Luger worked for WWF without a contract for several weeks after SummerSlam
  - On September 4, Luger walked to ringside during the Sting vs. Ric Flair match on WCW Monday Nitro and agreed to face Hulk Hogan the following week
  - Luger told WWF he would be staying, but WCW made an offer he couldn't turn down
  - Very few people knew about his departure, possibly only Sting and Eric Bischoff
- **Impact:** This was a significant moment in the Monday Night Wars. Luger had been positioned as the "next Hulk Hogan" in WWF but never reached that level. His departure to WCW was a blow to WWF and a boost for WCW's new Monday Nitro program.
- **Confidence:** Confirmed fact.
- **Source:** en.wikipedia.org (Lex Luger); wrestling20yrs.com; thesignaturespot.com; fightful.com; bwwe.fandom.com (Lex Luger); pwtorch.com.

### 3B. British Bulldog — Heel Turn, Joins Jim Cornette (September 1995)

- **Date:** September 1995
- **Person:** The British Bulldog (Davey Boy Smith)
- **Change:** British Bulldog turned heel and joined Jim Cornette's management
- **Details:**
  - Bulldog replaced Owen Hart at In Your House 3 (September 24) due to Owen's "family emergency"
  - Bulldog defeated Bam Bam Bigelow at In Your House 3
  - By September 25, Bulldog was aligned with Jim Cornette, Owen Hart, and Yokozuna
  - On October 9 Raw, Davey Boy Smith, Owen Hart, & Yokozuna defeated Diesel, Shawn Michaels & The Undertaker
- **Impact:** This was a significant heel turn for one of WWF's top babyfaces. Bulldog became part of the "Camp Cornette" faction with Owen Hart and Yokozuna.
- **Confidence:** Confirmed fact.
- **Source:** onlineworldofwrestling.com (Owen Hart); thehistoryofwwe.com (Raw 1995); darrenswinloss.wordpress.com (Raw 1995).

### 3C. Jeff Jarrett — Departure to USWA (Late July 1995)

- **Date:** Late July 1995 (after In Your House 2)

- **Change:** Jeff Jarrett left WWF after losing the Intercontinental Championship to Shawn Michaels at In Your House 2 on July 23, 1995

  - Jarrett performed "With My Baby Tonight" live at In Your House 2

- **Source:** prowrestling.fandom.com (Jeff Jarrett); bradywwe.fandom.com (Jeff Jarrett); en.wikipedia.org (Jeff Jarrett).

## 4. Injuries / Backstage Incidents / Controversies

### 4A. In Your House 3 Tag Team Title Controversy

- **Date:** September 24-25, 1995
- **People involved:** Owen Hart, Yokozuna, British Bulldog, Diesel, Shawn Michaels, Jim Cornette, Clarence Mason, Gorilla Monsoon
- **What happened:** At In Your House 3, Diesel & Shawn Michaels faced British Bulldog & Yokozuna for the tag team titles. Owen Hart was supposed to be in the match but was kayfabe at the hospital with his wife giving birth. British Bulldog replaced him. Diesel pinned Owen Hart (who interfered late in the match) and supposedly won the tag titles. However, the next night on Raw, Clarence Mason (Jim Cornette's attorney) announced that the title change was not recognized because Owen Hart was not the legal man in the match. WWF President Gorilla Monsoon then made the scheduled non-title match between Owen & Yokozuna vs. The Smoking Gunns into a title match, which The Smoking Gunns won.
- **Why it mattered:** This was a controversial and confusing finish that undermined the tag team championship. The decision to reverse the title change the next night was seen as a mistake.
- **Confidence:** Confirmed fact.
- **Source:** en.wikipedia.org (In Your House 3); grokipedia.com (In Your House 3); prowrestling.fandom.com (In Your House 3); wrestlingrecaps.com (In Your House 3); thehistoryofwwe.com (Raw 1995); onlineworldofwrestling.com (Owen Hart, Smoking Gunns).

### 4B. Shawn Michaels vs. Sid — Post-SummerSlam Attack

- **Date:** Post-SummerSlam (late August/early September 1995)
- **People involved:** Shawn Michaels, Sycho Sid
- **What happened:** At SummerSlam, Shawn Michaels was one of the lumberjacks in the Diesel vs. Sid match. Michaels performed a double axe handle off the top rope onto Sid, helping Diesel retain the title. This was revenge for Sid powerbombing Michaels three times on April 3, 1995. On July 24, 1995 (Raw), Sid declared revenge for Michaels' actions at SummerSlam. This led to further tension between the two.
- **Why it mattered:** This continued the Michaels-Sid feud from earlier in 1995.
- **Confidence:** Confirmed fact.
- **Source:** en.wikipedia.org (SummerSlam 1995); thehistoryofwwe.com (Raw 1995); prowrestling.fandom.com (SummerSlam 1995).

### 4C. Alundra Blayze's Nose Injury (April 3, 1995)

- **Date:** April 3, 1995 (Raw)
- **People involved:** Alundra Blayze, Bertha Faye
- **What happened:** On April 3, 1995 (Raw), Alundra Blayze defeated Bull Nakano to regain the WWF Women's Championship. Immediately after the match, Bertha Faye attacked Blayze, breaking her nose. Blayze was forced to have reconstructive surgery and was sidelined. She returned in August 1995 and lost the title to Bertha Faye at SummerSlam.
- **Why it mattered:** This injury kept Blayze out of action for several months and set up the Bertha Faye push.
- **Confidence:** Confirmed fact.
- **Source:** thehistoryofwwe.com (Women's Title History); bwwe.fandom.com (Alundra Blayze); wrestlingrecaps.com (SummerSlam 1995).

### 4D. Lex Luger's Contract Situation

- **Date:** August-September 1995
- **People involved:** Lex Luger, Vince McMahon, Eric Bischoff, WCW
- **What happened:** After SummerSlam (August 27), Lex Luger worked for WWF without a contract for several weeks. He told Vince McMahon he would be staying, but WCW made an offer he couldn't turn down. Luger worked a WWF house show on September 3 in Saint John, New Brunswick. The next night (September 4), he debuted in WCW on Monday Nitro. Very few people knew about his departure, possibly only Sting and Eric Bischoff. Some reports say McMahon didn't know until it aired on television.
- **Why it mattered:** This was a significant moment in the Monday Night Wars. Luger's departure was a blow to WWF and helped launch WCW Monday Nitro.
- **Confidence:** Confirmed fact, though exact timing of when McMahon knew is uncertain.
- **Source:** en.wikipedia.org (Lex Luger); wrestling20yrs.com; pwtorch.com; fightful.com.

### 4E. Owen Hart's "Family Emergency"

- **Date:** September 24, 1995 (In Your House 3)
- **People involved:** Owen Hart, Jim Cornette
- **What happened:** At In Your House 3, Owen Hart was supposedly with his wife at the hospital as she was giving birth to their second child. British Bulldog replaced him in the tag team title match. However, Owen appeared at the show and interfered in the match, getting pinned by Diesel. The title change was not recognized because Owen was not the legal man.
- **Why it mattered:** This was a kayfabe storyline (Owen's wife was not actually giving birth at the time). The confusing finish led to the title change being reversed the next night.
- **Confidence:** Confirmed fact (kayfabe).
- **Source:** en.wikipedia.org (In Your House 3); grokipedia.com (In Your House 3); prowrestling.fandom.com (In Your House 3).

## Summary: Key Narrative Threads During This Period

1. **Shawn Michaels' IC Title Reign:** Michaels won his third Intercontinental Championship at In Your House 2 and defended it in the acclaimed ladder match against Razor Ramon at SummerSlam. This was a highlight of the period.

2. **Diesel's Continued Push:** Diesel remained WWF Champion but continued to struggle to connect with fans. His matches against King Mabel and Sycho Sid were poorly received.

3. **Lex Luger's Departure:** Luger's jump to WCW was a significant moment in the Monday Night Wars, helping launch WCW Monday Nitro.

4. **Tag Team Title Controversy:** The confusing finish at In Your House 3 and the reversal the next night undermined the tag team championship.

5. **Women's Division Neglect:** Bertha Faye's shock upset over Alundra Blayze at SummerSlam was a rare moment for the women's division, which continued to be neglected.

6. **British Bulldog Heel Turn:** The British Bulldog's turn and alignment with Jim Cornette was a significant character change.

7. **SummerSlam Ladder Match:** Shawn Michaels vs. Razor Ramon was the highlight of the year, receiving critical acclaim and Match of the Year honors.

8. **Monday Night Wars Begin:** Lex Luger's departure to WCW marked the beginning of the Monday Night Wars between WWF and WCW.

## Simulator-Use Notes

### Title Lineage Implications
- **WWF Title (Diesel):** Diesel's reign continued through this period but his push was not connecting with fans. SummerSlam main event against King Mabel was poorly received.
- **IC Title (Shawn Michaels):** Michaels won his third IC title at In Your House 2 and defended it in the acclaimed ladder match at SummerSlam. This was a highlight of his career.
- **Tag Titles (Owen Hart & Yokozuna → Smoking Gunns):** The controversial finish at In Your House 3 and reversal the next night undermined the titles. Smoking Gunns won them cleanly on Raw.
- **Women's Title (Bertha Faye):** Shock upset over Alundra Blayze at SummerSlam. Brief reign (57 days) before losing it back to Blayze.

### Roster Availability Changes
- **Lex Luger:** Departed to WCW on September 4, 1995. No longer available for WWF storylines.
- **Jeff Jarrett:** Left WWF after In Your House 2 (July 23), returned to USWA. Would return to WWF in late 1995.
- **Shawn Michaels:** Available throughout, won IC title, defended at SummerSlam.
- **Diesel:** Available throughout, WWF Champion.
- **British Bulldog:** Available, turned heel in September, joined Camp Cornette.

### Injuries or Absences
- **Alundra Blayze:** Nose broken by Bertha Faye on April 3, 1995. Had reconstructive surgery, returned in August, lost title at SummerSlam.
- **Owen Hart:** Kayfabe absence at In Your House 3 (wife giving birth). Actually appeared at the show and interfered.

### Morale/Backstage Politics Effects
- **Lex Luger departure:** [Observer-reported] Luger told McMahon he was staying but accepted WCW offer. Very few people knew about his departure. McMahon may not have known until it aired on TV.
- **In Your House 3 controversy:** Booking mistake that undermined tag team titles. Decision to reverse title change next night was confusing.
- **SummerSlam main event:** Diesel vs. King Mabel was poorly received by fans and critics.
- **Shawn Michaels ladder match:** Critically acclaimed, Match of the Year. High point for Michaels.

### Faction/Team Changes
- **Camp Cornette formed:** Jim Cornette, Owen Hart, Yokozuna, British Bulldog (heel turn).
- **Two Dudes with Attitudes:** Diesel & Shawn Michaels continued their babyface alliance.
- **Allied Powers disbanded:** Lex Luger's departure ended the tag team with British Bulldog.

### Ratings/Buyrate/Attendance Impact
- **In Your House 2:** 280,000 buys (0.7 buyrate), 6,482 attendance
- **SummerSlam:** 205,000 buys (0.9 buyrate), 18,062 attendance
- **In Your House 3:** 160,000 buys (0.70 buyrate), 5,146 attendance
- **Raw ratings:** Consistently around 2.0-2.6 during this period
- **SummerSlam ladder match:** 4.75 stars from Meltzer, Match of the Year

### Network/Sponsor/Censorship Pressure
- **USA Network:** WWF's primary TV partner. No major censorship issues.
- **Monday Night Wars:** Lex Luger's departure to WCW marked the beginning of direct competition with WCW Monday Nitro.
- **WCW Monday Nitro debut:** September 4, 1995. Luger's surprise appearance was a major moment.

### Uncertain Facts That Should Not Be Hard-Coded
- **When Vince McMahon knew about Lex Luger's departure:** Some reports say McMahon didn't know until it aired on TV. Others say he knew earlier. This is uncertain.
- **Owen Hart's "family emergency":** This was kayfabe. His wife was not actually giving birth at the time. The exact reason for the storyline is uncertain.
- **British Bulldog's heel turn motivation:** The exact reasons for Bulldog's turn and alignment with Jim Cornette are not well-documented.

### Events That Should Be Optional/Branching Rather Than Forced Canon
- **Lex Luger's departure:** Could be branched - in one branch Luger stays in WWF, in another he leaves for WCW (canon).
- **In Your House 3 tag team title finish:** Could be branched - in one branch the title change is recognized, in another it's reversed (canon).
- **SummerSlam main event:** Could be branched - in one branch Diesel faces a different opponent (Shawn Michaels, Owen Hart, etc.), in another he faces King Mabel (canon).
- **British Bulldog heel turn:** Could be branched - in one branch Bulldog stays babyface, in another he turns heel (canon).

### Taping vs. Air Date Notes
- **In Your House 2:** Aired live on PPV (July 23, 1995). No taping/air date discrepancies.
- **SummerSlam:** Aired live on PPV (August 27, 1995). No taping/air date discrepancies.
- **In Your House 3:** Aired live on PPV (September 24, 1995). No taping/air date discrepancies.
- **Raw episodes:** Aired live on USA Network. No taping/air date discrepancies.

### Rumor/Observer-Reported Items
- **Lex Luger's departure:** [Observer-reported] Luger told McMahon he was staying but accepted WCW offer. Very few people knew. Possibly only Sting and Eric Bischoff knew in advance.
- **Shawn Michaels & The Kliq:** [Observer-reported] Michaels was allegedly the leader of The Kliq, a backstage group with significant influence. Michaels disputes this, saying McMahon pushed only deserving wrestlers.
- **SummerSlam ladder match:** [Observer-reported] This match was critically acclaimed and named Match of the Year by Pro Wrestling Torch.
- **In Your House 3 controversy:** [Observer-reported] The decision to reverse the title change was seen as a booking mistake.
