## 1. Title Changes

### ECW World Heavyweight Championship — Raven → The Sandman → Raven

**Title Change #1: Raven → The Sandman**

- **Date:** October 5, 1996
- **Taping date:** October 5, 1996
- **Air date:** Aired on Hardcore TV
- **Championship:** ECW World Heavyweight Championship
- **Winner:** The Sandman
- **Loser:** Stevie Richards (Raven's substitute)
- **Show:** ECW Ultimate Jeopardy 1996
- **Venue:** ECW Arena, Philadelphia, PA
- **Attendance:** 700
- **Finish:** The Sandman pinned Stevie Richards in an Ultimate Jeopardy tag team steel cage match. The Sandman & Tommy Dreamer defeated Stevie Richards & Brian Lee. Due to pre-match stipulations, Sandman won the ECW Title. This was the match where Dreamer was chokeslammed through four tables from the eagle's nest by Brian Lee. Complete stipulations stated if Stevie lost he would lose Raven's ECW title, if Lee lost he'd have his head shaved, if Sandman lost he would be caned. Raven no-showed the event, so Stevie Richards substituted for him.
- **Event type:** Arena supercard
- **Why it mattered:** This marked The Sandman regaining the ECW World Heavyweight Championship from Raven's faction. Raven's no-show added controversy to the title change, as he was on a leave of absence. The Sandman's victory was a major moment in ECW's 1996 storylines, marking a shift in the Raven vs. Sandman feud.
