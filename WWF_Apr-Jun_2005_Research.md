# WWF April-June 2005 Research Report

**Period Covered:** April 1, 2005 - June 30, 2005  
**Report Created:** October 5, 2026  
**Report Length:** 534 lines

## Executive Summary

The April-June 2005 period was defined by the resolution of the WrestleMania 21 title changes, the mid-year **2005 WWE Draft Lottery** that reshuffled both brands (including moving the WWE Championship to Raw), and the emergence of Carlito as Intercontinental Champion and Ric Flair's arrival on the SmackDown! brand. It also contained the inaugural **ECW One Night Stand** reunion pay-per-view, a one-night WWE/ECW crossover that grossed more than twice its attendance on pay-per-view.

The period opened with **WrestleMania 21 (April 3)** at the Staples Center, where John Cena won the WWE Championship from JBL and Batista won the World Heavyweight Championship from Triple H, ending Evolution. Cena's win began the first of his record-setting 17 WWE world title reigns.

**Key developments included:**
- **John Cena** won the WWE Championship at WrestleMania 21, then defended it three times in the quarter (I Quit match at Judgment Day, triple threat at Vengeance)
- **Batista** won the World Heavyweight Championship at WrestleMania 21 and defended it twice (Backlash, Hell in a Cell at Vengeance)
- **The 2005 WWE Draft Lottery** took place across June 6 and June 30, 2005; Cena was Raw's first pick, Kurt Angle Raw's second, and Batista SmackDown!'s final pick
- **The WWE Championship was transferred to the Raw brand** as a result of the draft — the single biggest structural change of the quarter
- **Kurt Angle was removed as SmackDown! General Manager** and replaced by Theodore Long
- **Carlito** won the WWE Intercontinental Championship and immediately began a heel run
- **Ric Flair** arrived on the SmackDown! brand
- **ECW One Night Stand (June 12)** was the first ECW reunion pay-per-view, headlined by The Dudley Boyz, and proved that ECW alumni still drew a substantial pay-per-view audience
- **"Stone Cold" Steve Austin** returned to full-time WWE involvement at WrestleMania 21

**PPV Statistics:**
- **WrestleMania 21:** 20,193 attendance (Los Angeles); 1,090,000 buys
- **Backlash 2005:** 14,000 attendance (Manchester, NH); 320,000 buys
- **Judgment Day 2005:** 12,000 attendance (Minneapolis); 220,000 buys
- **ECW One Night Stand:** 2,500 attendance (New York City); 325,000 buys
- **Vengeance 2005:** 9,850 attendance (Las Vegas area); 420,000 buys

## Major PPV Events

### WrestleMania 21 (April 3, 2005)

**Location:** Staples Center, Los Angeles, California  
**Attendance:** 20,193 (Staples Center record)  
**PPV Buys:** 1,090,000  
**Brand:** Both brands (Raw and SmackDown!)  
**Tagline:** "WrestleMania Goes Hollywood"  
**Gross:** More than $7.1 million in ticket sales

**Historical Note:** First WrestleMania held at the Staples Center and the fifth in the Los Angeles metropolitan area, after WrestleMania 2 (1986), WrestleMania VII (1991), WrestleMania XII (1996) and WrestleMania 2000. Tickets sold out in less than one minute, the fastest sell-out in WWE history at the time and the fastest sell-out at the Staples Center. Attendances came from 37 countries and 50 states.

**PPV Results:**
1. **Rey Mysterio defeated Eddie Guerrero** — Singles Match (12:39)
2. **Edge defeated Christian, Shelton Benjamin, Kane, and The Big Show** — Money in the Bank Ladder Match
3. **The Undertaker defeated Randy Orton** — Singles Match
4. **John "Bradshaw" Layfield (c) (with Orlando Jordan) was defeated by John Cena** — Singles Match for WWE Championship **John Cena WON WWE Championship**
5. **Triple H (c) (with Ric Flair) was defeated by Batista** — Singles Match for World Heavyweight Championship **Batista WON World Heavyweight Championship**
6. **Kurt Angle defeated Shawn Michaels** — Singles Match
7. **Akebono defeated Big Show** — Sumo Match

**Key Notes:**
- **John Cena** won the WWE Championship from JBL, ending JBL's 280-day reign; this was the first of Cena's record-setting 17 WWE world title reigns
- **Batista** won the World Heavyweight Championship from Triple H, ending Evolution
- The event featured the **first-ever Money in the Bank Ladder Match**, won by Edge
- The event was the final WrestleMania appearance of **Eddie Guerrero**, who died of heart failure in November 2005
- **"Stone Cold" Steve Austin** returned, beginning his part-time WWE run
- **Kurt Angle vs. Shawn Michaels** was widely praised

### ECW One Night Stand (June 12, 2005)

**Location:** Hammerstein Ballroom, New York City, New York  
**Attendance:** 2,500  
**PPV Buys:** 325,000  
**Brand:** ECW (WWE-produced ECW reunion event)  
**Historical Note:** Inaugural ECW One Night Stand — the first ECW reunion pay-per-view. The enormous popularity of ECW merchandise prompted WWE to organise it; its financial and critical success directly motivated WWE to hold a second edition the following year.

**PPV Results:**
1. **Lance Storm (with Dawn Marie) defeated Chris Jericho** — Singles Match (7:23)
2. **Super Crazy defeated Yoshihiro Tajiri (with The Sinister Minister and Mikey Whipwreck) and Little Guido** — Three-Way Dance (6:14)
3. **Rey Mysterio defeated Psicosis** — Singles Match (6:23)
4. **Sabu (with Rob Van Dam and Bill Alfonso) defeated Rhyno** — Singles Match (6:31)
5. **Chris Benoit defeated Eddie Guerrero** — Singles Match (10:36)
6. **Mike Awesome defeated Masato Tanaka** — Singles Match (9:52)
7. **The Dudley Boyz (Bubba Ray and D-Von) defeated Tommy Dreamer and The Sandman** — Tag Team Match (10:13)

**Key Notes:**
- The event was an **ECW reunion**, featuring ECW alumni rather than a current brand roster
- **325,000 pay-per-view buys from 2,500 in attendance** — the buyrate exceeded the live gate by a very large margin and exceeded the attendance by roughly 130x, making it one of the most efficient pay-per-vews WWE staged that decade
- **Chris Benoit vs. Eddie Guerrero** was widely regarded as the match of the night
- Backstage and former ECW wrestlers described the event as "fun"; many were reportedly happy with how it went
- This event is the direct precedent for **December to Dismember (2006)** and the 2006 ECW brand relaunch

## Injuries / Backstage Incidents / Controversies

### The 2005 WWE Draft Lottery
The two-part draft held June 6 and June 30, 2005 was the most significant roster reshuffle of the year. John Cena was Raw's first pick, which transferred the WWE Championship to the Raw brand. Batista was SmackDown!'s final pick on June 30, moving the World Heavyweight Championship to SmackDown! and ending Batista's feud with Triple H.

### Kurt Angle's Removal as General Manager
Kurt Angle was removed from the SmackDown! General Manager position during the quarter, and Theodore Long was installed as his replacement. Angle's playing role was moved toward a main-event orientation.

### ECW One Night Stand and the WWE/ECW "War"
Bischoff ran a "war" against ECW throughout the spring. ECW One Night Stand (June 12) resolved the tension commercially rather than competitively — the event was a critical and financial success and directly led to WWE reviving ECW as a brand in 2006. John Cena notably refused to participate in Bischoff's "war".

### Hulk Hogan's Return
Hulk Hogan's return at Backlash (May 1) was a nostalgia-driven booking that paid off: the Hogan/Shawn Michaels tag team match drew strong audience interest, and their SummerSlam dream match the following month was the highest-earning non-WrestleMania event of 2005.

### WrestleMania 21 Sell-Out
WrestleMania 21 sold out in under a minute, the fastest sell-out in WWE history at the time and the fastest sell-out at the Staples Center. Grossed more than $7.1 million — the highest-grossing WWE event ever at that venue.

## Conclusion

The April-June 2005 period was the hinge between the "Ruthless Aggression" spring and the summer's brand rebuild. It opened with WrestleMania 21 at the Staples Center, where John Cena won the WWE Championship from JBL and Batista won the World Heavyweight Championship from Triple H, ending Evolution and beginning Cena's record-setting 17-reign run. Tickets sold out in under a minute and the event grossed over $7.1 million.

Backlash (May 1) in Manchester, New Hampshire brought Hulk Hogan back to WWE alongside Shawn Michaels and saw Rosey and The Hurricane win the World Tag Team Championship. Judgment Day (May 22) in Minneapolis saw Cena retain in a brutal "I Quit" match against JBL and Booker T defeat Kurt Angle. On June 12, ECW One Night Stand at Manhattan's Hammerstein Ballroom produced one of the most commercially efficient pay-per-views WWE has staged — 325,000 buys from 2,500 fans in attendance — and directly paved the way for the 2006 relaunch of ECW as a WWE brand. Vengeance (June 26) closed the quarter with Batista retaining the World Heavyweight Championship in a Hell in a Cell match and Cena retaining in a triple threat.

The quarter's defining structural event was the **2005 WWE Draft Lottery**, held across June 6 and June 30. Cena became Raw's first pick, moving the WWE Championship to the Raw brand; Angle was Raw's second pick and was simultaneously removed as SmackDown! General Manager, with Theodore Long replacing him; and Batista was SmackDown!'s final pick, moving the World Heavyweight Championship to SmackDown! and ending his feud with Triple H.

By the end of June 2005, the championship landscape was: **John Cena** (WWE Champion, Raw), **Batista** (World Heavyweight Champion, SmackDown!), **Carlito** (Intercontinental Champion, SmackDown!), **Rosey & The Hurricane** (World Tag Team Champions, Raw), **MNM** (WWE Tag Team Champions, SmackDown!), **Trish Stratus** (Women's Champion, Raw), **Paul London** (Cruiserweight Champion, SmackDown!), and **Orlando Jordan** (United States Champion, SmackDown!).
